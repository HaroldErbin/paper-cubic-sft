\subsection*{Derivation}
*(1.1) The plumbed density.* With $X_i\in\{T,D\}$, $\mathcal P(q)=\big\langle B[\partial_s]B[\partial_\theta]\;\prod_{i=1}^4F_i\circ X_i(0)\big\rangle$ in path-integral (Grassmann) order, contours leftmost, $F_{1,2}=h_{1,2}\circ g$, $F_{3,4}=T\circ h_{1,2}\circ g$ (096 C2.1-halfB: one $(\lambda,\kappa)$). Split $B=\mathcal N+\mathcal D$ (neck, dressing):
$$
\mathcal P=\langle\mathcal N_s\mathcal N_\theta X\rangle+\langle\mathcal N_s(\mathcal D_\theta X)\rangle-\langle\mathcal N_\theta(\mathcal D_sX)\rangle+\langle(\mathcal D_s\mathcal D_\theta X)\rangle, \tag{1.1}
$$
$(\mathcal DX)$ the graded action on $X_1\otimes\dots\otimes X_4$ (Koszul), each $B_i$ applied before $U_{g}$. Each term is brought to radial order $(\phi_3\phi_4)\,\mathcal N\,(\phi_1\phi_2)$ with the sign $(-1)^{|\phi_{34}|(|\phi_{12}|+n_{\mathcal N})}$ and evaluated as
$$
\langle\Psi_{34}|\,\mathcal N\,|\Psi_{12}\rangle,\qquad |\Psi_{12}\rangle=h_1\circ(U_g\phi_1)(0)\,h_2\circ(U_g\phi_2)(0)|0\rangle_u,\qquad \langle\Psi_{34}|=\mathrm{bpz}\big(q^{L_0}\bar q^{\bar L_0}|\Psi'_{34}\rangle\big), \tag{1.2}
$$
$|\Psi'_{34}\rangle$ the same as $|\Psi_{12}\rangle$ for legs 3, 4 in half $B$'s frame $u'$ (the fixed half: same $h_{1,2}$), carried to $u=\infty$ by $T=I\circ d_q$; the sewn sum is $\sum_\chi\langle\Psi_{34}|\mathcal N|\chi\rangle\langle\chi^*|\Psi_{12}\rangle$ truncated at intermediate level $N$ (level $=L_0+1$ per chirality).

*(1.3) The legs.* Holomorphic parts: $T\to c_1|0\rangle$; $D\to c_1c_{-1}|0\rangle$ and $|0\rangle$ (the antiholomorphic term). $b_0$ annihilates both legs, so only the $b_1,\bar b_1$ parts of $B_i$ act: $b_1c_1c_{-1}|0\rangle=c_1|0\rangle$, $b_1T=0$. $U_g$: $L_1c_1c_{-1}|0\rangle=-c_1c_0|0\rangle$, $L_1c_0c_1|0\rangle=0$, $L_1c_1|0\rangle=0$. Every state is $c$-only.

*(1.4) Neck zero modes (C1.2).* Writing $F_{3,4}=\sigma_q\circ(h\circ g)$, $\sigma_q(u)=1/(qu)$, the $\sigma_q$ part of $\partial_sF_a/F_a'$ pushes forward to the global field $u\partial_u$ ($\partial_s\sigma_q=+\sigma_q$), the $\theta$ part to $-\mathrm iu\partial_u$; the rest is $\partial g/g'$, R93's dressing. A global field with no pole on the glued sphere: $\oint_{u_3,u_4}u\,b(u)\,\tfrac{\mathrm du}{2\pi\mathrm i}=-\oint_{|u|=\rho}u\,b(u)\tfrac{\mathrm du}{2\pi\mathrm i}=-b_0^{(u)}$ (the circle at $|u|\to\infty$ gives 0: $\langle0|b(u)=O(u^{-4})$); the conjugate field gives $-\bar b_0$; with $\theta$: $\mathrm ib_0-\mathrm i\bar b_0$. So $\mathcal N_s=-b_0^+$, $\mathcal N_\theta=\mathrm ib_0^-$ in $u$-modes, and $b_0^{(u)}=b_0^{(w_A)}$ ($w=1/u$: $\oint_{w=0}w\,b^{(w)}\mathrm dw=\oint_{u,\rm ccw}u\,b\,\mathrm du$): R93's neck operator, in the convention A6. A frame change $N_q$ adds a global $\mathfrak{sl}(2)$ field whose total contour vanishes, so the sister's frame $(0,1,\infty,Z)$ and the $u$-frame give the same $B[V]$.

*(1.5) Termination (C1.4).* $|\Psi_{12}\rangle$ is built from $c(x)|s\rangle=\sum_{m\le1}x^{1-m}c_m|s\rangle$ on $c$-only $|s\rangle$, so it is $c$-only; so is $\langle\Psi_{34}|$. In $\langle0|(\text{$c$-word})\,\mathcal N\,(\text{$c$-word})|0\rangle$ with $\mathcal N\subset\{b_0,\bar b_0\}$, a mode $c_{-n}$, $n\ge2$, of the ket can only be removed by $\langle0|c_{-n}=0$ and a mode $c_n$, $n\ge2$, of the bra by $c_n|0\rangle=0$: only the zero-mode sector $\{c_{-1},c_0,c_1\}^{\wedge}\otimes\{\bar c_{-1},\bar c_0,\bar c_1\}^{\wedge}$ ($L_0\le1$, level $\le2$) contributes. The sewn sum is exact at $N=2$; the convergence table of S3 tests this rather than presupposing it (the script truncates at $N$ and runs $N=0,\dots,5$).

*(1.6) The density engine's inputs.* `Omega(assign)` is a sympy expression in $(Z,\bar Z,R,\bar R,\partial_ZR,\partial_{\bar Z}R,\partial_Z\bar R,\partial_{\bar Z}\bar R,S,\bar S)$ (module-level function of the sister's script, imported by path, not run as main; the interface wrappers `Omega_D4`, … are defined under its `__main__` and are reproduced by lambdifying the same expression). At the test point: $R=F_1(\infty)$, $S=F_1'(0)$ of the package's $F_1$ at $Z$ (on the FD tile these are $R_{\rm interior}$, $S_0$); $\partial_ZR=\tfrac12(\partial_x-\mathrm i\partial_y)R$, $\partial_{\bar Z}R=\tfrac12(\partial_x+\mathrm i\partial_y)R$ by 4th-order central differences; $\partial_Z\bar R=\overline{\partial_{\bar Z}R}$, $\partial_{\bar Z}\bar R=\overline{\partial_ZR}$. That these are the package's maps: C1.1.

*(1.7) Normalisation (C1.3).* The engine's chiral correlator is $\langle c(x)c(y)c(w)\rangle=(x-y)(x-w)(y-w)$, i.e. $\langle0|c_{-1}c_0c_1|0\rangle=1$ per chirality, multiplied with the factorisation sign that brings each word to holomorphic-then-antiholomorphic order: $\langle0|c_{-1}c_0c_1\bar c_{-1}\bar c_0\bar c_1|0\rangle=1$, i.e. base $=-1$, the book's (A4). The engine's `Omega` is the bare $bc$ correlator at unit chiral normalisation, i.e. at the book's base $-1$; the sister's normalisation enters its delivered couplings through overall constants (041 §1), not through $\Omega$. R43's $\times\tfrac12$ therefore applies to the sister's $V_4$, $G_2$, $C_3$, $C_4$ and not to the density compared here; no conversion factor enters this comparison. The plumbed side carries $\mathcal C^2/\mathcal C=\mathcal C$ (two amplitudes, one inverse pairing; in (1.2) the pairing is the identity resolution of the same Fock space) and is computed at base $-1$. So no conversion factor enters; a residual factor 2 would show in $T^4$ and is not fitted.
