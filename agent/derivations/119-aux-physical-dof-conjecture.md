## Angle 1 – cohomology invariance and the inert fluctuation

\subsection*{Setup}
A2, A3. $z=(\Psi,\Sigma_2)$ the fields, $z^+$ their antifields, $s$ the BRST differential of R62's master action, $\mathcal Q_0$ its linearization around a background $(\Psi_0,\Sigma_*(\Psi_0))$; $\hat\sigma_2$ the flat fluctuation of R60 (`07b` eq. `eq:further:flat-variable`).

\subsection*{Derivation}
(1.1) A gauge transformation of the fields, a field redefinition preserving ghost numbers, or a BV canonical transformation, acts on the master action by an anticanonical change of variables and conjugates $s$; the cohomology $H(s)$, and $H(\mathcal Q_0)$ around any background, are invariant. This is the standard statement that gauge-fixed forms of one theory have the same physical content (Henneaux–Teitelboim, ch. 17).

(1.2) R73: $\mathcal Q_0\simeq Q^{(k)}_{\Psi_0}\oplus(\text{contractible pair})$, hence $H(\mathcal Q_0)\cong H(Q^{(k)}_{\Psi_0})$ in every ghost number. Combined with (1.1): every member of a family related to the cubic action by A3's operations has the spectrum of the original theory. "Physical states in $\Sigma_n$" can therefore mean only that some cohomology classes are represented, in that member, by vectors with a $\Sigma_n$ component. It cannot mean new states, and in particular not the multi-particle states of a Fock space (Angle 4). $\Rightarrow$ C1.

(1.3) Within the cubic action itself the gauge parameter is $\Lambda\in\mathcal H$ (R11). In the flat variables (R60), $\delta_\Lambda\hat\sigma_2=0$ and $\delta_\Lambda\Psi=Q_B\Lambda+g_s\ell_2(\Psi,\Lambda)+\dots$: the gauge orbit of any configuration lies in the $\Psi$ sector at fixed $\hat\sigma_2$, and a gauge-fixing condition can only be imposed on $\Psi$ (Siegel gauge, say), never trade a $\Psi$ component against a $\hat\sigma_2$ component. No transformation of the cubic theory moves a state out of $\Psi$. $\Rightarrow$ C1.1.

(1.4) The content-free version. In the master action the pair is $s\hat\sigma_2=0$, $s\hat\sigma_2^+=-\tfrac{g_s^2}{12}p_{2,2}(\hat\sigma_2)$; since $p_{2,2}$ is invertible on the quotient (PH1), $\hat\sigma_2=s\big(-\tfrac{12}{g_s^2}p_{2,2}^{-1}\hat\sigma_2^+\big)$ is exact, and a representative of any class can be given an arbitrary $\hat\sigma_2$ component by adding an exact term. This "moves" nothing physical. $\Rightarrow$ C1.2.

\subsection*{Commentary}
(1.3) is the decisive negative point for the idea in its literal form: a re-split of the cohomology between $\Psi$ and $\Sigma_2$ requires a gauge symmetry whose orbits have a component along $\Psi$ *and* a condition that can be imposed on $\Sigma_2$ in its place; the cubic action has neither. Any sound version of the idea therefore concerns an *enlarged* gauge symmetry, i.e. a parent theory (Angle 3), which is the question the paper already states as open (`07b` l. 55, `09` l. 30).

\subsection*{Result}
C1, C1.1, C1.2 hold under R73's hypotheses. Within the cubic action's own gauge group the idea is false; the only admissible meaning of "physical states in $\Sigma_n$" is a re-split of $H(Q^{(k)}_{\Psi_0})$ in a parent theory with a larger gauge group.

## Angle 2 – ghost-number counting under redefinitions

\subsection*{Setup}
A1, A3. $K:\Hn{n}\to\Hn{n}$ a candidate kinetic operator of $\Sigma_n$; $\hat Q_B=\sum_i 1\otimes\cdots\otimes Q_B\otimes\cdots\otimes 1$ (ghost number $1$ on every $\Hn{n}$).

\subsection*{Derivation}
(2.1) Saturation: $\pair[n]{\Sigma_n}{K\Sigma_n}\ne0$ requires $2n+2n+N_{\rm gh}(K)=5n$, i.e. $N_{\rm gh}(K)=n$. A strand-wise $\hat Q_B$ has ghost number $1$, so $n=1$ only; for $n=2$: $4+4+1=9\ne10$ (R18 (1)). $N_{\rm gh}(p_{n,n})=3n-2n=n$ (A1), which is the paper's statement at l. 72–74.

(2.2) Invariance. The operations of A3 preserve the ghost number of every field, hence the ghost-number content of every term of the action: in any member of the family, the quadratic form on $\Sigma_n$ is a pairing of two ghost-$2n$ vectors and (2.1) applies unchanged. In particular a ghost-number-preserving linear redefinition $\Psi'=\Psi+a(\Sigma_2)$, $N_{\rm gh}(a)=-2$, or $\Sigma_2'=\Sigma_2+b(\Psi)$, $N_{\rm gh}(b)=+2$, produces on $\Sigma_2$ the operator $p_{2,2}+a^\dagger Q_B a+\dots$ of ghost number $2$ again, and no cohomology change (it is a redefinition). The counting is a property of the field content $(\Hn{n},\,N_{\rm gh}=2n)$ and of the pairing, not of a gauge choice. $\Rightarrow$ C2, first clause.

(2.3) Where $\hat Q_B$ can enter. An off-diagonal term $\pair[n]{\Pi_n}{\hat Q_B\Sigma_n}$ saturates iff $N_{\rm gh}(\Pi_n)=5n-2n-1=3n-1$: $\Pi_2$ at ghost $5$, $\Pi_3$ at $8$. This is R18 (3)'s partner construction, which is a *change of field content*, outside A3, and whose closure is PH13 (open). The other way to have $\hat Q_B$ as a differential on every strand is CFL's: the fields form a Maurer–Cartan element of an operator $\hat{\mathfrak p}$ on the symmetric algebra, with degrees requiring $d=\tfrac12$ (record 114 C3; R111 states that CFL assumes an integer $d$ the raised family does not satisfy, by R31), not an action with $n$-string pairings – again another field content and grading. $\Rightarrow$ C2, second clause.

\subsection*{Commentary}
One reading of the paragraph l. 72–75 takes it as a statement about the gauge (the remark assumes the general form of IBL, while the action is gauge-fixed). (2.2) shows it is a statement about the field content: no gauge transformation or redefinition within the content $(\Psi,\Sigma_n)$ at ghost $(2,2n)$ can produce a strand-wise $Q_B$ kinetic term. The remark survives; what the gauge reading would require is the partner field of (2.3).

\subsection*{Result}
C2 holds (ghost-number arithmetic, A1). The ghost-number argument of l. 72–74 is gauge-independent; a strand-wise $\hat Q_B$ appears only with a ghost-$(3n-1)$ partner or in CFL's half-integer grading, both outside the family of A3.

## Angle 3 – which parameter could move states

\subsection*{Setup}
A1–A3, C1, C2. A parent theory $S_{\rm par}[\Psi,\Sigma_2,\dots]$ with a two-string parameter $\Lambda_2\in\Hn{2}$, $N_{\rm gh}(\Lambda_2)=3$, acting as $\delta\Psi=D(\Lambda_2)+\dots$, $\delta\Sigma_2=E(\Lambda_2)+\dots$, with $N_{\rm gh}(D)=-1$ ($\Hn{2}\to\mathcal H$) and $N_{\rm gh}(E)=+1$ ($\Hn{2}\to\Hn{2}$).

\subsection*{Derivation}
(3.1) Necessary feature. By (1.3), a re-split of the cohomology between $\Psi$ and $\Sigma_2$ needs an orbit with a $\Psi$ component that can be traded against a $\Sigma_2$ condition: $D\ne0$ on a subspace of $\Lambda_2$ on which the gauge-fixing condition of the cubic action acts through $E$. With $D=0$ the orbits stay in the $\Sigma$ sector, the $\Psi$ content is the same in every gauge, and the parent is at most an enlargement that adds a trivial pair and removes it again.

(3.2) The examined classes. R106: within $D=0$ ("$\Lambda_2$ not acting on $\Psi$") and a field-independent $E$ (R106's own name for $E$ is "$D$"), every two-string parameter is a shift along null directions of the enlarged quadratic form; it moves nothing, by (3.1). R108 (Proven) admits a field-independent $D\ne0$ and shows, at $O(g_s^2)$ and generic momentum on the present field content ($\Sigma_2\in(\mathcal H_2)^{\wedge2}/\ker p_{2,2}$), that $D(\Lambda_2)\in\operatorname{im}Q_B$: the $\Lambda_2$-linear part of any symmetry is a gauge transformation with a parameter built from $\Lambda_2$, plus a kernel shift, plus a trivial symmetry – the orbit of $\Lambda_2$ in $\Psi$ is a $\Lambda$-orbit, so no class is re-split there. R109 (Conditional(PH1, PH27, PH43) – Argued) extends this to the single-string sector. Hence, within the present field content at $O(g_s^2)$ and generic momentum, a $\Lambda_2$ acting on $\Psi$ moves nothing out of the $\Lambda$-orbits: this is a third negative item, stronger than C1.1. The open class is therefore the enlarged field content (the ghost-$5$ partner, the enlarged $\Sigma_2$ sectors of R108's criterion 3a), $O(g_s^3)$, or on-shell momenta (R108 Scope) – R108's own "weakest relaxation".

(3.3) The candidate law. The ghost-number-$(-1)$ operations $\Hn{2}\to\mathcal H$ built from the theory's data are $\ell_2=p_{2,1}$ ($N_{\rm gh}=-1$, A1) and its dressings $\ell_3(\Psi,\cdot)$, $\dots$: $D(\Lambda_2)=g_s\ell_2(\Lambda_2)+g_s^2\ell_3(\Psi,\Lambda_2)+\dots$, which is exactly the $E_1$ component of the IBL gauge law (R110, 111 C2.2), with $E(\Lambda_2)=\hat Q_B\Lambda_2+\dots$ (111 C2.1). Alone, $\delta\Psi=g_s\ell_2(\Lambda_2)$ is not a symmetry of the cubic action (R18 (5), 103 C8.2); with the $\Sigma_2$ law it preserves the Maurer–Cartan components $\mathcal M_1,\mathcal M_2^{(5)}$ at $\hbar^0$ through $O(g_s)$ (111 C3.2), which are not the equations of motion of the cubic action. In the parent, by (2.3), the term $\hat Q_B\Lambda_2$ in $\delta\Sigma_2$ must be matched by a kinetic term it leaves invariant, i.e. by a ghost-$5$ partner $\Pi_2$ (R18 (3)) or by a dressed form of $p_{2,2}$ with $\hat Q_B\Lambda_2$ among its null directions at the given background – the two routes the decision of 2026-10-22 leaves to the later project ("$\Lambda_2$ acting on $\Psi$", "field-dependent operator").

(3.4) What moves, if the parent exists. The gauge-fixing condition of the cubic action is then the one that uses $\Lambda_2$ up on the $\Sigma$ side (R18 (3): $\Pi_2=0$, admissible iff $\delta\Pi_2\propto m_2(\Lambda_2)+\dots$ is surjective, $m_2=p_{2,2}$ up to normalization; PH13), so that every class is represented in $\Psi$ and $\Sigma_2=\Psi^2+\hat\sigma_2$ with $\hat\sigma_2$ inert. A gauge condition on $\Psi$ instead kills the directions $\ell_2(\Lambda_2)$ with $\Lambda_2$ at ghost $3$, i.e. $\ell_2$ of $(2,1)$ and $(3,0)$ two-string states. Whether these meet a non-trivial class of $H^2(Q_B)$ is not generic: at generic momentum on both strands $H^0=H^1=0$, so a $\hat Q_B$-closed $\Lambda_2$ is exact, $\Lambda_2=\hat Q_BM_2$, and $\ell_2(\Lambda_2)=\mp Q_B\ell_2(M_2)$ is exact ($\ell_2$ is a chain map); a non-closed $\Lambda_2$ gives a closed $\ell_2(\Lambda_2)$ only if $\hat Q_B\Lambda_2\in\ker\ell_2$. The classes a $\Psi$-gauge would move into $\Sigma_2$ therefore sit at zero momentum on a strand (ghost-$1$ global-symmetry states) or come from $\hat Q_B\Lambda_2\in\ker\ell_2$ – the generic-momentum obstruction of R108 seen from the state side. The re-split is thus part of the conjecture, not a consequence of it. The total cohomology is $H(Q^{(k)}_{\Psi_0})$ in both gauges (C1) *if* the parent is proper and the cubic action its gauge fixing – which is the conjecture itself; the $(\Sigma_2,\Pi_2)$ sector with both $\pair[2]{\Pi_2}{\hat Q_B\Sigma_2}$ and the $p_{2,2}$ term is not obviously contractible (R18's boundary anomaly, PH13).

\subsection*{Commentary}
(3.3) is the content of the conjecture beyond what the paper already says: the open question "is the cubic action the gauge-fixed form of a theory with $\Lambda_2$" (`07b` l. 55) is sharpened to the requirement $D\ne0$, with $D=g_s\ell_2+\dots$ as the candidate fixed by the IBL law; R106's class ($D=0$) could not realize that picture, and R108 excludes $D\ne0$ on the present field content at $O(g_s^2)$, generic momentum, so the conjecture lives in the enlarged content. (3.4) gives the precise meaning of "degrees of freedom moved from $\Psi$ to $\Sigma_2$": the classes, if any, that the $\Lambda_2$-orbits in $\Psi$ reach – non-generic, hence a clause of the conjecture. The paper's l. 55 describes R106's class only; whether it may cite R108's $\Lambda_2$-on-$\Psi$ content is the orchestrator's decision (scoping of 2026-10-22).

\subsection*{Result}
C3 holds (Conditional through R109, R110). The idea is sound only for a parent with $\Lambda_2$ acting on $\Psi$, at leading order by $g_s\ell_2(\Lambda_2)$, on an enlarged field content (partner $\Pi_2$, enlarged $\Sigma_2$ sectors), since R108 excludes it on the present one at $O(g_s^2)$, generic momentum; no such parent is constructed or excluded, and its construction is the question left open by workflow B.

## Angle 4 – the IBL canonical model

\subsection*{Setup}
A3, C1, C2; R111 (CFL Thm 6.1 via the cached reviews).

\subsection*{Derivation}
(4.1) In an IBL$_\infty$ algebra with $\mathfrak p_{1,1,0}=Q_B$, the canonical model lives on $E\,H(Q_B)=\mathrm{Sym}\,H(Q_B)$: the $k$-string sector carries $\mathrm{Sym}^kH(Q_B)$, the $k$-particle states, and these directions are not auxiliary (R111; record 114 C4.1–C4.2). Around a background the first-quantized spectrum of the cubic theory is $H(Q^{(k)}_{\Psi_0})$ (R73), a single copy of the one-particle states.

(4.2) $\mathrm{Sym}\,H(Q_B)\supsetneq H(Q_B)$ as graded spaces. A proper parent and its gauge-fixed form have isomorphic BRST cohomology (A3, Henneaux–Teitelboim ch. 17), and so do the members related by redefinitions (1.1); the cubic action's is $H(Q^{(k)}_{\Psi_0})$ (1.2). A theory whose multi-string sectors carry $\mathrm{Sym}^kH(Q_B)$ has a larger cohomology, so the IBL formulation is not a gauge-unfixed parent of the cubic action, nor any other member of the family. Its multi-string states are second-quantized (Fock) states – a different object from the re-split of (3.4) – and its relation to the cubic action is the $L_\infty$ strictification/transfer of `07-ibl.tex` `sec:ibl:transfer` (R41, R111), with the grading mismatch $d=\tfrac12$.

(4.3) Consequently the sentence l. 74–75 ("in an IBL algebra the differential acts on every strand … we would expect $1\otimes\cdots\otimes Q_B\otimes\cdots\otimes1$") compares two field contents and gradings, not two gauges of one theory, and the objection (the remark assumes the IBL form; ours is gauge-fixed) does not apply to it. $\Rightarrow$ C4.

\subsection*{Result}
C4 holds (Conditional through R111). The IBL formulation realizes "physical states in the multi-string fields" in the Fock sense, which is not a gauge-equivalent reformulation of the cubic action; the sound conjecture (Angle 3) is of the re-split kind and does not have the IBL form.
