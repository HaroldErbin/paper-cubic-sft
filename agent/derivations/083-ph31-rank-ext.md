**Reading.** (a) The $b$-identity alone gives 3 ($K=2$) and 34 ($K=3$) relations; these are $P$-independent ($O_b$ contains no $\alpha_0$), counted over $\mathbb Q$ as well (34 at $P_q$, `E-exact-b-K3-d2`; being $P$-independent, the count over $\mathbb Q$ holds at every $P$): the 078 lower end "$\ge34$" is exactly the $b$-count. (b) The $L$-functionals alone give no relation at $K=2$ and 2 at $K=3$: $O_L$ contains $b_{\pm1}c_0$-type ghost terms and $\alpha_{\pm1}\alpha_0$ matter terms, so an $L$-functional always has components in the $c_0$, $\bar c_0$ sectors, which $W$ does not detect. (c) The $b$-alone and $L$-alone relations are independent of each other (stacked rank $3+0=3$ at $K=2$, $d=3$; $34+2=36$ at $K=3$; `E3-split-d3`, `E1-split-K3`), so the remaining relations (2 at $K=2$, 24 at $K=3$) come only from *mixed* combinations, in which the zero-mode-sector components of $L$-functionals are cancelled by $b$-functionals of those sectors. So "the cokernel is generated by the two identities" means: by their joint span, not by the sum of the separate counts. (d) At $K=2$ the count does not depend on $d_{\rm eff}$ (2 → 3), measured both for the identities ($b$ 3, $L$ 0, both 5) and for the rank (deficit 5). *Structure of the $b$-part* (critic 083 #5). $O_b$ acts on ghosts only, so the $b$-relation space factorises over matter: $R_b(K,d)=\bigoplus_m m\otimes R^{\rm gh}_b(K-a,K-\bar a)$, $m$ running over the matter Fock states at levels $(a,\bar a)$ with $a-\bar a$ even. The identity is blind to matter; its *count* scales with the matter multiplicities. At $K=2$: matter $(1,1)$ leaves the ghost budget $(1,1)$, where there is no relation ($K=1$ is full rank, 078 C3); matter $(2,0)$ or $(0,2)$ leaves the budget $(2,0)$/$(0,2)$, and a ghost relation there would be a combination of 078's null vectors 1–3 with no $\bar N>0$ component, which is impossible (null vector 3 alone has a (2,2) entry, then null vector 1 alone a (0,2) entry, then null vector 2 a (1,1) entry). So the $b$-count at $K=2$ is 3 for every $d$: structural. At $K=3$ the $d^2$ matter states $\alpha^\mu_{-1}\bar\alpha^\nu_{-1}$ times the 3 $K=2$ ghost relations contribute $3d^2$, and every other term is non-decreasing in $d$, so
$$b\text{-count}(K=3,d)\ \ge\ 34+3(d^2-4):\qquad 49\ (d=3),\quad \ge 2050\ (d=26).$$
Hence the $K=3$ cokernel ($\ge$ the $b$-count) is necessarily $d$-dependent, and 60/487 is a $d_{\rm eff}=2$ number. At $K=2$ the $d$-independence of the 2 mixed relations ($O_L$ acts on matter) is measured only ($d=2,3$); a likely reason, not tested: they come from matter-vacuum $\chi$, on which the matter part of $O_L$ produces only the longitudinal $P\cdot\alpha_{-1}$. The brief's expectation that the $L$-contribution changes with $d$ is not borne out at $K=2$; at $K=3$, $d=3$ was not run, but the bound above predicts a cokernel $\ge49$ there.

**What is exact, and where.** Three statements, of decreasing strength.
1. *Rigorous, all $P$:* the $b$-relations (34 at $K=3$, counted over $\mathbb Q$ by `E-exact-b-K3-d2`) hold for every $P$ and every input level, so $\operatorname{coker}\ge34$ at $K=3$ for every $L$.
2. *Rigorous at the evaluation point:* at the rational momenta $P_q$ of `E-exact-K*` the relations are computed over $\mathbb Q$ and hold for every $\Delta$ and every input level, and the mod-$p$ scan at $P_q$ bounds $\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q(\Delta)}M(P_q,\Delta)$ from below; relations + rank = dim, so at $P_q$ the rank is exactly 68/73 ($K=2$, $d=2$), 159/164 ($K=2$, $d=3$), 427/487 ($K=3$, $d=2$) for every $L\ge2$. The same holds over $\mathbb F_p$ at the mod-$p$ points of the main scans.
3. *Generic $P$, probabilistic:* the rank is $\ge$ these values for generic $P$ (a non-zero minor at the sample point is a non-zero polynomial in $P$ over $\mathbb Q$). For the upper bound, write $F$ for the matrix of identity functionals and $F_{\rm out}$ for its block on the outside coordinates; the count is $\operatorname{rk}F-\operatorname{rk}F_{\rm out}$. At a special point a drop of $\operatorname{rk}F$ only lowers the count, so the count at the sample point exceeds the generic count only if $\operatorname{rk}F_{\rm out}$ drops there; otherwise generic count $\ge60$, hence generic rank $\le427$, hence $=427$. *Degree bound:* the $b$-functionals are $P$-independent and the $L$-functionals are affine in $P$ (each term of $L_{\pm1}$ carries at most one $\alpha_0$), so every minor of $F_{\rm out}$ has degree $\le\#\{L\text{-functionals}\}\le12670$ in $P$ ($\le1972$ at $K=2$, $d=2$; $\le5004$ at $K=2$, $d=3$). By the Schwartz–Zippel lemma (Schwartz 1980; Zippel 1979) a uniformly random point of $\mathbb F_p^d$ lies on the zero set of a non-zero polynomial of degree $\le D$ with probability $\le D/p$: the failure probability of the random mod-$p$ point of the main scans is $\le12670/(2^{31}-1)\approx6\times10^{-6}$ at $K=3$ ($\approx9\times10^{-7}$ and $\approx2.3\times10^{-6}$ at $K=2$, $d=2,3$). Caveat: this holds for $p$ not a bad prime, i.e. if the generic rank of $F_{\rm out}$ over $\mathbb F_p(P)$ equals its generic rank over $\mathbb Q(P)$ (not checked). The rational points $P_q$ are drawn from a set of about 700 values per coordinate, smaller than the degree bound, and carry no Schwartz–Zippel weight; agreement of the count there is a consistency check only. This precision applies equally to 078's "$K=2$: exact for every $L$": 078 §3's sentence "their linear independence at the random $P\bmod p$ implies independence over $\mathbb Q(P)$" presumes the relations at the sample point are specialisations of relations over $\mathbb Q(P)$, which is the same genericity statement. It does not touch R84's refutation, which rests on (4.3) and a single witness.

**Closure at $K=3$.** 078's bracket $[34,60]$ is closed: cokernel $=60$, rank $=427$ at every $L\ge2$ (in the sense of items 2–3). The $L=3$ scan adds nothing (12000 level-3 pairs in the script; evidence run `k3l3-big.py`, seed 20261085: full $L=2$ echelon 427/487 in 55.7 s, then 40000 random ordered pairs with a level-3 input at the same $\Delta$, rank 427/487 (+0) after 220.2 s, 9.2 % of the 434688 such pairs), as the relations require; the scan is a consistency check of the engine on level-3 inputs.
