## §1 Method

**Reduction to amplitudes.** $\Phi_2(A_1\otimes A_2)=f_1\circ A_1(0)\,f_2\circ A_2(0)|0\rangle$ in the $u$-plane, $u=1/w_0$ (071 A3). For any $B$, $\langle\mathrm{bpz}(B)|\Phi_2\rangle=\langle I\circ B(0)\,f_1\circ A_1(0)\,f_2\circ A_2(0)\rangle$ with $I(w)=1/w$. The BPZ pairing pairs $W_{(N,\bar N)}(P)$ non-degenerately with $\mathcal H^\downarrow_{(N,\bar N),\,\rm gh\,2}(-P)$ and annihilates the other three summands against $\mathcal H^\downarrow$ (074 (2.1)). Hence $\operatorname{rank}\pi_{\le K}\pi_W\Phi_2=\operatorname{rank}M$, $M_{B,\rm col}=\langle\mathcal V_3'|B\otimes\mathrm{col}\rangle$, $B$ over the Fock basis of $\mathcal H^\downarrow_{\rm gh\,2}$ at levels $N,\bar N\le K$; the even block is the rows with $N-\bar N$ even, $\dim W^{\rm even}_{\le K}$ = number of those rows.

**Maps.** $f_1'^{-1}(z)=2\beta\frac{1-z}{1+z}$, so $u=\frac{1+z}{2\beta(1-z)}$. Puncture $z=0$: $f_1(w)=u(f_0'(w))=\frac{(\alpha'_{\rm v}+1)w+\gamma'}{2\beta((\alpha'_{\rm v}-1)w+\gamma')}$; puncture $z=\infty$: $u(1/f_0')=-u(f_0')$, so $f_2=-f_1$ exactly (the hypothesis of 071 C3, confirmed). Special leg $h_0(w)=1/w$.

**Chiral factorisation.** For fixed momenta the correlator is (plane-wave prefactor $|\rho|^{\alpha'(k_1^2+k_2^2)/2}|2z|^{\alpha'k_1\cdot k_2}$, common to both terms of a symmetrised column because $|\rho_1|=|\rho_2|$, dropped) $\times$ holomorphic matter $\times$ antiholomorphic matter $\times$ ghost factor. The maps have real coefficients, so the antiholomorphic Neumann coefficients equal the holomorphic ones; momenta enter both chiralities as $\ell=\sqrt{\alpha'/2}\,k$, not conjugated.

**Matter (per component).** Local mode $\alpha^{(r)}_{-m}=\oint w^{-m}h_r'(w)\,j(h_r(w))$, $j=i\sqrt{2/\alpha'}\partial X$, $\langle jj\rangle=(u-u')^{-2}$, classical field $J(u)=\sum_{s=1,2}\ell_s/(u-z_s)$. Wick: sum over partial matchings of the oscillators, pairs weighted by
$N^{rs}_{mn}=[w^{m-1}w'^{n-1}]\Big(\frac{h_r'(w)h_s'(w')}{(h_r(w)-h_s(w'))^2}-\frac{\delta_{rs}}{(w-w')^2}\Big)$, singletons by $L^r_m=[w^{m-1}]\big(h_r'(w)J(h_r(w))-\ell_r/w\big)$ ($\ell_0=-\ell_1-\ell_2$). The subtracted singular parts integrate to zero for creation modes $m,n\ge1$ on nested contours.

**Ghosts (per chirality).** Leg states written as modes on $|0\rangle$ ($b_n$, $n\le-2$; $c_n$, $n\le1$; a $b_{-1}$ on $c_1|0\rangle$ is first removed by $b_{-1}c_1|0\rangle=|0\rangle$). Sphere correlator, proven by residues and the $O(v^{-4})$ decay:

**Mod-$p$ ranks and their closure over $\mathbb Q$.** $M$ is a matrix over $\mathbb Q[P,\Delta]$ (rational Neumann and ghost data, polynomial in the momenta; $p=2^{31}-1$ divides none of the denominators, which are products of powers of 2 and 3 at $\beta=3/2$, $\gamma'=3$). ## §4 Reading (C4): the deficit is a selection rule of the $\alpha'_{\rm v}=0$ vertex

\subsection*{Setup}
A1, A2 with $\alpha'_{\rm v}=0$, A4. $z:=\frac1{2\beta}$, so $z_{1,2}=\pm z$. $b_n^-:=b_n-\bar b_n$, $L_n^-:=L_n-\bar L_n$ on the $u$-plane (output) Fock space.

\subsection*{Derivation}
$$
\begin{aligned}
&f_1(w)=\frac{w+\gamma'}{2\beta(\gamma'-w)},\quad f_1(\infty)=-z=z_2,\quad f_2(\infty)=z_1, \qquad w=f_1^{-1}(u)=\gamma'\frac{2\beta u-1}{2\beta u+1},\quad f_1'(w)=\frac{(1+2\beta u)^2}{4\beta\gamma'}, \\
&v:=(f_1)_*\big(w\partial_w\big)=w f_1'(w)\,\partial_u=\frac{(2\beta u-1)(2\beta u+1)}{4\beta}\partial_u=\beta(u^2-z^2)\,\partial_u,\qquad (f_2)_*\big(w\partial_w\big)=-v .
\end{aligned}\tag{4.1}
$$
$v$ is a global conformal Killing vector of the sphere ($\mathfrak{sl}_2$; $\beta u^2\partial_u=-\beta\partial_{w_0}$ is regular at $u=\infty$), vanishing at $u=\pm z$ and not at the special puncture $u=\infty$, which it moves — this is why the leg-0 form of the operator contains the creation mode $b_{-1}$; it equals $+w\partial_w$, resp. $-w\partial_w$, *exactly* in the local coordinates of legs 1 and 2. The 1-form $v(u)b(u)du$ is holomorphic on the $u$-plane minus the two ordinary discs; deforming $\oint_{|u|=1}$ onto the discs and using $\oint w\,b(w)\,\frac{dw}{2\pi i}=b_0$ in each local frame,
$$
\beta\big(b_1-z^2b_{-1}\big)\Phi_2(A_1,A_2)=\Phi_2(b_0A_1,A_2)-(-1)^{|A_1|}\Phi_2(A_1,b_0A_2),
\tag{4.2}
$$
and the same with bars ($v$ has real coefficients). Subtracting, and repeating with $T$ in place of $b$:
$$
\boxed{\;\big(b_1^--z^2b_{-1}^-\big)\Phi_2(A_1,A_2)=0\ \ \text{for }A_i\in\ker b_0^-,\qquad \big(L_1^--z^2L_{-1}^-\big)\Phi_2(A_1,A_2)=0\ \ \text{for }A_i\in\ker L_0^-\;}
\tag{4.3}
$$
$O_b:=b_1^--z^2b_{-1}^-$ anticommutes with $c_0,\bar c_0,b_0,\bar b_0$, so it preserves each of $\mathcal H^\downarrow$, $c_0^\pm\mathcal H^\downarrow$, $W$, and $\pi_WO_b=O_b\pi_W$: $\pi_W\Phi_2(\mathrm{Sym}^2\mathcal H_0)\subset\ker(O_b|_W)$. Within the truncation, every dual vector $\chi$ at level $(N,\bar N)$ with $N+1\le K$, $\bar N+1\le K$ gives a left-null vector $\chi\circ O_b$ of $M$; at $K=2$ there are four admissible $\chi$ (ghost number 3, $N+\bar N$ odd so that the components hit are even), of rank 3: $\chi\in\{(1,0):c_{-1},\ (0,1):\bar c_{-1}\}$, with supports $\{(0,0),(2,0),(1,1)[c_{-1}\bar b_{-1}]\}$ and $\{(0,0),(0,2),(1,1)[b_{-1}\bar c_{-1}]\}$ — exactly null vectors 2 and 1 of the scan (working notes below) — and $(1,2):c_{-1}\bar c_{-1}\bar b_{-1}$ with its mirror $(2,1):c_{-1}b_{-1}\bar c_{-1}$, whose level-3 component vanishes by $\bar b_{-1}^2=0$ (resp. $b_{-1}^2=0$); null vector 3 is theirs.

\subsection*{Commentary}
(4.1): direct algebra; the key fact is $f_1(\infty)=z_2$: the pole of the Möbius coordinate at puncture 1 sits at puncture 2, which happens iff in the original $z$-sphere the coordinates at $0,\infty$ are pure dilations, $f_0'(w)=w/\gamma'$ ($\alpha'_{\rm v}=0$, 032 §1), so that the dilation $z\partial_z$ of the $z$-sphere is $w\partial_w$ in both ordinary frames. For $\alpha'_{\rm v}\ne0$ the same CKV reads $w\partial_w+\frac{\alpha'_{\rm v}}{\gamma'}w^2\partial_w$ in the local frame, the right side of (4.2) contains $b_1^-A_i\ne0$ on $\mathcal H_0$, and no selection rule follows. (4.2): standard conservation law (contour deformation of a holomorphic 1-form, Erbin 2023 Ward identities), with the Koszul sign for passing $b$ through $A_1$; it is an instance of the conservation laws of a vertex (a vector field with prescribed behaviour at the punctures gives linear relations among vertex coefficients; Rastelli–Zwiebach, *Tachyon potentials, star products and universality*, JHEP 09 (2001) 038, arXiv:hep-th/0006240, §3). For $\alpha'_{\rm v}\ne0$ such conservation laws still exist, with $b_1^-$ acting on the inputs; they only stop being selection rules on $\mathcal H_0$. (4.3): $b_0^-A_i=0$, $L_0^-A_i=0$ on $\mathcal H_0$; the $L$-identity does not commute with $\pi_W$ in general (the ghost part of $L_n$ contains $b_nc_0$, $b_0c_n$), so its contribution to the $W$-cokernel is measured, not derived.

\subsection*{Result}
For R2's $\mathcal V_3'$ at $\alpha'_{\rm v}=0$ (any $\gamma'$, $\beta$), the image $\pi_W\Phi_2(\mathrm{Sym}^2\mathcal H_0)$ lies in $\ker(b_1^--z^2b_{-1}^-)$ — an exact identity for inputs of every level, hence a deficit that persists as $L$ grows and grows with $K$; with level matching a second identity adds further relations. Measured cokernel at $K=2$: 3 (ker $b_0^-$ inputs, not level-matched), 5 ($\mathcal H_0$ inputs), both generated by (4.3); 0 when the inputs are all of $\mathcal H$ at ghost number 2. At $K=3$, $L=2$: the cokernel lies in $[34,60]$ (60 is the mod-$p$ deficit, an upper bound on the cokernel over $\mathbb Q$ at $L=2$; 34 is the rank of the $b$-functionals; $L=3$ not run); the (3,1), (1,3), (3,3) blocks are not individually onto at the mod-$p$ rank. At $K\le2$ every single level block is reached; the unreached directions tie the lowest ghost levels together ((0,0) with the $b_{-1}c_{-1}$-type pairs at (1,1), (2,0), (0,2), (2,2)) — the "ghost sector" failure mode of PH31's motivation, but produced by the input constraint $b_0^-$, not by $c(x)c(-x)$ starting at $c\partial c$.
