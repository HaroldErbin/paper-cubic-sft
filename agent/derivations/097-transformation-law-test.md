## S1 — What is compared, independence, ghost number, normalisation

**Stage A, what is compared.** For four inputs $A_i$ (Fock monomials) and $q\in Q_V$:
$$
\mathcal D(q):=\big\langle F_3\circ A_3(0)\,F_4\circ A_4(0)\,F_1\circ A_1(0)\,F_2\circ A_2(0)\big\rangle_{S^2},\qquad F_i:=F_i^{(u)}(\cdot\,;q), \tag{1.1}
$$
against
$$
\mathcal P_N(q):=\sum_{\chi,\chi'\,:\,N(\chi),\bar N(\chi)\le N}\psi_B^{\chi}\,q^{h_\chi}\bar q^{\bar h_\chi}\,G_{\chi\chi'}\,\psi_A^{\chi'},\qquad \psi_A=G^{-1}a_A,\ \ (a_A)_\chi=\big\langle I\circ\chi(0)\ h_1\circ U_{g_1}A_1(0)\ h_2\circ U_{g_2}A_2(0)\big\rangle, \tag{1.2}
$$
and $\psi_B$ the same with $(A_3,A_4)$ in half $B$'s own frame $u'$ (punctures $\pm z$, the same maps $h_{1,2}$). Derivation of (1.2): the correlator (1.1) in the $u$-plane, with $|u_{3,4}|=2\beta/|q|>z=|u_{1,2}|$ for $|q|<4\beta^2$, is the pairing of the ket $\Psi_A$ at $u=0$ created by legs 1, 2 with the state created by legs 3, 4 outside; in half $B$'s frame the latter is the ket $\Psi_B$ at $u'=0$, carried to $u=\infty$ by $T(u')=1/(qu')=I(qu')$, so $T\circ\Psi_B=I\circ(q^{L_0}\bar q^{\bar L_0}\Psi_B)$ (dilation law, standard) and $\mathcal D=G(q^{L_0}\bar q^{\bar L_0}\Psi_B,\Psi_A)$; components from $a_A=G\psi_A$. The recipe enters through $F_i=h_i\circ g_i$, i.e. $F_i\circ A_i=h_i\circ U_{g_i}A_i$ (A3 of 096).

**Why the two sides are independent.** $\mathcal D$ uses the package's four germs, $F_3,F_4$ read from the package (not from Klein). $\mathcal P_N$ uses no package data except $(\lambda,\kappa)(q)$ read from $F_1$ alone, the rigid half maps, the BPZ pairing and the operator $U_g$ built as a matrix on the Fock space (Virasoro generators $L_0,L_1$ from the mode algebra, not from geometry). A wrong $U_g$ (sign of $L_1$, missing $L_1$, holomorphic continuation of $\bar\kappa,\bar\mu$) or a wrong claim "one $(\lambda,\kappa)$ on all four legs" makes $\mathcal D\ne\lim_N\mathcal P_N$. To locate a failure the comparison is split: C1.2 (sewing, no $U_g$), C1.3 (A3 alone, no sewing, exact), C1.4 (both). The level of the independence: the engine's treatment of a germ on a non-primary (Neumann data of the map) is itself one realisation of the transformation law, so C1.3 is the Virasoro realisation of $g$ against the Neumann-data realisation of $h\circ g$; it tests R93's operator form (sign of $L_1$, conjugate parameters, $\kappa^{L_0}\bar\kappa^{\bar L_0}$ with its branch at $\kappa=-1$, one $(\lambda,\kappa)$ on all legs, $\lambda_0=\tfrac12$) and not the Neumann-data engine, which is anchored by C1.1 (an independent implementation of the same ghost-determinant scheme as 078) and by C1.2 (structural: a wrong four-point $N^{rs}$ or $E^{rs}$ breaks the sewing identity).

**Ghost number.** Without antighosts a sphere correlator needs ghost number $3$ in each chirality. Choice: compare full correlators, not Neumann data, on inputs whose holomorphic (antiholomorphic) ghost numbers add to 3 (3) – e.g. three legs $c_1\bar c_1$-based and one leg of ghost number $(0,0)$, or ghost oscillators redistributing $(2,1,0,0)$ and the like. Reason: the full correlator tests the Neumann data, the linear (momentum) terms, the ghost determinant with its zero modes, and the action of $U_g$ on ghost oscillators (where $L_1$ contains $b,c$ terms) in one number; comparing Neumann data would test the geometry and not the operator $U_g$ acting on states. Non-saturating inputs give $0=0$ and are not used. The sewn side then needs the intermediate states at ghost numbers $3-\mathrm{gh}_{1}-\mathrm{gh}_2$ per chirality, including the $c_0,\bar c_0$ sectors; the basis includes all of them.

**Normalisation.** Every correlator here – the four-point $\mathcal D$, the two three-point vectors $a_A,a_B$, the pairing $G$ – is computed by one engine with one normalisation constant $\mathcal C$ of the sphere correlator. $\mathcal P_N$ carries $\mathcal C^2/\mathcal C=\mathcal C$, the same as $\mathcal D$: the comparison is normalisation-free, and the sister/book factor (record 041, $\times\tfrac12$ per sphere correlator) does not enter stage A. No constant is fitted anywhere in stage A. (Stage B normalisation: handed to record 098.)

**Momentum prefactor.** Included on both sides: $\prod_{r<s}|u_r-u_s|^{2\ell_r\cdot\ell_s}\prod_r|F_r'(0)|^{\ell_r^2}$ for $\mathcal D$, the same for the three- and two-point data, and $q^{L_0}\bar q^{\bar L_0}$ with $L_0=\tfrac12\ell^2+N-1$. With complex momenta the moduli are taken as $\exp(c\ln|x|)$ (no branch).

**Free constants.** None: $\mathcal C$ cancels (above); the momentum prefactors and $q^{L_0}\bar q^{\bar L_0}$ are computed, not fixed; $(\lambda,\kappa)$ are read from $F_1$; $\lambda_0=\tfrac12$, $\gamma'=\beta$ are R93's canonical choice.

**Regime of the sum.** Components of $\Psi_{A,B}$ at level $N$ scale as $z^N$, and heuristically $\mathcal D-\mathcal P_N=O(|q|^Nz^{2N})=O(|y|^N)$ up to the polynomial growth of the components, which is not quantified: this suggests convergence for $|y|<1$, i.e. on $Q_V$ off the fold arcs ($|q|=4\beta^2$ there), but it is tested only at $|y|\le0.36$; on the fold arcs the behaviour is governed by exponents, not by $|y|$ (R83). Points: inner seam $q=1.05\,\mathrm e^{\mathrm i\theta}$ ($|y|\approx0.12$) and an interior point with $|y|\approx0.3$–$0.4$; both asserted in the vertex region.
