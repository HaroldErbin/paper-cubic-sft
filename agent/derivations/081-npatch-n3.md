$$
w=\frac{\lambda}{u-c},\qquad(\lambda,c)\in\mathbb C^*\times\mathbb C,
\tag{1.1}
$$
and the Möbius freedom of $u$ fixing $\infty$ (affine maps) is used to put two ordinary punctures at $u=0,1$; the remaining ordinary punctures are the internal moduli. At $n=2$: $\mathcal V_3'$ has ordinary punctures at $w=\pm2\beta$ (A3), i.e. $u=1/w=\pm\tfrac1{2\beta}$; the affine map $u'=\beta u+\tfrac12$ puts them at $0,1$ and gives $w=\beta/(u'-\tfrac12)$:
$$
\mathcal V_3'=\big(\{0,1,\infty\};\ (\lambda,c)=(\beta,\tfrac12)\big).
\tag{1.2}
$$

*(1.5) Why the scale is fixed, not chosen.* The $\mathcal V_4$–$\mathcal V_4$ Feynman region of $\mathcal M_{0,6}$ in the channel $\{0,1,m_L\}|\{0,1,m_R\}$ is, by definition (A2), the plumbing $w_Lw_R=q$, $|q|\le1$, of two $\mathcal V_{0,4}$ surfaces *in their own local coordinates at the joined legs*; its boundary is $|q|=1$. The brief's requirement — that the twist-only plumbing $|q|=1$ of two patches reproduce these propagator-boundary surfaces — is therefore satisfied if the special coordinate of the patch is $\mathcal V_{0,4}$'s own coordinate at the special leg up to a phase (a rotation $\lambda\to e^{i\alpha}\lambda$ is $q\to e^{i\alpha}q$, invisible on the set $|q|=1$). Conversely, given the centre $c(m)$, the scale is forced: if the patch's scale is $f(m)$ in place of $|\lambda(m)|$ (same centre), $w'_i=(f_i/|\lambda_i|)\,w_i$ up to phase, and the plumbing $w'_Lw'_R=q$, $|q|=1$, is the $\mathcal V_{0,4}$ plumbing $w_Lw_R=q_V$ at $|q_V|=|\lambda(m_L)||\lambda(m_R)|/(f(m_L)f(m_R))$, which (the $\mathcal V_{0,4}$ plumbing family being locally injective near $|q_V|=1$) is the propagator boundary for all $(m_L,m_R)$ only if $f(m_L)f(m_R)=|\lambda(m_L)||\lambda(m_R)|$, hence (at $m_L=m_R$) $f=|\lambda|$. Necessity is not claimed for $c(m)$: a different centre could foliate the same hypersurface $\{|q|=1\}$ differently, and $c(m)$ is left to the sister's section (fix pass 1, #7). This is R2's logic at $n=2$ ($\mathcal V_3'$'s special map is $\mathcal V_3$'s coordinate at $\beta'=\beta$, and $|q|=1$ gives $\partial\mathcal V_{0,4}$).
