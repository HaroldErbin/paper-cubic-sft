## Task and objective

- **Objective.** One convention under which every pairing of odd-strand against even-strand tensors ($\Theta_k$ against $\Sigma_k$, outputs of $p_{m,n}$ against fields) and every product with two odd arguments ($\ell_{n+2}(\Lambda_1,\Lambda_2,\Psi^n)$) is well defined and non-vanishing where the records say it is.

A1 (local): a strand is *input* when it is an argument of a vertex or product, *output* when $\omega_n$ sets it against a field strand ($\Theta_k$ multiplies $\Sigma_k-\Psi^k$ through $\omega_k$). **Answer (1).** MS define $SA_c$ as the graded symmetric algebra in the degree shifted by two, an even shift: graded symmetric in the physical parity on every strand, as EKS. ## Step 3 – 026, 055, C6; cases (b)–(d); answer to question (2)

Rule (iii), (1.7): raising a leg of parity $\varepsilon_b$ gives a vector of parity $\varepsilon_a=\varepsilon_b+1$; the vertex symmetry in $\varepsilon_b$ becomes symmetry of the outputs in $\bar\varepsilon_a$: $p_{k,\ell}:\mathrm{Sym}^k_\varepsilon\mathcal H\to\mathrm{Sym}^\ell_{\bar\varepsilon}\mathcal H$ – the proposal, word for word. R1 (1.8) is the same rule for Koszul commutation of an output strand.

**Answer (2).** The paper does write "graded symmetric"; what must be added is *in which parity*, and that depends on the role: $\varepsilon$ for arguments of vertices and products, $\varepsilon+1$ for strands dual to them under the $n$-string pairing. In the physical parity on every strand (EKS, MS) the multiplier term vanishes identically (N5) and the cubic action has no $\Theta_k(\Sigma_k-\Psi^k)$ term. The per-role sentence settles the ghost-number-1 case without a separate rule (N3).

## Step 4 – Decision; case (e)

**N4.** Exchange signs $(-1)^{\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j}$ and $(-1)^{(\varepsilon_i+1)(\varepsilon_j+1)}=(-1)^{\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j+\varepsilon_i+\varepsilon_j+1}$: equal iff $\varepsilon_i+\varepsilon_j=1$. Mixed tensors (strands $(1,2)$, $(3,2)$, …) have the same type in both gradings and in EKS/MS. On homogeneous strands the types are different subspaces ($\dim\mathrm{Sym}^2=d(d+1)/2\ne d(d-1)/2$), and no re-signing $T^{ab}\mapsto(-1)^{f(a,b)}T^{ab}$ maps one into the other (the diagonal $a=b$ would need $(-1)^0=-1$): the per-role distinction is a fact of the arrays, not a sign choice.

**Decision.** The proposal passes every case; no fix. (1) *Input strands* (arguments of vertices and products: $\Psi^n$, $\Sigma_n$, gauge parameters, equations of motion): graded symmetric in $\varepsilon$ – EKS, MS; even strands symmetric, odd strands antisymmetric. (2) *Output strands* (set against inputs by $\omega_n$: outputs of $p_{m,n}$, $\Theta_k$): graded symmetric in $\varepsilon+1$ – the dual under $\omega_n$ (055 B2) and the image of the raising (026 (1.7)); at $N_{\rm gh}=3$ symmetric components, C6's $\Lambda^n_{\rm gr}\mathcal H_3$; departs from EKS/MS, who never pair such strands. (3) *Mixed tensors*: sign $+1$ either way, no role to name. (4) *IBL outputs of section 7*: output strands of any ghost number, rule (2). One symbol $\Hn{n}$ for both roles; an `\agcomment{choice}` offers a second symbol for the dual space.
