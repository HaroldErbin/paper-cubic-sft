## §1 (C1)

*Setup.* Sector $m=0$, constant cutoff $\sigma$: $\hat f_0(h)=2\pi\,\hat\mu(h)$ with $\hat\mu(h)=(e^{\sigma h}-1)/h=\mathcal L[\mu]$, $\mu=\mathbf 1_{[-\sigma,0]}$ (074 (2.6)); $1/\hat f_0=\frac{1}{2\pi}\cdot\frac{h}{e^{\sigma h}-1}$, so beyond the ghost pair $c_0^-c_0^+$ and the twist normalization $1/2\pi$ the whole content of the inverse is the scalar $1/\hat\mu$ per $L_0^+$-eigenvalue (within the circle-state block, i.e. with the projector $P_{\mathcal I}$ of §6, Conditional(PH31)). 074 l. 154 already records the Bernoulli series and its radius in one line ("local in the sense of finitely many derivatives at each order and non-local as a whole") without a worldsheet reading; this section develops that reading and compares the two.

*(1.1) Two expansions of one function (proven).* $\frac{h}{e^{\sigma h}-1}$ admits the geometric/tower series (074 (2.6)), converging iff $\Re h>0$, and the Bernoulli series $\frac{h}{e^{\sigma h}-1}=\frac{1}{\sigma}\sum_{k\ge0}\frac{B_k}{k!}(\sigma h)^k$ ($B_0=1$, $B_1=-\tfrac12$, $B_2=\tfrac16$, odd $B_{k\ge3}=0$), converging iff $|h|<2\pi/\sigma$. The domains overlap on the lens $\{0<\Re h\}\cap\{|h|<2\pi/\sigma\}$, where both represent the same analytic function; hence, applied eigenvalue by eigenvalue to $(L_0^+,L_0^-)$ eigenstates, the two series define the same sector operator $F^{-1}|_{m=0}$ wherever both converge, and each is the analytic continuation of the other elsewhere.

*(1.2) The two kernels (proven at the transform level).* Since $\mathcal L[\delta^{(k)}](h)=h^k$, the Bernoulli series is the Laplace transform of $B(s):=\frac{1}{\sigma}\sum_{k\ge0}\frac{B_k\sigma^k}{k!}\delta^{(k)}(s)$, an infinite-order differential operator supported at $s=0$: a zero-length neck carrying $L_0^+=-\partial_s$ insertions of the plumbing family at the gluing circle. The tower is the transform of $T(s)=\sum_{n\ge1}\delta'(s-n\sigma)$ (074 (2.6)). Distinct objects, one operator: $T$ is an honest distribution (discrete support on $\sigma\mathbb Z_{>0}$, order 1), $B$ is not a distribution but an analytic functional at the point $s=0$ (infinite order); $\mathcal L[T]=\mathcal L[B]$ on the lens, so both realise $F^{-1}|_{m=0}$ where their series converge, and neither covers all of $\mathcal H$: the tower needs $\Re h>0$ per eigenvalue, $B$ needs $|h|<2\pi/\sigma$ per eigenvalue.

*(1.3) Radius and poles (proven).* The Bernoulli radius is exactly $2\pi/\sigma$: the nearest singularities of $h/(e^{\sigma h}-1)$ are the simple poles at $h=\pm2\pi i/\sigma$, the nearest zeros of $\hat f_0$ (074 (2.7), poles at $h=2\pi ik/\sigma$; the residue of $h/(e^{\sigma h}-1)$ at $h_0=2\pi i/\sigma$ is $h_0/\sigma\neq0$, so they are genuine poles, not removable). The local reading thus makes the pole lattice of $p^{-1}$ visible as its circle of convergence: the series fails exactly where the block operator $G_W$ degenerates on the sector. The tower's half-plane $\Re h>0$ has the entire pole lattice on its boundary ($\Re(2\pi ik/\sigma)=0$), from the periodicity $e^{-n\sigma(h+2\pi i/\sigma)}=e^{-n\sigma h}$; and the tower series is not even defined at $h=0$ (terms $he^{-n\sigma h}\to h\neq0$), its boundary value $h\to0^+$ being $1/\sigma$, whereas the Bernoulli series is regular at $h=0$ with value $1/\sigma$ — the massless on-shell point is covered by the local reading alone. The two lattices are reciprocal: neck lengths $n\sigma$ in $s$, poles at $2\pi ik/\sigma$ in $h$.

*(1.4) Domains and physical reading (argued).* The readings are adapted to complementary regions of the $m=0$ sector. Local: near $h=0$ — the massless on-shell point, and the tachyon ($h=-2$ at $P=0$, $N=\bar N=0$), inside $|h|<2\pi/\sigma$ for the actual region's $\sigma\in[\ln\tfrac94,\ln4\beta^2]$ ($2\pi/\sigma\in[2.87,7.75]$) — the near-on-shell and mildly timelike regime, where the tower's Neumann branch diverges and only its reversed branch (negative lengths, 074 (2.6) second line) covers it. Tower: deep Euclidean / high level ($h\to+\infty$), where the local series diverges. This complements R80, which worked the tower branch only. For the actual cutoff the local reading extends to the $m=0$ sector as the Taylor expansion of $\frac{1}{2\pi}\frac{h}{\hat\nu(h)-1}$ about $h=0$ (regular, value $1/(2\pi\bar\sigma)$, $\bar\sigma$ the mean of $\sigma_V$; radius = nearest complex zero of $\hat\nu(h)-1$, none of them real, 074 (2.8)) — checked numerically below.

*(1.5) The composite in the two readings (collapse identities proven; the worldsheet readings argued).* $\hat f_0\hat f_0^{-1}\hat f_0=\hat f_0$ factorises through $\hat\mu\cdot\frac{h}{e^{\sigma h}-1}=1$ exactly. In $s$-space, both kernels collapse the left neighbour's slab to the same point:

the first by infinite-order differentiation at the slab's two endpoints ($\mu*B=\frac{1}{\sigma}\sum_k\frac{B_k\sigma^k}{k!}\partial^k\mu$, $\partial^k\mu=\delta_0^{(k-1)}-\delta^{(k-1)}_{-\sigma}$ type terms, everything at the outer boundary $s=-\sigma$ cancelling against the inner one), the second because $\partial_S\mu(S-n\sigma)=\delta(S-(n-1)\sigma)-\delta(S-n\sigma)$ and the chain telescopes to $\delta(S)=\delta_{s=0}$.
