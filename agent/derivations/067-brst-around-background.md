## 0. Task and objective

**Literal task.** For a solution $(\Psi_0,\Sigma_0)$ of the cubic HS equations at truncation order $k\in\{2,3\}$ (R1, R17), construct the linearised BRST operator $\mathcal Q_0=(\,\cdot\,,S_{\rm BV})|_{\rm lin}$ of R62's master action expanded at the background, in block form on the fluctuations $(\delta\Psi,\delta\Sigma,\delta c,\dots)$, and prove in the finite cyclic $L_\infty$ model of 058/060: (i) $\mathcal Q_0^2=O(g^{k+1})$; (ii) the auxiliary directions form a contractible pair around the background if $g\ne0$ and $\mathcal K_{\Psi_0}$ is invertible (necessity is shown only for $\ker V_4$ kept at $k=2$); (iii) $H(\mathcal Q_0)\cong H(Q^{(k)}_{\Psi_0})$, $Q^{(k)}_{\Psi_0}$ the truncated shifted operator of Zwiebach 1993 (4.112).

**Motivating objective (Task 22).** The spectrum of physical fluctuations of the cubic theory around a background is that of the nonpolynomial theory. R66 showed that the $\Psi$ block alone is not nilpotent even at the saddle (it carries one third of the $\ell_3$ part); the theorem must be about the joint operator on $(\delta\Psi,\delta\Sigma)$ with the ghost sector. This record constructs that operator and proves the statement at the linearised level, where it turns out to be R65's congruence lifted to the BV Hessian: the linearised cotangent lift of $\Sigma\mapsto\Sigma-\Sigma_*(\Psi)$ block-diagonalises $\mathcal Q_0$ into $Q^{(k)}_{\Psi_0}$ (to the truncation order) and an exactly contractible pair with coefficient $\mathcal K_{\Psi_0}$.

## 1. Block form of $\mathcal Q_0$ (C1)

\subsection*{Setup}
A1–A4. Coordinates $z=(\psi,\Sigma,\Sigma^+)$ with $\psi$ Zwiebach's unrestricted field (components by ghost number $G$: fields $\Psi$ at $G=2$, ghosts $c$ at $G=1$, ghosts-for-ghosts $G\le0$, antifields $\Psi^+$ at $G=3$, $c^+$ at $G=4$, …), $\Sigma\in\mathcal S$ even, $\Sigma^+$ odd. Antibracket bivector $P_{\rm ext}=P\oplus J$, $P$ the $\psi$-sector bivector of C2 ($P=\omega^{-1}\operatorname{diag}((-1)^{\deg})$ in the model, 058 Part B), $J$ the $(\Sigma,\Sigma^+)$ pair with $P^{\Sigma\Sigma^+}=1$. Linearised BRST operator: $s\,\delta z^A=(\delta z^A,S_{\rm BV})|_{\rm lin}=P_{\rm ext}^{AB}\,\partial_B\partial_C S_{\rm BV}|_{z_0}\,\delta z^C$, i.e.
$$\mathcal Q_0=P_{\rm ext}\,H_{\rm ext},\qquad H_{\rm ext}:=\partial\partial S_{\rm BV}\big|_{z_0}\ \text{(graded symmetric)}. \tag{1.1}$$
$T^{(k)}_{\psi_0}:=P\,\partial_\psi\partial_\psi S^{(k)}_{\rm BV}|_{\psi_0}$ is the truncated shifted operator on the unrestricted field, $Q^{(k)}_{\Psi_0}=Q+g\ell_2(\Psi_0,\cdot)+\dots+\tfrac{g^k}{k!}\ell_{k+1}(\Psi_0^k,\cdot)$ (Zwiebach (4.112) truncated; on fields $\omega(x,T^{(k)}y)=\mathrm{Hess}\,S^{(k)}_{\rm eff}$, 060 (2.1)). $H_{\Psi\Psi},H_{\Sigma\Psi},H_{\Sigma\Sigma}$ are the classical blocks 060 (1.2)–(1.4); $\Sigma_*'=-H_{\Sigma\Sigma}^{-1}H_{\Sigma\Psi}$ (060 (1.6)); $D_{\Psi_0}:=T^{(k)}_{\psi_0}|_{c\to\Psi}$ the linearised gauge generator $\partial_cu_c(\Psi_0)=Qc+g\ell_2(\Psi_0,c)+\dots$.

\subsection*{Derivation}
In the $(\Psi,\Sigma)$ chart (A1) the BV action is $S_{\rm BV}=S^{\rm cub}(\Psi,\Sigma)+[S^{\Psi,(k)}_{\rm BV}-S^{(k)}_{\rm eff}](\psi)|_{\Psi^+\to\Psi^++(\partial_\Psi\Sigma_*)^\top\Sigma^++\dots}+\Sigma^+\!\cdot F^{\rm flat}_{u_c}$, where $S^{\rm cub}$ is the classical cubic action, the bracket is Zwiebach's antifield tower (it contains $\Psi^+\!\cdot u_c$ with the full $\tfrac{g^2}2\ell_3(\Psi,\Psi,c)$: these terms are not generated by $\Sigma$), and $F^{\rm flat}_u=\Sigma_*'(\Psi)u-G_\Psi^{-1}(\partial_uG_\Psi)(Y)$ is the flat $\Sigma$-law (056 (3.1); $Y=\Sigma-\Sigma_*(\Psi)$). At the background ($c=0$, antifields $0$, $Y_0=0$) every antifield term is at least linear in $c$, so the second derivatives that survive are: the classical Hessian on $(\Psi,\Sigma)$; the antifield–ghost entries $\partial_{\Psi^+}\partial_c=D_{\Psi_0}$ and $\partial_{\Sigma^+}\partial_c=\partial_c F^{\rm flat}_{u_c}|_{Y_0=0}=\Sigma_*'(\Psi_0)D_{\Psi_0}$ (the $G$-term vanishes at $Y=0$); and the tower entries $\partial_{c^+}\partial_{c_{(0)}}$, …, which are those of $S^{(k)}_{\rm BV}$. Hence, with the overall sign of the classical block read off from $P^{-1}T^{(k)}$ (in the model $H_{\rm ext}[\Psi,\Psi]=(P^{-1}T)[\Psi,\Psi]=-\mathrm{Hess}$, so all classical blocks enter with one common sign),
$$H_{\rm ext}=\begin{pmatrix}H^{\rm cub}_{\psi\psi}&-H_{\Psi\Sigma}\,\delta_{\Psi}&0\\-H_{\Sigma\Psi}\,\delta_\Psi^\top&-H_{\Sigma\Sigma}&0\\\Sigma_*'D_{\Psi_0}\,\delta_c&0&0\end{pmatrix}\!,\qquad H^{\rm cub}_{\psi\psi}:=P^{-1}T^{\rm cub},\quad T^{\rm cub}:=T^{(k)}_{\psi_0}\ \text{with}\ T^{(k)}\big|_{\Psi\to\Psi^+}\to\omega^{-1}H_{\Psi\Psi}, \tag{1.2}$$
where $\delta_\Psi,\delta_c$ select the field and ghost components, the $(c,\Sigma^+)$ entry is $-(\Sigma_*'D)^\top$ (odd–odd antisymmetry), and by 060 (2.2)
$$\omega^{-1}H_{\Psi\Psi}=Q+g\ell_2(\Psi_0,\cdot)+\tfrac{g^2}{3}L_3(\Sigma_0)\quad(=T^{(k)}|_{\Psi\to\Psi^+}-\tfrac23g^2L_3(P_0)\ [+\tfrac{g^2}3L_3(\Sigma_0-P_0)-\tfrac{g^3}{3!}\ell_4(\Psi_0^3,\cdot)=-\tfrac45\tfrac{g^3}{3!}\ell_4(\Psi_0^3,\cdot)+O(g^4)\ \text{at }k=3,\ \text{under R38}]). \tag{1.3}$$
The flat law fixes the $(\Sigma^+,c)$ entry independently of any sign convention: $s\,\delta\Sigma=\Sigma_*'\,s\,\delta\Psi$ on the gauge orbit, i.e. $\mathcal Q_0\,y=\Sigma_*'D_{\Psi_0}\delta c$. Multiplying (1.2) by $P_{\rm ext}$ (with $P[\Psi^+,\Psi]=-\Omega_{12}^{-1}$, $\Omega_{12}=\omega|_{\Psi\times\Psi^+}$, $J[\Sigma^+,\Sigma]=-1$, $J[\Sigma,\Sigma^+]=+1$):
$$\boxed{\begin{aligned}\mathcal Q_0\,\psi&=T^{\rm cub}\psi+\Omega_{12}^{-1}H_{\Psi\Sigma}\,y\ \ (\text{in the }\Psi^+\text{ components})-P[c^+,c]\,(\Sigma_*'D_{\Psi_0})^\top y^+\ \ (\text{in the }c^+\text{ components}),\\ \mathcal Q_0\,y&=\Sigma_*'(\Psi_0)\,D_{\Psi_0}\,\delta c,\\ \mathcal Q_0\,y^+&=H_{\Sigma\Psi}\,x+H_{\Sigma\Sigma}\,y=\tfrac{g^2}{6}\Big[V_4(\cdot,\Psi_0\odot x)+\tfrac{g}{10}V_5(\cdot,\Sigma_0,x)\Big]-\tfrac{g^2}{12}\mathcal K_{\Psi_0}\,y,\end{aligned}} \tag{1.4}$$
with $x=\delta\Psi$, $\mathcal K_{\Psi_0}=V_4-\tfrac g5V_5(\cdot,\cdot,\Psi_0)$ at $k=3$ ($=V_4$ at $k=2$), $H_{\Psi\Sigma}=H_{\Sigma\Psi}^\top$. The sign $\sigma$ of the $y^+$ row is $+1$ in these conventions: a local convention tied to the sign of $J$ (absorbed by $y^+\to-y^+$), not fixed by nilpotency. Graded symmetry of $H_{\rm ext}$ relates $H[\Psi,\Sigma]$ to $H[\Sigma,\Psi]$ and $H[c,\Sigma^+]$ to $H[\Sigma^+,c]$ (imposed by construction in the script on the $\Sigma$ sector, so C1-sym cannot fail there); it does not fix the relative sign of $H_{\Sigma\Sigma}$ against $H_{\Sigma\Psi}$ and $H_{\Psi\Psi}$, which is their common origin in $S^{\rm cub}$ and is falsified by $T^{\rm eff}=T^{(2)}$ in C3-decouple. Affinity: every block of (1.4) is affine in $(\Psi_0,\Sigma_0)$ at $k=2$ ($T^{(2)}$ is quadratic in $\Psi_0$ through $\ell_3(\Psi_0,\Psi_0,\cdot)$, but $H_{\Psi\Psi}$ is affine in $(\Psi_0,\Sigma_0)$ and $D_{\Psi_0}$ carries the full $\ell_3(\Psi_0,\Psi_0,c)$ – so "affine in the background" holds for the classical blocks, which is Task 21's statement, and not for the ghost blocks, which are those of Zwiebach's operator).

\subsection*{Commentary}
- (1.2): the only non-obvious entries are the two $\Sigma$-couplings. The $(\Psi,\Sigma)$ entry is the classical cross Hessian $H_{\Sigma\Psi}$ of 060 (1.3); the $(\Sigma^+,c)$ entry is the linearised flat law at $Y_0=0$, where the $G_\Psi$-term of 056 (3.1) drops out, so the hatted chart's $G_\Psi$ plays no role at the linearised level (its only effect is a $\Psi_0$-dependent, fluctuation-independent rescaling of the pair).
- (1.3): 060 (2.2), R66. The field–antifield block of the cubic BV operator carries one third of the $\ell_3$ part; the ghost blocks ($c\to\Psi$, $\Psi^+\to c^+$, tower) carry the full $\tfrac{g^2}2\ell_3(\Psi_0,\Psi_0,\cdot)$ because they come from $S^{\Psi,(k)}_{\rm BV}$'s antifield terms, which the auxiliary does not generate. R66's operator $Q^{\rm cub}$ on all degrees is therefore not the $\psi$ block of the BV operator; the two agree on fields only.
- (1.4), last line: at $k=2$ the $y$-coupling $\tfrac{g^2}{6}V_4(\cdot,\Psi_0\odot x)$ is exactly $H_{\Sigma\Psi}$; on the quotient $\mathcal S$ it is well defined because $V_4$ vanishes on $\ker V_4$.

\subsection*{Result}
(1.4) with (1.3). Verification: C1-sym, C1-blocks of `codes/verify-brst-background.py` (§4).

## 2. Nilpotency (C2)

\subsection*{Setup}
A1–A4; R62 ($(S_{\rm BV},S_{\rm BV})=O(g^{k+1})$ identically in the fields, in the hatted chart hence in every chart). $z_0$ the background, $H=H_{\rm ext}$, $P=P_{\rm ext}$.

\subsection*{Derivation}
Differentiating $(S,S)=\partial^rS\,P\,\partial^lS$ twice (convention C2):
$$\partial_A\partial_B(S,S)=2\,(HPH)_{AB}+2\,\partial_CS\;P^{CD}\,\partial_D\partial_A\partial_BS \qquad(\text{signs of C2; 058 (3.5)}). \tag{2.1}$$
At the background, $\partial S_{\rm BV}|_{z_0}$ has only the classical components $\partial_\Psi S^{\rm cub}$, $\partial_\Sigma S^{\rm cub}$: $\partial_{\phi^+}S=u_c+\dots$ vanishes at $c=0$, $\partial_cS=\Psi^+\!\cdot\partial_cu_c+\dots$ at $\phi^+=0$. So
$$\mathcal Q_0^2=(PH)^2=P\,(HPH)=\tfrac12P\,\partial\partial(S_{\rm BV},S_{\rm BV})\big|_{z_0}-P\,\partial_CS^{\rm cub}\,P^{CD}\,\partial_D\partial\partial S_{\rm BV}\big|_{z_0}. \tag{2.2}$$
The first term is $O(g^{k+1})$ by R62 (the coefficient function of $(S,S)$ at $g^{k+1}$ is a polynomial in the fields, and its Hessian at an $O(1)$ background is $O(1)$). The second term is linear in the classical EOM residual. Hence:
$$\boxed{\mathcal Q_0^2=O(g^{k+1})\ \text{at a critical point of }S^{(k)}_{\rm BV}\ (\text{exactly}\ \tfrac12P\,\partial\partial(S,S)),\quad\text{and at an untruncated MC point}\ (\text{residual }O(g^{k+1}),\ \text{R63}).} \tag{2.3}$$
Only the classical $\Psi$- and $\Sigma$-EOM are needed; the $\Sigma$-EOM is $\hat Y_0=0$, i.e. $\Sigma_0=\Sigma_*(\Psi_0)$ (A4). Off shell, $\mathcal Q_0^2=-P\,\partial S^{\rm cub}\cdot P\,\partial^3S_{\rm BV}+O(g^{k+1})$: the defect is linear in the residual (Zwiebach (4.117): $Q'^2=-[F,\cdot]'$), of order $g\times$residual. At $k=2$ the statement is $O(g^3)$ (R1, $\mathbb F\equiv0$); at $k=3$, $O(g^4)$ with R62's coefficient $-2(S_2,S_6)+2(S_2,\mathbb F^{(6)})$.

\subsection*{Commentary}
(2.1)–(2.2) are the linearisation of the master equation; nothing specific to the cubic theory enters except through R62. The order counting is in the formal parameter $g$ with $\Psi_0=O(1)$ (A3); for a background $\Psi_0=O(1/g)$ every order mixes and the statement is "$\mathcal Q_0^2=\tfrac12P\,\partial\partial(S^{(k)},S^{(k)})$ at a critical point", with no small parameter.

\subsection*{Result}
(2.3). Verification: C2-order and C2-break-offshell (§4).

## 3. Contractible pair and quasi-isomorphism (C3)

\subsection*{Setup}
A1–A4, C1. $\mathcal L$: the linearisation at $z_0$ of the cotangent lift of the field map $(\Psi,\Sigma)\mapsto(\Psi,Y=\Sigma-\Sigma_*(\Psi))$ (056 C1 without the $G_\Psi$ factor):
$$y'=y-\Sigma_*'(\Psi_0)\,x,\qquad \delta\Psi^{+\prime}=\delta\Psi^++\Omega_{12}^{-1}\Sigma_*'^{\,\top}\,y^+,\qquad\text{all other coordinates unchanged}. \tag{3.1}$$
The field part is R65's block-unipotent $L$ (060 (1.15)); the antifield shift is the unique one making $\mathcal L$ canonical, $\mathcal LP_{\rm ext}\mathcal L^\top=P_{\rm ext}$ (derived by direct multiplication; check C3-canonical). $T^{\rm eff}$: $T^{\rm cub}$ with its field–antifield block replaced by $\Omega_{12}^{-1}H_{\rm eff}$, $H_{\rm eff}=H_{\Psi\Psi}-H_{\Psi\Sigma}H_{\Sigma\Sigma}^{-1}H_{\Sigma\Psi}$ (R65 (1.5)).

\subsection*{Derivation}
Conjugating (1.4) by (3.1). The $y'$ row: $\mathcal Q_0'y'=\mathcal Q_0y-\Sigma_*'\mathcal Q_0x=\Sigma_*'D\delta c-\Sigma_*'D\delta c=0$. The $y^+$ row in the new variables: $H_{\Sigma\Psi}x+H_{\Sigma\Sigma}(y'+\Sigma_*'x)=H_{\Sigma\Sigma}y'$ since $H_{\Sigma\Sigma}\Sigma_*'=-H_{\Sigma\Psi}$. The $\Psi^{+\prime}$ row (using the new form $s\,y^+=H_{\Sigma\Sigma}y'$ and $s\,x$ from (1.4)): $\Omega_{12}^{-1}[H_{\Psi\Psi}x+H_{\Psi\Sigma}(y'+\Sigma_*'x)]+\Omega_{12}^{-1}\Sigma_*'^\top H_{\Sigma\Sigma}\,y'=\Omega_{12}^{-1}[H_{\Psi\Psi}+H_{\Psi\Sigma}\Sigma_*']\,x+\Omega_{12}^{-1}[H_{\Psi\Sigma}+\Sigma_*'^\top H_{\Sigma\Sigma}]\,y'=\Omega_{12}^{-1}H_{\rm eff}\,x,$ since $\Sigma_*'^\top H_{\Sigma\Sigma}=-H_{\Psi\Sigma}$ kills the second bracket and $H_{\Psi\Sigma}\Sigma_*'=-H_{\Psi\Sigma}H_{\Sigma\Sigma}^{-1}H_{\Sigma\Psi}$. The $c^{+\prime}$ row: $H[c,\Psi^+]$ and $H[c,\Sigma^+]$ are the $\Psi^+$- and $\Sigma^+$-derivatives of the same term $\hat\Psi^+\!\cdot D\,\delta c$, $\hat\Psi^+=\Psi^++\Sigma_*'^\top\Sigma^+$ at $Y=0$ (the cotangent-lift identity that also gives the $(\Sigma^+,c)$ entry), so $s\,\delta c^+=A\,(\delta\Psi^++\Omega_{12}^{-1}\Sigma_*'^\top y^+)=A\,\delta\Psi^{+\prime}$ with $A=T^{(k)}|_{\Psi^+\to c^+}$: derived, and confirmed numerically (C3-decouple). $\mathcal L$ shifts each coordinate by coordinates of the same ghost number, hence preserves ghost number (needed for the degree-by-degree isomorphism). Hence, exactly,
$$\boxed{\mathcal L\,\mathcal Q_0\,\mathcal L^{-1}=T^{\rm eff}_{\psi_0}\ \oplus\ \mathcal Q^{\rm pair},\qquad \mathcal Q^{\rm pair}:\ y'\mapsto0,\quad y^+\mapsto H_{\Sigma\Sigma}\,y'=-\tfrac{g^2}{12}\,\mathcal K_{\Psi_0}\,y'.} \tag{3.2}$$
$\mathcal Q^{\rm pair}$ is nilpotent exactly. ### 6.4 Transfer of the finite-model proof (C5.1–C5.4)

\subsection*{Derivation}
**(a) Which entries of $H_{\rm ext}$ survive at the background (C5.1).** In the $(\Psi,\Sigma)$ chart, by R60, $S_{\rm BV}=S^{\rm cub}(\Psi,\Sigma)+\big[Z^{(k)}-S^{(k)}_{\rm eff}\big](\psi)\big|_{\Psi^+\to\hat\Psi^+}$ (the antifield-free part is $S^{\rm cub}$ exactly, 056 (3.3): $S_{\rm eff}+\mathbb F+\Phi^*=S^{\rm cub}$; the $\Sigma^+F^{\rm flat}_{u_c}$ term is generated by $\hat\Psi^+\!\cdot u_c$) with $\hat\Psi^+\!\cdot w=\Psi^+\!\cdot w+\Sigma^+\!\cdot F^{\rm flat}_w$. A vertex of $Z^{(k)}$ with $n$ legs contributes to $\partial_a\partial_bS_{\rm BV}|_{z_0}$ only if its $n-2$ undifferentiated legs are all $\Psi$ ($G=2$), i.e.
$$G_a+G_b+2(n-2)=2n\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad G_a+G_b=4. \tag{6.4}$$
So the surviving $\psi$-sector entries pair $G_a$ with $4-G_a$: $(2,2)$ the classical Hessian, $(1,3)=(c,\Psi^+)$ the gauge generator $D_{\Psi_0}$, $(0,4)=(c_{(0)},c^+)$, $(-1,5)$, … the tower's kinetic-plus-background entries – every one of them an entry of $T^{(k)}_{\psi_0}$ – and no entry with two antifields ($G_a+G_b\ge6$) or two ghosts ($\le2$). Hence the antifield-number-two terms (the open-algebra term $\hat\Psi^+\hat\Psi^+Mcc$ and 056 C3.3's untracked normalisation) do not enter $\mathcal Q_0$ at all. For the $\Sigma$ sector: $S^{\rm cub}$ gives the $(\Sigma,\Psi)$ and $(\Sigma,\Sigma)$ entries (R65 (1.3)–(1.4)); the only $\Sigma^+$-terms are $\Sigma^+\!\cdot F^{\rm flat}_w$ with $w$ a coefficient of $\Psi^+$ in $Z^{(k)}$, i.e. legs of total ghost number $2n-3$ on $n-1$ legs: either $(c,\Psi^{n-2})$ – linear in the fluctuations, $w=D_{\Psi_0}\delta c$ – or combinations with at least two non-$\Psi$ legs ($\Psi^+c^+_{(0)}c_{(0)}c_{(0)}\Psi^{n-4}$ and the like), which two derivatives at the background kill. With $F^{\rm flat}_u(0;\Psi)=\Sigma_*'(\Psi)u$ (056 (3.1): the $G_\Psi$-term vanishes at $Y=0$; equivalently $s\hat Y=0$ and $G_\Psi(0)=0$ give $sY|_{Y=0}=0$), the $\Sigma^+$ row has the single entry $(\Sigma^+,c)=\Sigma_*'(\Psi_0)D_{\Psi_0}$, and (1.2)–(1.4) hold with the string-vertex blocks (6.2). Any other $\Sigma$-law admissible in R57's sense (R17's, R19's) agrees with the flat one at $Y=0$ exactly (R57 C1: admissible laws differ by trivial symmetries $\propto\nu(Y)$, $\nu(0)=0$), so the block form is chart-independent within that class; a law admissible only to $O(g^{k+1})$ may shift the $(\Sigma^+,c)$ entry at $O(g^{k-1})$ (since $\delta S\supset\partial_Y\Phi^*\cdot\delta Y=-\tfrac{g^2}{12}\mathcal K(Y,\delta Y|_{Y=0})+O(Y^2)$), and the $y'$ row of (3.2) then picks up $\epsilon\,\delta c$ with $\epsilon=O(g^{k-1})$; the theorem uses R62's flat law and is unaffected. The pair therefore couples only to $x=\delta\Psi$ ($G=2$, through $H_{\Sigma\Psi}$) and $\delta c$ ($G=1$, through $\Sigma_*'D$), and by $P_{\rm ext}$ its image lands in the $\delta\Psi^+$ ($G=3$) and $\delta c^+$ ($G=4$) rows: 056's minimal sector, the $G\in\{1,2\}/\{3,4\}$ part of Zwiebach's field. The ghost-for-ghost tower ($G\le0$ and $G\ge5$) is untouched by the pair and by $\mathcal L$ (which acts on $x$ and $\delta\Psi^+$ only): its block of $\mathcal Q_0$ is the tower block of $T^{(k)}_{\psi_0}$ verbatim, so the theorem's pair structure is degree-blind and the isomorphism (iii) is trivial at $G\le0$, $G\ge5$. What the full tower adds to 056's minimal sector is only this: the ghost numbers at which the shifted theory's reducibility bookkeeping lives; the theorem says nothing new there.

**(b) Nilpotency (C5.2).** (2.1)–(2.2) are the second derivative of the antibracket identity and use only that $\partial S_{\rm BV}|_{z_0}$ has classical components (antifield derivatives are $\propto$ ghosts, ghost derivatives $\propto$ antifields): dimension-free. The input is R62: $(S_{\rm BV},S_{\rm BV})=O(g^{k+1})$ identically in the fields, in the hatted chart hence in the $(\Psi,\Sigma)$ chart (the antibracket is invariant under 056 C1). On the algebraic layer the $g^{k+1}$ coefficient is the finite sum 058 (3.3), $\sum_{a+b=k+5,\,3\le a,b\le k+2}(S_a,S_b)+2\delta_{k,3}(S_2,\mathbb F^{(6)})$, a polynomial functional whose Hessian at $z_0$ is a finite sum of compositions $\ell_a(\Psi_0^{\,\cdot},\ell_b(\Psi_0^{\,\cdot},\cdot))$ with at most $k+1$ background insertions (and, in the $\mathbb F$ term, $V_4^{-1}$ on the quotient, defined under (b) of (6.1)): $O(1)$ under A7. Momentum: the master equation is an identity in the fields with no momentum restriction; R62's exclusion of on-shell momenta belongs to its properness clause (rank of the Hessian at generic momentum), which (ii) does not use.
