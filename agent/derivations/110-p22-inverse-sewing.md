## §1 Definitions (C1)

\subsection*{Setup}
Uses A1, A3, A6. $\mathcal H^\downarrow=\ker b_0^+\cap\ker b_0^-$, $W=c_0^+c_0^-\mathcal H^\downarrow$ (074 §2); $\mathcal H^\downarrow_2$ its ghost-number-2 part, "even" meaning even $L_0^-$ sectors (the only ones the circle states of a graded-symmetric $\Sigma_2$ populate, 071 C3). A *pair functional* is $u\in(\mathrm{Sym}^2\mathcal H_0/\ker p)^*$; the composite is $p^{-1}(u,w)$ with $u=\mathcal V_5(\Psi^3,\cdot)$, $w=\mathcal V_5(\cdot,\Psi^3)$ (R38's condition, $u$ vanishing on $\ker p$, is the composite's definedness and is assumed). A *representative* of $u$ relative to a circle map $\Phi:\mathrm{Sym}^2\mathcal H_0\to\mathcal H_4$ is a state $x_u\in\mathcal H_2$ with $u(\Sigma)=\omega(x_u,\Phi\Sigma)$ for all $\Sigma$ (level sums under A6).

\subsection*{Derivation}
*(1.1) Sewing form of $p^{-1}$ on a class.* Let $\mathcal A\subset\mathcal H_2$ be a class of states and $T$ a current on $\mathbb R\times S^1\ni(s,\theta)$ (a distribution of finite order on each compact set, derivatives of measures allowed; the tower $\sum_n\delta'(s-n\sigma)$ and the deconvolution kernels of opencode-p22 (2.2) are examples). *$p^{-1}$ acts as a geometric sewing on $\mathcal A$ with current $T$* if for all $x,y\in\mathcal A$
$$
p^{-1}\big(\Phi^\dagger x,\Phi^\dagger y\big)=\int T(s,\theta)\,\big\langle x\big|\,c_0^-c_0^+\,\mathcal S(q)\,\big|y\big\rangle\,ds\,d\theta ,
\qquad \mathcal S(q)=e^{-sL_0^+}e^{i\theta L_0^-},
\tag{1.1}
$$
where $\Phi^\dagger x:=\omega(x,\Phi\,\cdot)$ is the functional $x$ represents and the matrix element is the level sum $\sum_{h,m}\langle x|c_0^-c_0^+|y\rangle_{h,m}e^{-sh+im\theta}$, so that (1.1) reads $p^{-1}(\Phi^\dagger x,\Phi^\dagger y)=\sum_{h,m}\hat T(h,m)\langle x|c_0^-c_0^+|y\rangle_{h,m}$ with $\hat T(h,m)=\int T e^{-sh+im\theta}$ the Laplace–Fourier transform, wherever the sum converges (A6). Two shapes are distinguished: **(i)** the representatives $x,y$ are $q$-independent states (the form above); **(ii)** a *$q$-dependent input map* $T_q$: the representatives are sections $x(q)$, $y(q)$ over the $(s,\theta)$ plane, $x(q)=T_q(u)$, and (1.1) is replaced by $p^{-1}(u,v)=\int\langle T_q u|\,c_0^-c_0^+\mathcal S(q)\,|T_qv\rangle\,d\tau(s,\theta)$ with a measure $\tau$ — the two shapes R80's annotation of 2026-10-05 (record 094) leaves open. Shape (i) with $\Phi$ a $q$-independent circle map is what "a current on $(s,\theta)$ alone" means in N7.3. [C1.1]

*Pexider.* Vary $(\ell,\ell')$ at fixed $\ell\!\cdot\!\ell'$, $P\!\cdot\!\ell$, $P\!\cdot\!\ell'$: in $d\ge4$ dimensions $\ell^2$ and $\ell'^2$ move independently on an open set (e.g. $\ell=(a,b,0,\dots)$, $\ell'=(c/a,0,f,\dots)$ in the complement of $P$: $\ell\!\cdot\!\ell'=c$ fixed, $\ell^2=a^2+b^2$, $\ell'^2=c^2/a^2+f^2$ free in $b,f$). *(3.5) The sl(2) lemma: which dressed kernels factorise.* At fixed momentum $P$ (generic, so that the matter Verma modules of $\mathfrak{sl}(2)\oplus\overline{\mathfrak{sl}(2)}$ generated by $L^{\rm m}_{0,\pm1},\bar L^{\rm m}_{0,\pm1}$ are irreducible; other $P$ by continuity of the matrix elements), the matter Fock space decomposes as $\bigoplus_{n,\bar n}\mathrm{QP}_{n\bar n}\otimes\mathcal V_{h_P+n}\otimes\bar{\mathcal V}_{h_P+\bar n}$, with $\mathrm{QP}_{n\bar n}$ the quasi-primaries at level $(n,\bar n)$ (the multiplicity spaces) and $h_P=\tfrac{\alpha'}4P^2$. Let $\mathfrak M$ be the operators on (matter $\otimes$ ghost) of the form $A=\bigoplus_{n\bar n}\mathbb 1_{\mathrm{QP}_{n\bar n}}\otimes A_{n\bar n}$, $A_{n\bar n}$ arbitrary on $\mathcal V\otimes\bar{\mathcal V}\otimes(\text{ghosts})$. Then:

- (i) $\mathfrak M$ is closed under sums, products, $q$-integrals and inverses (block by block).
- (ii) It contains $c_0^\pm$, $b_0^\pm$, every ghost operator, functions of $L_0^\pm$ (total), $\mathcal S(q)$, and the Virasoro operator of every Möbius map fixing the special puncture, $\tilde\kappa^{L_0}\bar{\tilde\kappa}^{\bar L_0}e^{\tilde v L_1+\bar{\tilde v}\bar L_1}$, and its BPZ conjugate ($e^{\tilde vL_1}=e^{\tilde vL^{\rm m}_1}e^{\tilde vL^{\rm gh}_1}$, the matter factor acting module by module). It does not contain $L^{\rm m}_{n}$, $n\ge2$, which map quasi-primaries to non-quasi-primary combinations across multiplicity spaces.
- (iii) For $A\in\mathfrak M$ and any fixed half whose local coordinate at the special puncture is Möbius (legs anywhere, any leg coordinates), $\langle\phi^h(\ell)|A|\phi^h(\ell')\rangle$ is a function of $P^2$, $P\!\cdot\!\ell$, $P\!\cdot\!\ell'$, $\ell\!\cdot\!\ell'$ only.

Proof of (iii). By the block form, the $(n,\bar n)$ term is $\sum_{Q}\overline{c_Q(\ell)}\,c_Q(\ell')\,\langle\kappa_{n\bar n}(h_{12})|A_{n\bar n}|\kappa'_{n\bar n}(h_{34})\rangle$ in a basis of $\mathrm{QP}_{n\bar n}$ orthonormal for the Fock form, with $c_Q(\ell)=\langle Q|\phi^h(\ell)\rangle$ and $\kappa$ the vector of descendant overlaps divided by $c_Q$. The descendant overlaps follow from $\langle\phi|L^{\rm m}_{-1}=(L^{\rm m}_1|\phi\rangle)^\dagger$, with $L^{\rm m}_1$ acting on the two-puncture state as $\sum_i(a_i^2\partial_{a_i}+2h_ia_i)$ on the correlator: on $a_{12}^{h_Q-h_1-h_2}$ this gives $h_Q(a_1+a_2)+(h_1-h_2)(a_1-a_2)$, the $h_1+h_2$ terms cancelling. So $\kappa$ depends on $\ell$ only through $h_{12}=h_1-h_2=\tfrac{\alpha'}2P\!\cdot\!\ell$ (chirally). The multiplicity sum $\sum_Q\bar c_Q c'_Q$ is the coefficient $a_{n\bar n}$ of the global-block expansion of the four-tachyon correlator in the $s$-channel: in the frame $z_{1,2,3,4}=x,0,1,\infty$ the correlator is $|x|^{\alpha'k_1\cdot k_2}|1-x|^{\alpha'k_1\cdot k_3}$, and $\alpha'k_1\!\cdot\!k_2=2(h_P-h_1-h_2)$ is exactly the exponent the block prefactor $|x|^{2(h-h_1-h_2)}$ absorbs at $h=h_P+n$. The coefficients are therefore fixed by $h_P$, $h_{12}$, $h_{34}$ and $\alpha'k_1\!\cdot\!k_3=\alpha'\big(-\tfrac{P^2}4+\tfrac12P\!\cdot\!(\ell'-\ell)+\ell\!\cdot\!\ell'\big)$, and none of these contains $\ell^2$ or $\ell'^2$. \subsection*{Derivation}
*(4.1) Representability of the slices in the model's class.* (Fix pass 1, critic #6: the first run applied the model's kernel to the actual slices.) In the model, $p^{\rm mod}=(\pi_W\Phi^{\rm rig})^\dagger G(\pi_W\Phi^{\rm rig})$ (074 (2.2)(b)), so $\ker\pi_W\Phi^{\rm rig}\subseteq\ker p^{\rm mod}$.
