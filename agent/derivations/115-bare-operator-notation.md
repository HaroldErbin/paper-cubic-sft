# 115 – Can the bare operator of $\Sigma_k$ be written $w_{k,k}$ without an order label?

## Status

Complete (2026-11-07): steps 1–4 written, script 21/21 PASS, decision entry proposed. No ledger entry proposed: the result is a notation decision resting on a one-line structural fact already stated in `02-6-octic.tex` after `eq:hs8:scaling`.

## Task and objective

- **Literal task.** Decide whether the bare kinetic operator of $\Sigma_k$, written in the paper as `\kin{k}{}{2k-2}` $= k^{(2k-2)}_{k,k}$, can be written $w_{k,k}$ with no order label, i.e. whether it receives contributions beyond the order $g_s^{2k-2}$ at which $\Sigma_k$ enters.
- **Objective.** Fix the notation of section 3 before its rewrite (review comments at `paper/sections/03-all-orders.tex` lines 70, 91, 152): which operators need an order label, which do not.

## Conventions and assumptions

- C-ledger: the project `knowledge/conventions.md` applies; nothing in this record fixes a new convention.
- A1. The all-orders cubic action is eq. `eq:all:action` of `paper/sections/03-all-orders.tex` (R12 counting, R25 septic closure), with $\Sigma_1 := \Psi$ and the reduced vertices $\mathcal W_n$ built from the elementary vertices and the lower-order auxiliary fields only.
- A2. The pairing $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_k$ on $\mathcal H^{\otimes k}$ is symmetric and nondegenerate on the even tensors met here; $w_{k,k}$ is defined by $\mathcal W_{2k}(A,B) = \langle A, w_{k,k}(B)\rangle_k$ (eq. `eq:all:kinetic`).

## Claim tree

- **Root.** The $\Psi$-independent part of the quadratic form of $\Sigma_k$ is $\frac{g_s^{2k-2}}{(2k)!} w_{k,k}$ at every order in $g_s$, for every truncation $K \ge k$ and after the elimination of any subset of the other auxiliary fields; $w_{k,k}$ does not depend on $K$. Hence the bare operator is well defined without an order label. ← N1, N2, N3
- **N1.** Every term of the cubic action other than the kinetic terms of the $\Sigma_k$ and $S_{\le 3}$ contains $\Psi$. ← A1 [proven: Step 2, N1; checked: `N1-*`]
- **N2.** At $\Psi = 0$ the auxiliary sector is block-diagonal in the tower, with block $\frac{g_s^{2k-2}}{(2k)!} w_{k,k}$; eliminating any auxiliary field therefore adds only $\Psi$-dependent terms to the quadratic form of the others. ← N1, A2, R70 (analyticity of the inverses at $g_s\Psi = 0$) [proven: Step 2, (2.2); checked: `N2-*`]
- **N3.** $\mathcal W_{2k}$, hence $w_{k,k}$, is fixed at order $g_s^{2k-2}$ by the matching and the choice of representative, and is not modified at higher orders or by raising $K$. ← A1, `eq:hs7:deficit` [proven: Step 2, N3; checked: `N3-*`]
- **N4.** The order label is needed on the *dressed* operators: $k^{(2k-1)}_{k,k;\Psi}$ (bare plus odd vertex) differs from the nested $k^{(2K-1)}_{k,k;\Psi}$ (after eliminating $\Sigma_{k+1},\dots,\Sigma_K$) by $\Psi$-dependent exchange terms at $O(g_s^2)$, which depend on $K$. ← N2, `eq:all:nested` [proven: Step 2, Result; checked: `N4-*`]

Open gaps: none.
Hypotheses in force: none.

## Step 1 – Definitions

- **Bare operator.** $w_{k,k}\colon \mathcal H^{\otimes k}\to\mathcal H^{\otimes k}$ (on $\mathrm{Sym}^k$, level-matched), $\mathcal W_{2k}(A,B) =: \langle A, w_{k,k} B\rangle_k$, with $\mathcal W_{2k}$ the reduced even vertex introduced at order $g_s^{2k-2}$ by the completed square `eq:all:square`. Instances: $w_{2,2} = p_{2,2}$ (reduced quartic $=$ elementary quartic), $w_{3,3} = p_{3,3} - \tfrac65\, p_{2,3}\, p_{2,2}^{-1}\, p_{3,2}$ (derivation notes, `sft_hubbard_stratonovich.tex:516`).
- **Dressed operators.** (i) $k^{(2k-1)}_{k,k;\Psi} = w_{k,k} - \frac{g_s}{2k+1}\mathcal W_{2k+1}(\cdot,\cdot,\Psi)$, `eq:all:dressed-odd`: the full quadratic form of $\Sigma_k$ in the action at fixed other fields. (ii) $k^{(2K-1)}_{k,k;\Psi}$, `eq:all:nested`: the quadratic form of $\Sigma_k$ once $\Sigma_{k+1},\dots,\Sigma_K$ are eliminated.
- **"Receives a contribution."** A term of the quadratic form of $\Sigma_k$ at order above $g_s^{2k-2}$ that is *independent of $\Psi$* (it would change $w_{k,k}$, its kernel and the quotient $\mathcal H^{\otimes k}/\ker w_{k,k}$). A term carrying $\Psi$ is a dressing by the background, not a contribution to the bare operator.
- Two ways the bare operator could depend on the order: (a) a $\Psi$-independent term quadratic in $\Sigma_k$ appears at a higher order of the action, or is generated by eliminating another auxiliary field; (b) $\mathcal W_{2k}$ itself is redefined when the truncation is raised. N1–N2 address (a), N3 addresses (b).

## Step 2 – All-orders proof

**Setup.** A1, A2. Write $a_k := g_s^{2k-2}/(2k)!$, $b_k := g_s^{2k-1}/(2k+1)!$, $\Sigma_1 := \Psi$. The action `eq:all:action` is
$$
\tilde S = S_{\le 3}(\Psi) + \sum_{k\ge2}\Big[-a_k\langle\Sigma_k,w_{k,k}\Sigma_k\rangle_k + 2a_k\langle\Sigma_k,w_{k,k}(\Psi\otimes\Sigma_{k-1})\rangle_k + b_k\,\mathcal W_{2k+1}(\Sigma_k,\Sigma_k,\Psi)\Big]. \tag{2.1}
$$

**N1 (list of the terms containing $\Sigma_k$).** $\Sigma_k$ appears in exactly four terms of (2.1): (i) its kinetic term, order $g_s^{2k-2}$, quadratic in $\Sigma_k$, $\Psi$-free; (ii) its own source, order $g_s^{2k-2}$, linear in $\Sigma_k$, linear in $\Psi$; (iii) its odd coupling, order $g_s^{2k-1}$, quadratic in $\Sigma_k$, linear in $\Psi$; (iv) the source of $\Sigma_{k+1}$, order $g_s^{2k}$, linear in $\Sigma_k$, linear in $\Psi$. No other term contains $\Sigma_k$, because every term of (2.1) involves at most two consecutive levels. Every term other than the kinetic terms and $S_{\le3}$ is linear in $\Psi$ (the quartic source $\langle\Sigma_2,p_{2,2}\Psi^2\rangle$ quadratic). Hence the only $\Psi$-independent term quadratic in $\Sigma_k$ is (i), at the single order $g_s^{2k-2}$. $\square$

**N2 (elimination of the other fields).** Write the auxiliary sector as $-\tfrac12\boldsymbol\Sigma^{\rm T}H(\Psi)\boldsymbol\Sigma + J(\Psi)^{\rm T}\boldsymbol\Sigma$, with $H(\Psi)$ the block-tridiagonal Hessian ($H_{kk} = 2a_k\,k^{(2k-1)}_{k,k;\Psi}$, $H_{k+1,k} = -2a_{k+1}\,w_{k+1,k+1}(\Psi\otimes\cdot)$) and $J$ the quartic source. By N1, $H(\Psi) = H(0) + O(\Psi)$ with
$$
H(0) = \operatorname{diag}\big(2a_2\,w_{2,2},\;2a_3\,w_{3,3},\;\dots\big). \tag{2.2}
$$
Eliminating a set $E$ of auxiliary fields (by their equations, i.e. a Gaussian integral at tree level) replaces the block $H_{RR}$ of the remaining fields $R$ by the Schur complement $H_{RR} - H_{RE}H_{EE}^{-1}H_{ER}$. At $\Psi = 0$, $H_{RE}(0) = 0$ by (2.2), so the Schur complement at $\Psi=0$ is $H_{RR}(0)$, block-diagonal with blocks $2a_k w_{k,k}$. Since $H_{EE}(\Psi)^{-1}$ is analytic at $\Psi=0$ (the bare operators are invertible on their quotients, and the kernel condition `eq:hs8:kernel-condition` holds; R70: the nested kernels reduce at $g=0$ to those of the bare operators), the correction $H_{RE}H_{EE}^{-1}H_{ER}$ is $O(\Psi^2)$. This holds for any $E$, in particular for the top-down elimination $E = \{\Sigma_{k+1},\dots,\Sigma_K\}$ that defines the nested operator $k^{(2K-1)}_{k,k;\Psi}$, and for $K=\infty$ coefficient by coefficient in $g_s$. $\square$

**Dependence on $g_s\Psi$ only.** Under $\tilde\Sigma_k = g_s^{k-1}\Sigma_k$ (`eq:all:rescaled`), every kinetic term is $g_s$-free and every other term carries one $g_s$ and one $\Psi$; the effective form of $\tilde\Sigma_k$ is therefore a function of $g_s\Psi$, and its $g_s$-independent part equals its $\Psi$-independent part, $w_{k,k}/(2k)!$. This is the paper's own statement at `02-6-octic.tex` around `eq:hs8:scaling` ("the operator introduced with the square at order $g_s^{2k-2}$ is the value at $g_s = 0$ of the dressed operator of $\Sigma_k$ at every later order").

**N3 (the bare operator is not redefined at higher orders).** The construction fixes $\mathcal W_n$ at order $g_s^{n-2}$ by the deficit rule `eq:hs7:deficit`, $\mathcal W_n(\Psi^n) = V_n(\Psi^n) - n!\,[g_s^{n-2}]\,\tilde S_{\le n-1}|_{\rm sol}$, which takes all lower $\mathcal W_m$ ($m<n$) as given and determines only the diagonal of $\mathcal W_n$. No later step modifies $\mathcal W_{2k}$: the order-$g_s^m$ mismatch for $m > 2k-2$ is always absorbed by the new vertex $\mathcal W_{m+2}$. Hence $w_{k,k}$ depends on $V_4,\dots,V_{2k}$ and on the representative choices made up to order $g_s^{2k-2}$, never on $K$ or on the order of truncation.
Two choices enter $w_{k,k}$, both made once, at or below order $g_s^{2k-2}$: (a) the off-diagonal (Veronese-null) part of $\mathcal W_{2k}$ – the diagram representative of `eq:all:square` is one choice (septic section, *Choice of the partition*; R25/R26 "kernels unique only up to Veronese-diagonal-null forms"); (b) in the multiplier branch where $V_5$ does not vanish on $\ker V_4$, the complement $C$ of $\ker V_4$, through $\mathcal W_6^{(C)} = V_6 - \tfrac65\mathcal C^{(C)}$ (R75), hence $w_{3,3}^{(C)}$. Neither is an order dependence; both are conventions of the construction. $\square$

**Commentary.**
- The "running" reading of `02-5-septic.tex` lines 315–329 (the chain term of $\mathcal W_7$ is the first Taylor coefficient of $p_{3,3} - \tfrac65 p_{2,3}(k^{(3)}_{2,2;\Psi})^{-1}p_{3,2}$) is a $\Psi$-dependent resummation; its value at $g_s\Psi = 0$ is $w_{3,3}$, so it does not contradict N1–N2.
- The exchange of $\Sigma_{k+1}$ produces a term quadratic in $\Sigma_k$ at order $g_s^{2k}$, but it carries $\Psi^2$ (two sources (iv)); this is the case the brief asked about, and it is a dressing.
- Eliminating a *lower* field $\Sigma_{k-1}$ produces a term quadratic in $\Sigma_k$ from two copies of (ii), again with $\Psi^2$.

**Result.**
$$
\boxed{\;\big[\text{quadratic form of }\Sigma_k\big]_{\Psi=0} = a_k\,w_{k,k}\ \text{at every order, every }K\ge k,\text{ after any elimination}\;}
$$
so the bare operator $w_{k,k}$ needs no order label. **N4.** The order label carries information on the *dressed* operators only: $k^{(2k-1)}_{k,k;\Psi} = w_{k,k} - \tfrac{g_s}{2k+1}\mathcal W_{2k+1}(\cdot,\cdot,\Psi)$ is the quadratic form of $\Sigma_k$ in the action with the other fields kept; the nested $k^{(2K-1)}_{k,k;\Psi}$ adds the exchange of $\Sigma_{k+1},\dots,\Sigma_K$ and depends on $K$ (its $g_s^m$ coefficient stabilises for $K \ge k + \lfloor m/2\rfloor$, `03-all-orders.tex`). At $K = \infty$ the nested operator is a single formal series and needs no label either.

## Step 4 – Verdict and notation

**Verdict: yes.** $w_{k,k}$ is well defined without an order label. It is the $\Psi$-independent (equivalently, in the rescaled fields, $g_s$-independent) part of the quadratic form of $\Sigma_k$, fixed once at order $g_s^{2k-2}$ by $\mathcal W_{2k}$, and unchanged at every higher order, for every truncation $K$, and after the elimination of any other auxiliary field: every later term quadratic in $\Sigma_k$ (odd coupling at $g_s^{2k-1}$, exchange of $\Sigma_{k+1}$ at $g_s^{2k}$, exchanges further up) carries $\Psi$. Its kernel, hence the quotient $\mathcal H^{\otimes k}/\ker w_{k,k}$, is truncation-independent (review comment at line 91). The only freedom in $w_{k,k}$ is a convention made at its own order: the representative of $\mathcal W_{2k}$ off the diagonal (and, in the multiplier branch of R75, the complement of $\ker V_4$).

**Answer to the review comment at line 152.** The order label is needed on the dressed operator, not the bare one: after integrating out $\Sigma_{k+1},\dots,\Sigma_K$ the operator of $\Sigma_k$ receives the exchange terms (`eq:all:nested`), which depend on $K$ (`N4-K-dependence`). The operator with only the two contributions $w_{k,k}$ and $\mathcal W_{2k+1}$ is the diagonal block $\mathcal K_{k,k;\Psi}$ of `eq:all:blocks` up to the factor $g_s^{2k-2}/(2k)!$.

**Recommended notation (one line).** Bare: $w_{k,k}$, defined by $\mathcal W_{2k}(A,B) = \langle A, w_{k,k}(B)\rangle_k$ with $w_{2,2} = p_{2,2}$, replacing $k^{(2k-2)}_{k,k}$ everywhere; dressed: $k_{k,k;\Psi}$ without label for the fully dressed operator at $K = \infty$ (Harold's $k_{2,2;\Psi}$ notation of the derivation notes, defined coefficient by coefficient), and a label $k^{(n)}_{k,k;\Psi}$ only where a truncated operator is meant (sections 2.3–2.6 and the finite-$K$ statements of section 3); the one-step operator $w_{k,k} - \tfrac{g_s}{2k+1}\mathcal W_{2k+1}(\cdot,\cdot,\Psi)$ needs no symbol of its own, being the diagonal block of the tridiagonal form.
