## Block A — the map $\Phi_n$: definition, domain, ghost number, level matching, symmetry

\subsection*{Setup}
A1–A3. $U_f$ denotes the operator implementing the local coordinate change, $f\circ A(0):=U_fA(0)U_f^{-1}$ inserted at $f(0)$; for a primary of weight $(h,\bar h)$, $f\circ A(0)=f'(0)^h\bar f'(0)^{\bar h}A(f(0))$. $I(w)=1/w$. $\langle\!\langle\mathcal V'_{n+1}|$ is the surface state of the rigid sphere, `bos:eq:state-surface` with no $B$-insertions (no moduli).

\subsection*{Derivation}

$$\begin{aligned}
\langle\!\langle\mathcal V'_{n+1}|A_1\otimes\dots\otimes A_n\otimes B\rangle\!\rangle
&=\Big\langle\prod_{i=1}^n f_i\circ A_i(0)\;\; I\circ B(0)\Big\rangle_{u\text{-plane}} \tag{A.1}\\
&=\langle\mathrm{bpz}(B)|\;\prod_{i=1}^n f_i\circ A_i(0)\;|0\rangle
=:\langle B,\Phi_n(A_1,\dots,A_n)\rangle, \tag{A.2}\\
\Phi_n(A_1,\dots,A_n)&=\mathcal R\Big[\prod_{i=1}^n f_i\circ A_i(0)\Big]|0\rangle\in\mathcal H. \tag{A.3}
\end{aligned}$$

Level expansion (repeated OPE). Insert the resolution of the identity on the circle $|u|=r$, $\max_i\sup|f_i(D)|<r<1$:

$$\Phi_n=\sum_{a}|\phi_a\rangle\,\langle\phi^a|\mathcal R\Big[\prod_if_i\circ A_i(0)\Big]|0\rangle,\qquad
\langle\phi^a|\dots|0\rangle=\text{a finite correlator for each } a. \tag{A.4}$$

For finite-level $B$, $\langle B,\Phi_n\rangle$ picks finitely many $a$ (those with the level of $B$): (A.2) is a finite sum, exact. The infinite sum over $a$ arises when the special puncture is sewn to another surface state with plumbing $w_0w_0'=q$: the sewn correlator is $\sum_a\langle\dots|\phi_a\rangle q^{L_0}\bar q^{\bar L_0}\langle\phi^a|\dots\rangle$, which converges iff the two sides' disc images are separated by an annulus, i.e. $|q|<1$ on the unit-disc vertex condition of A3 — the standard sewing statement (`bos:eq:fact-plumbing-sep`, `bos:eq:fact-amp-sep`). On the reversed range $|q|>1$ the special disc must be extended to radius $e^{\sigma}$ while staying disjoint from the $f_i(D)$; this is R30 §5.4's coordinate-extension precondition, quantified in 032 (P): $\sigma\le\sigma_{\max}=\ln r_{\max}$, $r_{\max}=\frac{2\beta'(\gamma'-1)}{\gamma'+1}$ at $\alpha'=0$.

Ghost number. $|0\rangle$ has $N_{\rm gh}=0$ and $f_i\circ A_i(0)$ carries $N_{\rm gh}(A_i)$ ($U_{f_i}$ is built from $L_n$, ghost number 0), so

$$N_{\rm gh}\big(\Phi_n(A_1,\dots,A_n)\big)=\sum_iN_{\rm gh}(A_i)\;\;(=2n\text{ on }\mathcal H_2^{\otimes n}),\qquad N_{\rm gh}(B)=6-2n. \tag{A.5}$$

Rotations and $L_0^-$. $e^{i\theta L_0^-}$ implements $R_\theta(u)=e^{i\theta}u$ in the $u$-frame, $e^{i\theta L_0^-}\,(f\circ A)(0)\,e^{-i\theta L_0^-}=(R_\theta\circ f)\circ A(0)$ and $e^{i\theta L_0^-}|0\rangle=|0\rangle$, hence

$$e^{i\theta L_0^-}\Phi_n(\{f_i\})=\Phi_n(\{R_\theta\circ f_i\}),\qquad
P_m\Phi_n=\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{d\theta}{2\pi}e^{-im\theta}\,\Phi_n(\{R_\theta\circ f_i\}) \tag{A.6}$$

for the projector $P_m$ on $L_0^-=m$. $\Phi_n$ is an $L_0^-$ eigenstate iff $\Phi_n(\{R_\theta\circ f_i\})=e^{im\theta}\Phi_n(\{f_i\})$ for all $\theta$; a rotation moves the punctures to $e^{i\theta}z_i$, so for $n\ge2$ this fails at generic positions (C2.3 exhibits the $m=\pm2$ components at $n=2$ for every momentum assignment, and the $m=\pm1$ ones at $k_1\ne k_2$). The $L_0^-=0$ part of the image is the rotation average $P_0\Phi_n$, $P_0=\delta(L_0^-)$; $b_0^-$-closure is a separate condition, supplied in a sewing by the propagator's $b_0^-$.

$b_0^-$. In the $u$-frame $b_0^-=\oint\frac{du}{2\pi i}u\,b(u)-\oint\frac{d\bar u}{2\pi i}\bar u\,\bar b(\bar u)$ on a contour enclosing all $z_i$; deform to the punctures. On the $i$-th leg, with $u=f_i(w)$, $b(u)=(dw/du)^2b(w)$, $du=f_i'(w)dw$:

$$\oint\frac{du}{2\pi i}u\,b(u)\;\to\;\oint\frac{dw}{2\pi i}\frac{f_i(w)}{f_i'(w)}b(w)
=\frac{z_i}{\rho_i}\,b_{-1}^{(i)}+\Big(1-\frac{2a_iz_i}{\rho_i^2}\Big)b_0^{(i)}+\sum_{n\ge1}v^{(i)}_n b_n^{(i)},\qquad \frac{f_i(w)}{f_i'(w)}=\frac{z_i}{\rho_i}+\Big(1-\frac{2a_iz_i}{\rho_i^2}\Big)w+O(w^2),\quad a_i:=\tfrac12f_i''(0). \tag{A.7}$$

So $b_0^-\Phi_n=\sum_i\Phi_n(\dots,(\tfrac{z_i}{\rho_i}b_{-1}-\tfrac{\bar z_i}{\bar\rho_i}\bar b_{-1}+(1-\tfrac{2a_iz_i}{\rho_i^2})b_0-(1-\tfrac{2\bar a_i\bar z_i}{\bar\rho_i^2})\bar b_0+\dots)A_i,\dots)$; on $\mathcal H_0$ inputs the $b_{-1},\bar b_{-1}$ terms survive at generic $z_i$ (e.g. $A_i=c_1\bar c_1e^{ikX}$: $b_{-1}c_1\bar c_1|k\rangle=\bar c_1|k\rangle\ne0$; the sum over $i$ of the transported states does not cancel for generic $z_i$), and the $b_0,\bar b_0$ terms do not combine to $b_0^-$ unless $a_i=0$ or the two coefficients coincide — both failures go the same way. Hence $\Phi_n(\mathrm{Sym}^n\mathcal H_0)\not\subset\ker b_0^-$ either.

Symmetry. $\Phi_n(A_{\pi(1)},\dots,A_{\pi(n)})=\pm\Phi_n(A_1,\dots,A_n)$ for a permutation $\pi$ of the ordinary punctures holds exactly for one rigid surface iff a conformal automorphism $g$ of the sphere satisfies $g\circ f_i=f_{\pi(i)}$ and $g\circ f_0=f_0$, i.e. $g$ fixes $u=\infty$ and the special coordinate $w_0=1/u$ (A3). Dropping the second condition, $g\circ f_0=f_0\circ R_{-\theta}$ for $g(u)=e^{i\theta}u$; for $g(u)=e^{i\theta}(u-c)+c$, $c\ne0$, $g\circ f_0=f_0\circ h$ with $h(w_0)=e^{-i\theta}w_0/(1+c(1-e^{i\theta})e^{-i\theta}w_0)$ (not a rotation) and the output is acted on by $e^{cL_{-1}+\bar c\bar L_{-1}}e^{i\theta L_0^-}e^{-cL_{-1}-\bar c\bar L_{-1}}$. For $c=0$, by (A.6), $\Phi_n(A_{\pi(1)},\dots)=\pm\Phi_n(\{g\circ f_i\})=\pm e^{i\theta L_0^-}\Phi_n(A_1,\dots)$ — the permutation is realised modulo an $L_0^-$ phase on the output, exactly on $P_0\Phi_n$ (centre $c=0$ only; for $c\ne0$ the conjugated rotation above is not a phase and does not commute with $P_0$). Automorphisms fixing $\infty$ are $g(u)=au+b$; the realised permutations form a finite subgroup $G\subset\mathrm{Aff}(\mathbb C)$; $g\mapsto a$ is a homomorphism $G\to\mathbb C^*$ with kernel the translations of finite order, i.e. trivial, so $G$ embeds in $\mathbb C^*$ and is cyclic:

$$G\simeq\mathbb Z_d,\quad d=n\ \text{(transitive case: a regular $n$-gon)},\qquad n=2:\ \Phi_2(A_2,A_1)=(-1)^{|A_1||A_2|}(-1)^{L_0^-}\Phi_2(A_1,A_2)\ (\text{R2's }z_{1,2}=\pm z,\ f_2=-f_1). \tag{A.8}$$

The $n=2$ clause (it needs $f_2=g\circ f_1$ for $g(u)=-u$ for the whole local maps, so also $f_1=g\circ f_2$; not only $\rho_2=-\rho_1$): with that relation, $\Phi_2(A_2,A_1)=\mathcal R[f_1\circ A_2\,f_2\circ A_1]|0\rangle=\mathcal R[(g\circ f_2)\circ A_2\,(g\circ f_1)\circ A_1]|0\rangle=(-1)^{|A_1||A_2|}e^{i\pi L_0^-}\Phi_2(A_1,A_2)$ by (A.6) with $\theta=\pi$ (Koszul sign from reordering the two operators; no further ghost sign, the ghost weights are inside $L_0^-$; the sign convention of $L_0^-$ is immaterial at $\theta=\pi$). Consequence for graded-symmetric inputs ($\Sigma_2$ of any ghost-number split; the Koszul signs cancel): $\Phi_2(\tfrac12(A_1\otimes A_2+(-1)^{|A_1||A_2|}A_2\otimes A_1))=\tfrac12(1+(-1)^{L_0^-})\Phi_2(A_1,A_2)$, so

$$\Phi_2(\mathrm{Sym}^2\mathcal H_0)\subset\ker\big(1-(-1)^{L_0^-}\big):\quad\text{the odd-}L_0^-\text{ components of }\Phi_2(\Sigma_2)\text{ vanish on symmetric }\Sigma_2. \tag{A.8$'$}$$

If the germs are related by $g\circ f_1=f_2\circ R_\psi$ (a rotation of the local coordinate; this needs the whole germs related, not only $\rho_2e^{i\psi}=-\rho_1$), the exchange holds additionally up to phases $e^{i\psi L_0^-}$ on the inputs, invisible on $\mathcal H_0$ inputs. Consistency with (B.8): $\Delta\to-\Delta$ flips exactly the monomials with an odd number of odd-$n$ oscillators, i.e. the odd-$(N-\bar N)$ terms, and the ghost factor $c(z)c(-z)\bar c(\bar z)\bar c(-\bar z)$ is even under $z\to-z$ (two odd factors).

For $n\ge3$, $S_n$ is not cyclic; the symmetric map is $\Phi_n^{\rm sym}:=\Phi_n\circ\mathrm{Sym}=\frac1{n!}\sum_\pi\pm\Phi_n\circ\pi$, which is the map of the symmetrised vertex $\frac{|G|}{n!}\sum_{\pi\in S_n/G}\mathcal V'_{n+1}\circ\pi$ (a sum of $n!/|G|$ labelled copies of the same surface). On a symmetric input $\Sigma_n$ the two maps agree, $\Phi_n(\Sigma_n)=\Phi_n^{\rm sym}(\Sigma_n)$, so no condition on the vertex is needed to apply $\Phi_n$ to the paper's $\Sigma_n$; the condition "vertex symmetric in the ordinary punctures" is what makes $\Phi_n$ itself factor through $\mathrm{Sym}^n$.

Three-circle identity (C1.4). In the HS action $\Sigma_n$ is an independent field and every coupling is cubic: $V_{n+2}(\Sigma_n,\Psi,\Psi)$ and generally R12's $V_{a+b+c}(\Sigma_a,\Sigma_b,\Sigma_c)$, an integral over a region of $\mathcal M_{0,a+b+c}$ of the symmetric vertex with the $a+b+c$ punctures grouped in three clusters. On any surface of that region on which the three clusters are enclosed by three pairwise disjoint discs $D_A,D_B,D_C$ (always possible topologically: join the coordinate discs of one cluster by arcs in the complement of the others and thicken), choose coordinates $u_A,u_B,u_C$ on the discs and apply (A.1)–(A.3) three times, once per disc:

$$\Omega_\Sigma(V_1,\dots,V_p)=\sum_{S_A\sqcup S_B\sqcup S_C=\{1..p\}}\epsilon(S)\,\big\langle\mathcal V_3^{(abc)}\big|\,\Phi_a^{[S_A]}(\Sigma_a)\otimes\Phi_b^{[S_B]}(\Sigma_b)\otimes\Phi_c^{[S_C]}(\Sigma_c)\big\rangle,\quad\Phi_a^{[S]}:=\mathcal R\Big[\prod_{k\in S}B_A[V_k]\prod_{i\in A}f_i\circ A_i(0)\Big]|0\rangle, \tag{A.9}$$

pointwise in the moduli, where $\Omega_\Sigma(V_1..V_p)=\langle\Sigma|B[V_1]\cdots B[V_p]|\otimes_iA_i\rangle$ is the integrand of $V_{a+b+c}$ ($p=2(a+b+c-3)$), $B[V]=B_A[V]+B_B[V]+B_C[V]$ is the sum over punctures of the Schiffer contours $C_i$ just outside the coordinate discs, each lying inside one cluster disc, $\epsilon(S)$ is the Koszul sign of the reordering, and $\mathcal V_3^{(abc)}$ is the surface state of the pair of pants $S^2\setminus(D_A\cup D_B\cup D_C)$ with the three circles as its punctures' coordinate curves — a bare three-punctured sphere, a cubic vertex in $\mathcal H^{\otimes3}$, with ghost numbers $2a-|S_A|$, $2b-|S_B|$, $2c-|S_C|$ on its legs (total 6: the bare pairing with $(2a,2b,2c)$ vanishes for $a+b+c\ge4$, because a sphere surface state without antighost insertions pairs only at total ghost number 6; here $2n-p=6$). Each leg carries its cluster's total momentum with the relative momenta inside as level content (block B applies per circle; the $b$'s carry no momentum). The antighost insertions sit where they always sit, around the ordinary punctures, hence inside the cluster discs, and dress the leg states; those moving the punctures of one cluster relative to each other cannot be moved onto the pants (a contour around one puncture of a cluster with $\ge2$ punctures cannot be deformed to $\partial D_A$: it would need a vector field holomorphic on $D_A$ minus the cluster discs, equal to $v_i$ near $C_i$ and trivial near the others, zero by the identity theorem). In the slice holding one puncture per cluster fixed, each $B[V_k]$ lies in one disc, the sum has one term and the legs have ghost number $(2,2,2)$; other slices give other splits summing to 6. The disjoint discs are taken with the clusters and their coordinate discs in their interiors, so that the level sums converge (margin to $\partial D$; the separating-degeneration statement). What is not canonical is the choice of the three discs across the vertex region: as the moduli vary the clusters move and cross, no single family of circles covers the region, and the gluing of the local choices across the region is the higher-order form of R37's seam; the integrated coupling is therefore not one cubic vertex composed with three leg maps. This is the object assessment §3 (ii) (the cubic tower) has to control, and (A.9) is its building block. Expected, not checked: dressed legs of ghost number 2 match the single-strand auxiliary of (C.5).

## Block B — momenta: plane-wave inputs, general $n$ and the $n=2$ tachyon pair

\subsection*{Setup}
A1, A2, A4. Inputs $A_i=c\bar c\,e^{ik_i\cdot X}$, primaries of weight $(h_i,h_i)$, $h_i=\alpha'k_i^2/4-1$, so $f_i\circ A_i(0)=|\rho_i|^{2h_i}A_i(z_i)$. Split $X=X_-+x-i\tfrac{\alpha'}2p\ln|u|^2+X_+$ with $X_-(u,\bar u)=-i\sqrt{\tfrac{\alpha'}2}\sum_{n\ge1}\tfrac1n(\alpha_{-n}u^n+\bar\alpha_{-n}\bar u^n)$ (creation) and $X_+$ the annihilation part. $P:=\sum_ik_i$, $\Delta:=k_1-k_2$ at $n=2$, $K_n:=\sum_ik_iz_i^n$, $\bar K_n:=\sum_ik_i\bar z_i^n$. Radial order $|z_n|>\dots>|z_1|$ in the derivation; the result is symmetric.

\subsection*{Derivation}

$$e^{ik\cdot X(u,\bar u)}=e^{ik\cdot X_-(u,\bar u)}\,e^{ik\cdot x}\,|u|^{\alpha'k\cdot p}\,e^{ik\cdot X_+(u,\bar u)},\qquad
ik\cdot X_-(u,\bar u)=\sqrt{\tfrac{\alpha'}2}\sum_{n\ge1}\tfrac1n\big(k\cdot\alpha_{-n}u^n+k\cdot\bar\alpha_{-n}\bar u^n\big). \tag{B.1}$$

$$[\,ik_i\cdot X_+(z_i),\,ik_j\cdot X_-(z_j)\,]=-k_i\cdot k_j\,\frac{\alpha'}2\sum_{n\ge1}\frac1n\Big[\Big(\frac{z_j}{z_i}\Big)^n+\Big(\frac{\bar z_j}{\bar z_i}\Big)^n\Big]=\frac{\alpha'k_i\cdot k_j}2\ln\Big|1-\frac{z_j}{z_i}\Big|^2,\qquad |z_i|>|z_j|. \tag{B.2}$$

Moving every $X_+$ to the right and every $e^{ik\cdot x}|u|^{\alpha'k\cdot p}$ onto $|0\rangle$ (which gives $|z_i|^{\alpha'k_i\cdot(k_1+\dots+k_{i-1})}$ and the momentum shift), and combining $|z_i|^{\alpha'k_ik_j}|1-z_j/z_i|^{\alpha'k_ik_j}=|z_{ij}|^{\alpha'k_i\cdot k_j}$:

$$\boxed{\;\Phi_n\big(\otimes_ic\bar ce^{ik_iX}\big)=\prod_i|\rho_i|^{2h_i}\prod_{i<j}|z_{ij}|^{\alpha'k_i\cdot k_j}\;\mathcal R\Big[\prod_ic(z_i)\bar c(\bar z_i)\Big]\;\exp\Big(\sqrt{\tfrac{\alpha'}2}\sum_{n\ge1}\tfrac1n\big(K_n\cdot\alpha_{-n}+\bar K_n\cdot\bar\alpha_{-n}\big)\Big)|P\rangle\;} \tag{B.3}$$

Level content. The coefficient of the monomial $\prod_a\alpha_{-n_a}^{\mu_a}\prod_b\bar\alpha_{-\bar n_b}^{\nu_b}$ in (B.3) is a polynomial in the components of $K_{n_a},\bar K_{\bar n_b}$, hence in $k_i$ and $z_i,\bar z_i$. The vectors $K_0=P,K_1,\dots,K_{n-1}$ determine the $k_i$ through the Vandermonde matrix $(z_i^m)_{m=0..n-1,\,i=1..n}$, invertible for distinct $z_i$:

$$k_i=\sum_{m=0}^{n-1}\big(V^{-1}\big)_{im}K_m,\qquad V_{mi}=z_i^m. \tag{B.4}$$

So the $n-1$ relative momenta are read off from the levels $1,\dots,n-1$ (linear terms), the total momentum from the zero mode. Every level is populated for generic $z_i$ (if $K_n=0$ for all $n\ge1$, then by (B.4) with the rows $1..n$ all $k_i=0$). The $L_0^+$ eigenvalue of the level-$(N,\bar N)$ term is $\tfrac{\alpha'P^2}2+N+\bar N-2$ (ghosts included below), so $\Phi_n$ is a superposition over $L_0^+$ eigenvalues: off shell, not an eigenstate.

$n=2$, $z_{1,2}=\pm z$, $|\rho_1|=|\rho_2|=\rho$: $K_{2m+1}=z^{2m+1}\Delta$, $K_{2m}=z^{2m}P$, so the matter factor through holomorphic level 2 is

$$1+\sqrt{\tfrac{\alpha'}2}\,z\,\Delta\cdot\alpha_{-1}+\frac{\alpha'}4z^2(\Delta\cdot\alpha_{-1})^2+\sqrt{\tfrac{\alpha'}2}\,\frac{z^2}2\,P\cdot\alpha_{-2}+O(z^3)\quad(\times\ \text{the same with }\bar z,\bar\alpha). \tag{B.5}$$

Ghosts (mode algebra, $c_n|0\rangle=0$ for $n\ge2$):

$$c(z_1)c(z_2)|0\rangle=(z_1-z_2)\Big[c_0c_1+(z_1+z_2)\,c_{-1}c_1+z_1z_2\,c_{-1}c_0+(z_1^2+z_1z_2+z_2^2)\,c_{-2}c_1+O(z^3)\Big]|0\rangle, \tag{B.6}$$

$$c(z)c(-z)|0\rangle=2z\Big[c_0c_1+z^2\big(c_{-2}c_1-c_{-1}c_0\big)+O(z^4)\Big]|0\rangle. \tag{B.7}$$

Assembling, with $c\bar c(z_1)\,c\bar c(z_2)=-c(z_1)c(z_2)\bar c(\bar z_1)\bar c(\bar z_2)$:

$$\Phi_2=-\rho^{2(h_1+h_2)}|2z|^{\alpha'k_1\cdot k_2}\,4|z|^2\Big[c_0c_1+z^2(c_{-2}c_1-c_{-1}c_0)+\dots\Big]\Big[\bar c_0\bar c_1+\bar z^2(\bar c_{-2}\bar c_1-\bar c_{-1}\bar c_0)+\dots\Big]\Big[1+\sqrt{\tfrac{\alpha'}2}z\Delta\cdot\alpha_{-1}+\dots\Big]\Big[1+\sqrt{\tfrac{\alpha'}2}\bar z\Delta\cdot\bar\alpha_{-1}+\dots\Big]|P\rangle. \tag{B.8}$$

Level matching at $n=2$ (C2.3). The term $\sqrt{\alpha'/2}\,z\,\Delta\cdot\alpha_{-1}\,c_0c_1\bar c_0\bar c_1|P\rangle$ has $L_0^-=+1$, its conjugate $-1$; it vanishes for $\Delta=0$ and cancels on symmetric inputs $(k_1,k_2)+(k_2,k_1)$ (A.8$'$). The $(2,0)$ terms $z^2(c_{-2}c_1-c_{-1}c_0)\bar c_0\bar c_1|P\rangle$ (from (B.7), momentum-independent) and $\tfrac{z^2}2\sqrt{\alpha'/2}\,P\cdot\alpha_{-2}\,c_0c_1\bar c_0\bar c_1|P\rangle$ (from (B.5)) have $L_0^-=+2$ for every $(k_1,k_2)$ with $z\ne0$: level matching fails because the two-puncture configuration is not rotation invariant (A.6), whatever the momenta; the relative momentum only decides which levels carry it (odd levels need $\Delta\ne0$, even levels are always populated). Second route for the matter coefficients: the Ward identity $\langle\partial X^\mu(u)\prod_ie^{ik_iX}(z_i)\rangle=-i\tfrac{\alpha'}2\sum_i\tfrac{k_i^\mu}{u-z_i}\langle\prod_ie^{ik_iX}\rangle$ with $\alpha_m=i\sqrt{2/\alpha'}\oint\frac{du}{2\pi i}u^m\partial X$ gives $\langle P|\alpha^\mu_1|\Phi_2\rangle/\langle P|\Phi_2\rangle_{\rm level\,0}=\sqrt{\alpha'/2}\,K_1^\mu=\sqrt{\alpha'/2}\,z\Delta^\mu$ (`codes/verify-circle-map.py`, `C2.3-L10`, `C4.2-matter-L1`; at $k_1=k_2$ the same residues give $\tfrac{s}{2}K_2=s\,z^2k$ for $\alpha_{-2}$ and zero for $\alpha_{-1}$, `C2.3-sym-L20`). Second route for the ghost $(2,0)$ coefficient: $\langle0|c_{-1}c_0\,b_2\,c(z_1)c(z_2)|0\rangle=C_{-2,1}$ (only $c_{-2}c_1|0\rangle$ survives $b_2$ and the bra), evaluated as nested contour integrals of the $bc$ five-point function $\langle b(v)\prod_{i=1}^4c(u_i)\rangle=\sum_i\tfrac{(-1)^{i-1}}{v-u_i}\prod_{j<k;\,j,k\ne i}(u_j-u_k)$ (simple poles from $b(v)c(u)\sim1/(v-u)$, $O(v^{-4})$ at infinity, unique), giving $z_1^3-z_2^3$, $=2z^3$ at $\pm z$ (`C4.2-ghost-b-insertion`). The ghost sector at $\pm z$ has no odd levels ($c_{-1}c_1$ coefficient $z_1^2-z_2^2=0$, (B.7)) but is not level-matched: $(2,0)$ and $(0,2)$ are present. Rotation route (A.6): under $z\to e^{i\theta}z$ the level-$(N,\bar N)$ term picks $e^{i(N-\bar N)\theta}$ (`C2.1-rotation`); the $m=\pm1$ Fourier components are the $\Delta$ terms, the $m=\pm2$ ones the $P\cdot\alpha_{-2}$, $(\Delta\cdot\alpha_{-1})^2$ and ghost terms. On symmetric $\Sigma_2$ (A.8$'$) only even $m$ survive, which halves the level content the paper's $\Sigma_2$ sees; the $\theta$-average $P_0\Phi_2(\Sigma_2)$, the part a level-matched propagator sees, still determines $\Delta$ through the $(1,1)$ term $\tfrac{\alpha'}2|z|^2(\Delta\cdot\alpha_{-1})(\Delta\cdot\bar\alpha_{-1})$ — quadratically, i.e. up to the sign of $\Delta$, which the symmetrisation removes anyway.

Limits. $n=1$: (B.3) with one puncture at $z_1=0$, $\rho_1=1$ is $c_1\bar c_1|k_1\rangle$: $\Phi_1=\mathrm{id}$ (and $U_{f_1}$ in general). Separation: at fixed circle $|u|=1$ the level-$(N,\bar N)$ coefficient scales as $|z|^{N+\bar N}$; for $|z|\to0$ (punctures close together, circle far) the lowest level dominates and the pair is seen as the single operator $c\bar ce^{iPX}$ dressed by the prefactor — the OPE limit; the relative momentum first appears at order $|z|$. For the actual $\mathcal V_3'$, $|z|$ is fixed by $\beta$ (R2) and is of order one: no suppression, the full level sum is needed.

(B.1): normal-ordered form; the zero-mode factor is $|u|^{\alpha'k\cdot p}$ because $ik\cdot(-i\tfrac{\alpha'}2p\ln|u|^2)=\tfrac{\alpha'}2k\cdot p\ln|u|^2$. \subsection*{Result}
The image of $n$ plane waves is one string state of momentum $P=\sum_ik_i$ with every level populated, coefficients polynomial in $(k_i,z_i,\bar z_i)$, the $n-1$ relative momenta recoverable from the linear level-$1..n-1$ coefficients by the Vandermonde inverse (B.4): nothing is lost, the relative momenta become the level content of the composite. Reading for the puzzle (decision 2026-10-01, reading (iii) rejected as a definition, allowed for interpretation): the circle state is a single string state whose "internal" labels — the levels — are functions of the relative momenta, continuous and non-compact; this is why the merged state is not a field in $\mathcal H$ with its own discrete level structure: each $\Sigma_n$ configuration with different relative momenta gives a different, non-$L_0$-eigen, level-mixing state, and the sum over $\Sigma_n$'s relative momenta is a sum over such states. The picture is a change of basis in the sewn correlator, not a reduction of the auxiliary field's content.

## Block C — relation to the paper's $\Sigma_2$, the kernel, and the single-strand route

\subsection*{Setup}
A1–A4. $K(f_1,f_2):=\{T=\sum_aA^a\otimes B^a\in\mathcal H^{\otimes2}\text{ finite}:\ \Phi_2(T)=0\}$. $C_{(m)}$ denotes a mode of a field $C(u)$ (holomorphic: $C_{(m)}B:=\oint_0\frac{dx}{2\pi i}x^{m+h_C-1}C(x)B(0)|0\rangle$; antiholomorphic likewise); $\mathcal A$ is the algebra generated by all modes of $\partial X^\mu,\bar\partial X^\mu,b,c,\bar b,\bar c$ (zero modes included). $\mathcal K$, $\mathcal K^{-1}$: R30's reversed-range kinetic operator and propagator of the single-strand route; $J$: the $\omega_1$-dual of a BPZ-bra.

\subsection*{Derivation}

**C.1 Injectivity of $\Phi_2$ (C5.1).** Five steps.

(i) *Coordinates and translation.* With $\tilde f_i(w):=f_i(w)-z_i$, $f_i\circ A(0)=e^{z_iL_{-1}+\bar z_i\bar L_{-1}}\,(U_{\tilde f_i}A)(0)\,e^{-z_iL_{-1}-\bar z_i\bar L_{-1}}$ and $U_{\tilde f_i}$ is invertible on finite-level states (lower-triangular in level, leading coefficient $\rho_i^{L_0}\bar\rho_i^{\bar L_0}$). Hence

$$K(f_1,f_2)=\big(U_{\tilde f_1}^{-1}\otimes U_{\tilde f_2}^{-1}\big)K_0(z_{12}),\qquad K_0(z):=\Big\{T:\ \sum_aA^a(z)B^a(0)|0\rangle=0\Big\},\ z\ne0, \tag{C.1}$$

using $\sum_aA^a(z_1)B^a(z_2)|0\rangle=e^{z_2L_{-1}+\bar z_2\bar L_{-1}}\sum_aA^a(z_{12})B^a(0)|0\rangle$ and $e^{z_2L_{-1}+\dots}$ invertible.

(ii) *Mode invariance.* Let $T\in K_0(z)$, $C$ any field, $\phi$ any finite-level state. $F(x):=\langle\mathrm{bpz}(\phi)|\,\mathcal R[C(x)\sum_aA^a(z)B^a(0)]\,|0\rangle$ is real-analytic on $\mathbb C\setminus\{0,z\}$ (free-field correlator: products of $|x-z_i|^{\alpha'k\cdot k_i}$ and Laurent polynomials, Wick's theorem). For $|x|>|z|$, $F(x)=\langle\mathrm{bpz}(\phi)|C(x)\,\Phi_2(T)=0$. Hence $F\equiv0$; on $0<|x|<|z|$, $F(x)=\langle\mathrm{bpz}(\phi)|\sum_aA^a(z)C(x)B^a(0)|0\rangle$, and contour integration against $x^{m+h_C-1}$ around $0$ (resp. $(x-z)^{m+h_C-1}$ around $z$ on $0<|x-z|<|z|$) gives

$$(1\otimes C_{(m)})\,T\in K_0(z),\qquad (C_{(m)}\otimes1)\,T\in K_0(z)\qquad\text{for every mode of every field.} \tag{C.2}$$

(iii) *Grading.* $L_0,\bar L_0,p^\mu$ are modes ($T_{(0)}$, $\bar T_{(0)}$, $(\partial X)_{(0)}$), diagonalisable on the finite-dimensional span of the components of $T$; by (C.2) $K_0(z)$ is spanned by tensors bihomogeneous in level and momentum on both legs. WLOG $T=\sum_{a=1}^rA^a\otimes B^a$ with $\{B^a\}$ linearly independent, all $B^a$ at level $(N_2,\bar N_2)$ and momentum $k_2$, all $A^a$ at $(N_1,\bar N_1)$, $k_1$.

(iv) *Reduction to the vacuum.* The Fock space at fixed momentum is an irreducible $\mathcal A$-module (Heisenberg$\,\otimes\,$Clifford Fock module; standard), of countable dimension, so Jacobson density applies: there is $M\in\mathcal A$ with $MB^b=\delta^b_1|0;k_2\rangle$. By (C.2), $(1\otimes M)T=A^1\otimes|0;k_2\rangle\in K_0(z)$; repeating on the left leg with $M'A^1=|0;k_1\rangle$:

$$e^{ik_1X}(z)\,e^{ik_2X}(0)\,|0\rangle=0. \tag{C.3}$$

(v) *Contradiction.* By (B.3) at $n=2$ (matter part, no ghosts), the left side of (C.3) is $|z|^{\alpha'k_1\cdot k_2}\exp(\dots)|k_1+k_2\rangle$, whose level-$(0,0)$ component $|z|^{\alpha'k_1\cdot k_2}|k_1+k_2\rangle$ is non-zero. Hence $K_0(z)=0$ and, by (C.1),

$$\boxed{\;\ker\Phi_2\big|_{\text{finite tensors}}=0\quad\text{for every admissible }(f_1,f_2),\qquad\text{in particular }\ker\Phi_2\cap\mathrm{Sym}^2\mathcal H_0=0.\;} \tag{C.4}$$

**C.2 Descent to the quotient (C5.2).** $\Phi_2$ descends to $\mathrm{Sym}^2\mathcal H_0/\ker V_4$ iff $\ker V_4\subseteq\ker\Phi_2$. By (C.4) this fails as soon as $\ker V_4$ contains a non-zero finite-level element — the only kind PH5's support (R53, level 0, generic momentum) could produce — and holds trivially if $\ker V_4=0$ (PH5's first clause); an infinite-level kernel element is not covered by (C.4). Where $\ker V_4\ne0$ the state $\Phi_2(\Sigma)$ depends on the representative of $[\Sigma]$; the physically relevant object is then the quadratic form of C.4, whose kernel is compared with $\ker V_4$ only through R37's identity.

**C.3 Ghost number and the single-strand dictionary (C5.3).** By (A.5), $N_{\rm gh}(\Phi_2(\Sigma))=4$ while R30's auxiliary has $N_{\rm gh}(\Phi)=2$ (030 l. 1626). In the single-strand action $S\supset\tfrac12\omega_1(\Phi,\mathcal K\Phi)+g\,\mathcal V_3'(\Psi,\Psi,\Phi)$ with $\mathcal V_3'(\Psi,\Psi,\Phi)=\langle\Phi,\Phi_2(\Psi\otimes\Psi)\rangle$ by (A.2), so $\Phi_2(\Psi\otimes\Psi)$ is the *source* of $\Phi$:

$$\mathcal K\Phi=-g\,b_0^-\delta(L_0^-)\,\Phi_2(\Psi\otimes\Psi)\quad\Rightarrow\quad\Phi=-g\,\mathcal K^{-1}b_0^-\delta(L_0^-)\,\Phi_2(\Psi\otimes\Psi),\qquad N_{\rm gh}:\ 4\xrightarrow{b_0^-}3\xrightarrow{\mathcal K^{-1}\ni b_0^+}2, \tag{C.5}$$

where $b_0^-\delta(L_0^-)$ is the projection through which the $\omega_1$-dual $J$ of the BPZ bra $\langle\Phi_2(\Psi\otimes\Psi),\cdot\rangle$ is defined ($\omega_1$ exists on $\mathcal H_0$ only, C1; the $J$ of the earlier draft is this composite).

So the single-strand auxiliary is not $\Phi_2(\Sigma_2)$; it is the propagated source, and only on its equation of motion with $\Sigma_2=\Psi\otimes\Psi$ (the HS equation of motion, R1).

**C.4 Quadratic terms (C5.4).** Integrating $\Phi$ out gives $\tfrac{g^2}2\langle\Phi_2(\Psi\Psi),\mathcal K^{-1}b_0^-\delta(L_0^-)\Phi_2(\Psi\Psi)\rangle$; integrating $\Sigma_2$ out of the HS action gives $V_4(\Psi^4)$ (R1). The two theories coincide at this order iff, on $\mathrm{Sym}^2\mathcal H_0$,

$$V_4(\Sigma,\Sigma')\overset?=\big\langle\Phi_2\Sigma,\ \mathcal K^{-1}b_0^-\delta(L_0^-)\,\Phi_2\Sigma'\big\rangle=\text{two }\mathcal V_3'\text{ sewn at the special puncture with propagator }\mathcal K^{-1}. \tag{C.6}$$

Status of (C.6) by the ledger: twist-only $|q|=1$ propagator — the right side covers $\partial\mathcal V_{0,4}$ only (R2, Verified), so (C.6) fails: $V_4$ is the interior; reversed range with constant $\sigma$ — never covers $\mathcal M'_\times$ once (R37 (i), Numerical: Euclidean area at $\beta'=3/2$, $\alpha'=0$, one grid $h=0.004$; gap below $\sigma^*=0.879$, overlap above $0.434$), (C.6) fails; the $S_3$-seam family $\sigma(\theta)$ — (C.6) holds on shell, i.e. on $\Psi\otimes\Psi$ with four on-shell primaries, checked numerically for tachyons at two momenta (R37 (ii), Conditional(PH10) – Argued), off shell expected to fail on non-primary externals (032 §5, argued, not evaluated; PH11 collar cure). The difference between the two sides at $|q|=1$ is R30's neck family (the interior of $\mathcal V_{0,4}$ minus its boundary), and for constant $\sigma$ a gap or an overlap region of $\mathcal M'_\times$ (R37 (i)); no $\mathcal K$ with constant $\sigma$ closes it in the regime R37 covers.

**C.5 Verdict on the two theories (C5.5).** By C.3 and C.4: the HS theory with $\Sigma_2\in\mathrm{Sym}^2\mathcal H_0/\ker V_4$ and the single-strand theory are different off-shell formulations, not related by $\Phi_2$ as a field redefinition $\Sigma_2\to\Phi$. $\Phi_2$ relates their *sources*, not their fields; the auxiliary spaces differ in ghost number ($4$ vs $2$) and in level matching (block A); their quadratic forms differ for twist-only (R2, Verified) and constant $\sigma$ (R37 (i), Numerical). For the seam family, whether the two define the same on-shell theory is R37's conditional claim and the off-shell difference is conjectured (032 §5), so "different theories" is not established there. The reading map's "different construction" is confirmed at the quadratic level in the sense just stated.

\subsection*{Commentary}
(C.1): $U_{\tilde f}$ is $\exp(\sum_{n\ge1}v_nL_n)\rho^{L_0}$-type, invertible level by level. (C.2): the step "vanishes on an open set ⇒ vanishes identically" needs real-analyticity of the correlator in $x$, which holds for free-field correlators of finite-level states (single-valued because the plane-wave factors come as $|x-z_i|^{2\alpha'k\cdot k_i/2}$ with both chiralities); the mode extraction commutes with the pairing since the contour stays in the region of convergence of the radially ordered product. (iv): Jacobson density for a countable-dimensional irreducible module over $\mathbb C$ (Schur's lemma gives $\mathrm{End}_{\mathcal A}=\mathbb C$); irreducibility of the Fock module: every non-zero vector is lowered to the vacuum by annihilation modes and the vacuum is cyclic. (v): (B.3) with $n=2$ and no ghost factor, level-0 term. (C.5): the ghost-number count uses $\omega_1(A,B)=\langle A|c_0^-|B\rangle$ (C1), whose dual of a BPZ bra is $b_0^-\delta(L_0^-)$ applied to the ket, and the $b_0^+$ of R30's bounded-cylinder propagator; the signs are not needed for the conclusion. (C.6): the sewing identification is (A.2) applied twice, the propagator's $b$-insertions and twist integral supplying the measure on the neck family (030 §5.4, 032 §3).

\subsection*{Result}
$\Phi_2$ is injective on finite-level tensors, at every admissible position and coordinate choice (C.4), hence on the finite-level elements of $\mathrm{Sym}^2\mathcal H_0$; it descends to the paper's quotient iff $\ker V_4\subseteq\ker\Phi_2$, which fails for any finite-level kernel element and holds if $\ker V_4=0$ (PH5). The single-strand auxiliary is $-g\mathcal K^{-1}b_0^-\delta(L_0^-)\Phi_2(\Psi\otimes\Psi)$, of ghost number 2, not $\Phi_2(\Sigma_2)$ (ghost number 4). The quadratic forms $V_4$ and $\Phi_2^*\mathcal K^{-1}$ differ for twist-only (R2, Verified) and for constant $\sigma$ (R37 (i), Numerical at $\beta'=3/2$) and agree on shell only for the seam family, conditionally on PH10 (R37 (ii)): different off-shell formulations, not related by a field redefinition.

**Verdict (three sentences).** The "merge by repeated OPE" picture is exactly right as a statement about correlators: the state on the circle is one string state of ghost number $2n$ and total momentum $P$, in which the relative momenta sit as level content and nothing is lost, it is not level-matched for any momentum assignment because the puncture configuration is not rotation invariant (the relative momenta only decide which levels carry the mismatch), and all of this is established here with no hypothesis. At every $n$ the integrands of the trinion couplings of the HS action factorise, surface by surface, through three circles: a three-punctured-sphere vertex in $\mathcal H^{\otimes3}$ applied to the three clusters' circle states, each dressed with the antighost insertions of the moduli that move its own punctures, so that each leg carries its cluster's total momentum and its relative momenta as level content (A.9); what is not available is a single rigid $\mathcal V_3^{(abc)}$ over the vertex region — the circle choice varies with the moduli, the higher-order form of the seam — so the single-strand *theory* at $n\ge3$ needs the tower of assessment §3 (ii), and at $n=2$ $\Phi_2$ is not a field redefinition $\Sigma_2\to\Phi$ (it sends the source $\Psi\otimes\Psi$ to the source of the single-strand auxiliary, with ghost numbers 4 vs 2), the two quadratic forms differing for twist-only (R2, Verified) and constant $\sigma$ (R37 (i), Numerical at $\beta'=3/2$) and agreeing on shell only for the seam family (R37 (ii), conditionally on PH10). For the paper this favours (b) in its weak form, strengthened by (A.9): the main idea (one circle, one state, momenta as level content) can be stated as an interpretation of the coloured-strand sewing and of the cubic couplings themselves; the construction of a single-strand theory stays in another paper, and the one further record that would change the verdict is an off-shell evaluation of the seam identity (032 (5.1′)) at $n=2$, not anything at higher $n$.
