## §1 Definition and change of variables (C1)

\subsection*{Setup}

A1–A4. $|\alpha,k\rangle$ a basis of $\mathcal H_0$ at momentum $k$ ($\alpha$ the oscillator and ghost content; Grassmann parity $|\alpha|=N_{\rm gh}(\alpha)\bmod2=0$ on $\mathcal H_0$, A3, so all signs below are $+1$ and are kept only to show where a graded version would differ). $\Pi(\pi)$ the $n\times n$ permutation matrix of $\pi\in S_n$, $\mathbf 1=(1,\dots,1)^{\!\top}$, $E$ an $n\times(n-1)$ matrix whose columns are an orthonormal basis of $\mathbf 1^\perp\subset\mathbb R^n$ (each entry multiplies a $d$-vector).

\subsection*{Derivation}

*(1.1) Definition.* The $n$-string auxiliary field is the general (entangled) $n$-string state
$$\Sigma_n=\sum_{\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_n}\int\prod_{i=1}^n\frac{d^dk_i}{(2\pi)^d}\;\sigma_{\alpha_1\dots\alpha_n}(k_1,\dots,k_n)\;|\alpha_1,k_1\rangle\otimes\dots\otimes|\alpha_n,k_n\rangle,\tag{1.1}$$
with commuting coefficients (total ghost number $2n$, even). $\Sigma_n\in\operatorname{Sym}^n\mathcal H_0$ is the statement that the coefficients are symmetric under the **joint** exchange of a label and its momentum,
$$\sigma_{\dots\alpha_i\alpha_{i+1}\dots}(\dots,k_i,k_{i+1},\dots)=(-1)^{|\alpha_i||\alpha_{i+1}|}\,\sigma_{\dots\alpha_{i+1}\alpha_i\dots}(\dots,k_{i+1},k_i,\dots),\tag{1.2}$$
generated by adjacent transpositions. Symmetry "in the internal indices" alone, at fixed momentum arguments, is not a property of $\Sigma_n$: the exchange symmetry of a tensor power acts on the whole strand label $(\alpha_i,k_i)$. At $n=2$ this is the content of Harold's l. 256, and the measure of l. 252 is $\frac{d^dk_1}{(2\pi)^d}\frac{d^dk_2}{(2\pi)^d}$.

*(1.3) Jacobi coordinates.* Total and relative momenta:
$$P=\sum_{i=1}^nk_i,\qquad \ell_i=k_i-\frac Pn\ \ \Bigl(\sum_i\ell_i=0\Bigr),\qquad q_j=\sqrt{\frac{j}{j+1}}\Bigl(k_{j+1}-\frac1j\sum_{i\le j}k_i\Bigr),\ \ j=1,\dots,n-1.\tag{1.3}$$
The $\ell_i$ are the manifestly $S_n$-symmetric relative momenta (one redundant); the $q_j$ are an orthonormal parametrisation of them: writing $\ell=Eq$ (each $\ell_i=\sum_jE_{ij}q_j$) with $E$ the Jacobi matrix (columns $e_j=\sqrt{j/(j+1)}\,(-\tfrac1j,\dots,-\tfrac1j,1,0,\dots,0)$, $j$ entries $-\tfrac1j$), one has $E^{\!\top}E=\mathbb 1_{n-1}$, $E^{\!\top}\mathbf 1=0$, $EE^{\!\top}=\mathbb 1_n-\tfrac1n\mathbf 1\mathbf 1^{\!\top}$. The linear map $k\mapsto(P,q)$ has the matrix $M=\binom{\mathbf 1^{\!\top}}{E^{\!\top}}$ per spacetime component, $MM^{\!\top}=\operatorname{diag}(n,1,\dots,1)$, so $|\det M|=\sqrt n$ and
$$\prod_{i=1}^nd^dk_i=n^{-d/2}\,d^dP\prod_{j=1}^{n-1}d^dq_j,\qquad \sum_{i=1}^nk_i^2=\frac{P^2}{n}+\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}q_j^2.\tag{1.4}$$
At $n=2$: $q_1=(k_2-k_1)/\sqrt2=-\sqrt2\,\ell$ with the ledger's $\ell=\tfrac12(k_1-k_2)$ of 049 A2 ($k_{1,2}=\tfrac P2\pm\ell$), for which $d^dk_1d^dk_2=d^dP\,d^d\ell$ (unit Jacobian) and $k_1^2+k_2^2=\tfrac12P^2+2\ell^2$. The record keeps $\ell$ at $n=2$ (the ledger's variable) and $q$ for general $n$.

*(1.5) Symmetric-group action.* A permutation of the strands, $k\mapsto\Pi(\pi)k$, fixes $P$ and acts on the Jacobi momenta by
$$q\mapsto D(\pi)\,q,\qquad D(\pi)=E^{\!\top}\Pi(\pi)E\in O(n-1),\tag{1.5}$$
the standard ($(n-1)$-dimensional) representation of $S_n$: $D$ is orthogonal because $\Pi$ is and $E$ is an isometry onto the $\Pi$-invariant hyperplane $\mathbf 1^\perp$; $D(\pi\pi')=D(\pi)D(\pi')$ for the same reason ($EE^{\!\top}$ is the projector on $\mathbf 1^\perp$, which commutes with $\Pi$). At $n=3$: $q_1=(k_2-k_1)/\sqrt2$, $q_2=\sqrt{2/3}\,(k_3-\tfrac12(k_1+k_2))$, and
$$D((12))=\begin{pmatrix}-1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad D((23))=\begin{pmatrix}\tfrac12&\tfrac{\sqrt3}2\\[2pt]\tfrac{\sqrt3}2&-\tfrac12\end{pmatrix},\tag{1.6}$$
two reflections of the plane generating the dihedral group of the triangle (det $-1$ each, $D((23))$ computed by hand from $\ell_1=-q_1/\sqrt2-q_2/\sqrt6$, $\ell_2=q_1/\sqrt2-q_2/\sqrt6$, $\ell_3=\sqrt{2/3}\,q_2$; both checked by `C1.2-S3`). In the new variables (1.2) reads
$$\sigma_{\pi\alpha}\bigl(P,D(\pi)q\bigr)=\epsilon(\pi,\alpha)\,\sigma_\alpha(P,q),\qquad\epsilon(\pi,\alpha)=+1\ \text{(all strands even, C1.4)},\tag{1.7}$$
and at $n=2$, where $D((12))=-1$: $\sigma_{\alpha_2\alpha_1}(P,-\ell)=\sigma_{\alpha_1\alpha_2}(P,\ell)$; a component with equal labels is even in $\ell$ (the tachyon pair: 049 (1.1), the reason the level-0 space is $L^2_{\rm even}$).

*(1.8) Level matching.* $\mathcal H_0$ is level-matched strand by strand: $L_0^-|\alpha_i,k_i\rangle=(N_i-\bar N_i)|\alpha_i,k_i\rangle=0$ (no winding: $p_L=p_R=k_i$ on non-compact directions). The condition involves no momentum, so it imposes nothing on $P$ or $q$; the only place the change of variables touches the strand data is the kinetic combination $\sum_iL_0^{+}{}_{(i)}\ni\tfrac{\alpha'}2\sum_ik_i^2=\tfrac{\alpha'}2(P^2/n+|q|^2)$ by (1.4).

*(1.9) Reality.* $\ddagger$ (conv. C3) is antilinear, acts componentwise on $\mathcal H_0^{\otimes n}$ without a reordering sign (028 (C2)) and reverses the momentum of every strand ($\mathrm{bpz}^{-1}\circ\mathrm{hc}$ sends $|k\rangle$ to $|-k\rangle$ up to the basis conjugation, since the BPZ bra of $|k\rangle$ pairs with $|-k\rangle$, A1). In a $\ddagger$-real basis, $\ddagger|\alpha,k\rangle=|\alpha,-k\rangle$ — which exists because $\ddagger^2=1$ (conv. C3) and $\ddagger$ maps the sector $k$ onto the sector $-k$: choose the basis at $-k$ as the image of the basis at $k$ on a half-space of momenta, and a real form of the antilinear involution at $k=0$ — the condition $\Sigma_n^\ddagger=\Sigma_n$ reads
$$\sigma_{\alpha}(k_1,\dots,k_n)^*=\sigma_{\alpha}(-k_1,\dots,-k_n),\quad\text{i.e.}\quad \sigma_\alpha(P,q)^*=\sigma_\alpha(-P,-q),\tag{1.8}$$
for every component (all strands have $N_{\rm gh}=2$, so $\epsilon_\alpha=+1$; C3 extends $\ddagger$ componentwise without a reordering sign, 028 (C2)), since $k\mapsto-k$ is $(P,q)\mapsto(-P,-q)$ by linearity of (1.3); the tachyon pair and Zwiebach (3.6) via conv. C3 ($\phi(p)^*=\phi(-p)$ at $n=1$) are the examples. The condition pairs the fibre at $P$ with the fibre at $-P$: it is compatible with, and does not reduce, the diagonal form of §2.

*(1.10) The quotient at $n=2$.* $\ker V_4:=\{\Sigma\in\operatorname{Sym}^2\mathcal H_0:V_4(\Sigma,\cdot)=0\}$ (R9: the radical of $V_4\vert_{\operatorname{Sym}^2}$, $=\ker m_2$). By the diagonal form (2.2) of §2, $V_4(\Sigma,\Sigma')=\int\frac{d^dP}{(2\pi)^d}\,\sigma(-P,\cdot)\,K_P\,\sigma'(P,\cdot)$, so $\Sigma\in\ker V_4$ iff $K_P\sigma(P,\cdot)=0$ for almost every $P$:
$$\ker V_4=\int^{\oplus}\!d^dP\;\ker K_P,\qquad \operatorname{Sym}^2\mathcal H_0/\ker V_4=\int^{\oplus}\!d^dP\;\bigl(\text{relative-momentum functions at }P\bigr)/\ker K_P,\tag{1.9}$$
a $P$-dependent subspace. On the level-0 block, at real $s=P^2$, $\beta>\sqrt2$, $C\ne0$: $\ker K_P(s)=\{0\}$ on $L^2_{\rm even}(\mathbb R^{26})$ (R53, Conditional(PH25, PH29, PH30) – Proven), so the quotient is trivial there, in the level-0 ($TT,TT$) truncation (in the untruncated $V_4$ the $TT$ block mixes with $TD$, $DD$, R68, and R53 does not exclude a null vector of the full $K_P$ with a non-zero $TT$ component); at complex $s$ (PH18) and on the blocks of level $>0$ (PH19; PH1 (ii)) it is open.

\subsection*{Commentary}

- (1.2): the only symmetry a tensor power has is the exchange of whole strands; "symmetric in the internal indices" would be a different (and wrong) object, a function symmetric in $\alpha$ at fixed $(k_1,k_2)$.
- (1.4): the second identity is $k=\tfrac1n\mathbf 1P+Eq$ with $\mathbf 1\perp\operatorname{im}E$ and $E$ an isometry; it is what makes the free part of the strand kinetic operators separate into a centre-of-mass piece and a relative piece.
- (1.5): the Jacobi coordinates are not themselves permutation-symmetric (they single out an ordering); what is symmetric is the pair ($\ell$, the $S_n$ permutation action), and the $q$ carry the same action in an orthonormal frame. Any other orthonormal frame of $\mathbf 1^\perp$ is as good, by an $O(n-1)$ rotation of $q$.
- (1.9): the quotient is taken fibre by fibre in $P$ — a direct consequence of diagonality; "$\Sigma_2$ modulo $\ker V_4$" is a $P$-dependent statement, which is why the ledger's level-0 rows are stated at fixed $P$.

\subsection*{Result}

$\Sigma_n$ is the symmetric function (1.1)–(1.2) of $n$ momenta, equivalently of $(P,q)$ with (1.3)–(1.7); reality is (1.8); the $n=2$ quotient is (1.9).

## §2 Diagonality in the total momentum (C2)

\subsection*{Setup}

A2, A5, A6. $\hat V_N^{\alpha_1\dots\alpha_N}(k_1,\dots,k_N)$ the stripped vertex on basis states, defined by $V_N(|\alpha_1,k_1\rangle,\dots,|\alpha_N,k_N\rangle)=(2\pi)^d\delta^d(\sum_ak_a)\,\hat V_N^{\alpha_1\dots\alpha_N}(k_1,\dots,k_N)$ (A2). Measures $d\mu_n(q)=n^{-d/2}\prod_{j=1}^{n-1}\frac{d^dq_j}{(2\pi)^d}$ (from (1.4)); at $n=2$, $d\mu_2=\frac{d^d\ell}{(2\pi)^d}$.

\subsection*{Derivation}

*(2.1) Every vertex conserves the total momentum.* $V_N$ is one sphere correlator of the $N$ vertex operators (integrated over the vertex region with the antighost insertions, Erbin 2023 / conv. C4); the zero-mode integral of the matter CFT gives the factor $(2\pi)^d\delta^d(\sum_ak_a)$ for plane-wave states (book `bos_feynman.tex` l. 301–309; zero-mode footnote `bos_path_integral_closed_scattering.tex` l. 719; the explicit correlator is Polchinski vol. 1 ch. 6, equation number unverified), and no other factor of the vertex is singular in the momenta: the region, the local coordinates and the ghost insertions add no delta, only smooth momentum dependence ($\prod|\rho_a|^{\alpha'k_a^2/2}$, $\prod|z_{ab}|^{\alpha'k_a\cdot k_b}$). So $V_N$ has exactly one momentum delta, on the sum of all $N$ strand momenta, whatever the grouping of the strands into fields.

*(2.2) The quadratic form at every $n$.* Insert (1.1) for $\Sigma_n$ (momenta $k'$, labels $\alpha'$) and $\Sigma'_n$ (momenta $k$, labels $\alpha$) into $V_{2n}$, use (2.1) and change variables by (1.3)–(1.4) in each block: the delta becomes $(2\pi)^d\delta^d(P'+P)$, the $P'$ integral is done against it, and
$$V_{2n}(\Sigma_n,\Sigma'_n)=\int\frac{d^dP}{(2\pi)^d}\sum_{\alpha',\alpha}\int d\mu_n(q')\,d\mu_n(q)\;\sigma_{\alpha'}(-P,q')\;K^{(n)}_{P\,\alpha'\alpha}(q',q)\;\sigma'_{\alpha}(P,q),\qquad K^{(n)}_{P\,\alpha'\alpha}(q',q)=\hat V_{2n}^{\alpha'\alpha}\bigl(k'(-P,q'),k(P,q)\bigr),\tag{2.2}$$
with $k(P,q)=\tfrac1n\mathbf 1P+Eq$ the inverse of (1.3). This is the diagonal form: $\sigma(-P,\cdot)$ is paired with $\sigma'(P,\cdot)$ only, through an integral kernel $K^{(n)}_P$ in the $2(n-1)$ relative momenta (and the labels), which carries $P$ as a parameter. By Lorentz covariance $K^{(n)}_P$ depends on $P^2$, $P\cdot q_j$, $P\cdot q'_j$ and the $q\cdot q$ invariants, so in general only the stabiliser $O(d-1)$ of $P$ acts on the relative momenta at fixed $P$. At $n=2$, level 0, (2.2) is the ledger's object: $K^{(2)}_P{}_{TT,TT}(\ell',\ell)=K_P(\ell',\ell)=C\,F(P^2,(\ell+\ell')^2,(\ell-\ell')^2)$ (049 (1.1), with 049 A2's assignment $k_{3,4}=-(\tfrac P2\pm\ell')$, i.e. the second block at $-P$ with relative momentum $-\ell'$ in the convention of (1.3) — immaterial on the even functions), and R48's reduction to $(s,t,u)$ removes the $P\cdot\ell$ dependence, so that the full $O(26)$ acts on the level-0 fibre (049 (1.2)). The graded symmetry of $V_{2n}$ under exchange of the two blocks gives $K^{(n)}_{-P}(q,q')=\pm K^{(n)}_{P}(q',q)$ (sign of the block exchange, $+$ for even blocks), so the quadratic form at $P$ and at $-P$ is the same form.

*(2.3) Cubic terms conserve the total momentum only.* For $V_{N}(\Sigma_n,\Sigma_m,\Sigma_r)$, $N=n+m+r$, (2.1) gives one factor $(2\pi)^d\delta^d(P_n+P_m+P_r)$ and the $n+m+r-3$ relative momenta are integrated against $\hat V_N$ with no constraint:
$$V_N(\Sigma_n,\Sigma_m,\Sigma_r)=\int\frac{d^dP_nd^dP_m}{(2\pi)^{2d}}\int d\mu_n(q)\,d\mu_m(q')\,d\mu_r(q'')\;\sigma(P_n,q)\,\sigma(P_m,q')\,\sigma(-P_n-P_m,q'')\;\hat V_N\bigl(k(P_n,q),k(P_m,q'),k(-P_n-P_m,q'')\bigr).\tag{2.3}$$
The same holds with $\Psi$ strands: the mixed term $V_4(\Sigma_2,\Psi,\Psi)$ of R1 is (2.2) with $\sigma'$ replaced by the two-string state $\Psi\otimes\Psi$, whose coefficient in the variables (1.3) at $n=2$ is
$$(\psi\otimes\psi)_{\alpha_1\alpha_2}(P,\ell)=\psi_{\alpha_1}(\tfrac P2+\ell)\,\psi_{\alpha_2}(\tfrac P2-\ell):\qquad V_4(\Sigma_2,\Psi,\Psi)=\int\frac{d^dP}{(2\pi)^d}\int\frac{d^d\ell'\,d^d\ell}{(2\pi)^{2d}}\;\sigma(-P,\ell')\,K^{(2)}_P(\ell',\ell)\,(\psi\otimes\psi)(P,\ell),\tag{2.4}$$
a source for $\sigma$ at $P$ built from every $\Psi$ pair of total momentum $P$ — a convolution in the $\Psi$ momenta, diagonal in $P$. Under A6 this is the whole statement; a term $V_{2n+r}(\Sigma_n,\Sigma_n,\Psi_0^{\otimes r})$ of a dressed kernel (R70) at a background with momentum $\sum k_{\Psi_0}\ne0$ carries $\delta^d(P+P'+\sum k_{\Psi_0})$ and is diagonal in $P$ only for a translation-invariant background.

*(2.5) "Non-local in the relative coordinate".* The position-space form of the fibre kernel is its Fourier transform in the relative momenta, $\tilde K_P(y',y)=\int d\mu(q')d\mu(q)\,e^{-i q'\cdot y'+iq\cdot y}K_P(q',q)$, $y$ the relative coordinates conjugate to $q$ (at $n=2$, $y=x_1-x_2$ conjugate to $\ell$; since the relative momenta are Wick-rotated, PH25, $y$ is the Euclidean relative coordinate). A kinetic term local in the relative coordinate is $\tilde K_P(y',y)=D_P(\partial_y)\delta^d(y-y')$, i.e. $K_P(q',q)=(2\pi)^d\delta^d(q-q')\,p_P(q)$ with $p_P$ polynomial; its range is the number of derivatives. What the ledger has at level 0, $n=2$ (R51, under PH25, PH29, PH30, $\beta>\sqrt2$): $K_P(\ell',\ell)$ is a continuous function, entire in $(\ell,\ell')$ (050 (8.1)(a)), with the Gaussian bound $|K_P|\le Cc_s\exp[-a(\rho^2+\lambda^2)]$, $a=\alpha'(\ln\beta-\tfrac12\ln2)$ (049 (1.2)); hence $K_P\in L^1\cap L^2(\mathbb R^{52})$ (the bound gives $L^1$; Hilbert–Schmidt alone would give only that no $\delta$-type local part exists) and $\tilde K_P$ is a bounded continuous function, $|\tilde K_P(y',y)|\le\|K_P\|_{L^1}$ (also vanishing at infinity by Riemann–Lebesgue, and smooth by the Gaussian decay in momentum): no $\delta^d(y-y')$, no derivatives — the opposite extreme of a local operator, a smoothing (compact) one. The quadratic action at level 0 is a bilocal functional in the relative coordinate at every $P$, with a kernel that is nowhere singular. **The range** is the decay scale of $\tilde K_P$ in $(y',y)$. The Gaussian bound on real momenta bounds $\tilde K_P$ but not its decay (decay in $y$ needs control of $K_P$ off the real momentum axes); 049 (1.5) records the growth off the real axis in $s$ only (a factor $e^{\pi\alpha'|\operatorname{Im}s|/2}$ per tile), not in $(t,u)$, so no range is recorded. *Expected, not derived:* a kernel of Gaussian decay rate $a$ in $\rho^2+\lambda^2$ whose complex growth is that of a Gaussian would give $\tilde K_P$ a Gaussian decay of width $\sim(2a)^{1/2}\sim\sqrt{\alpha'}$ in the relative coordinate — a string-length range, as for a contact vertex — but the complex-$(t,u)$ growth bound needed to make this a statement is not in the records. Tagged as a gap (C2.4).

\subsection*{Commentary}

- (2.1)–(2.2) are a change of variables under one delta function; there is no computation in them. The content is the assignment of the delta to the total momenta of the two blocks, which holds for any grouping of the $2n$ strands — in particular also for the asymmetric groupings of record 071.
- (2.2): the exchange symmetry (1.7) of each $\sigma$ and the $S_{2n}$ symmetry of $V_{2n}$ make $K^{(n)}_P$ invariant under $(q',q)\mapsto(D(\pi')q',D(\pi)q)$ with the matching label permutations (all signs $+1$); at $n=2$, level 0, this is the evenness of $K_P$ in each argument (049 (1.1)).
- (2.5): the statement in the HS section, l. 257, that the kinetic term is not local is made precise at level 0 as: not a differential operator in the relative coordinate but a smoothing integral operator with a bounded continuous position kernel. Its range is open.

## §3 Translation-invariant configurations (C3)

\subsection*{Setup}

A3; (1.1)–(1.4), (2.2).

\subsection*{Derivation}

*(3.1)* A configuration of $\Sigma_n$ is translation invariant iff it is an eigenvector of the total translation generator with eigenvalue zero, $e^{ia\cdot\sum_ip_{(i)}}\Sigma_n=\Sigma_n$ for all $a$, i.e. iff its coefficient is supported at $P=0$:
$$\sigma_\alpha(k_1,\dots,k_n)=(2\pi)^d\delta^d(P)\;\tilde\sigma_\alpha(q_1,\dots,q_{n-1}).\tag{3.1}$$
The relative momenta are untouched: a translation-invariant $\Sigma_n$ is a function of $n-1$ momenta, not of one. At $n=2$, (3.1) is $\sigma_{\alpha_1\alpha_2}(k_1,k_2)=(2\pi)^d\delta^d(k_1+k_2)\,\tilde\sigma_{\alpha_1\alpha_2}(\ell)$, a function of one momentum $\ell=\tfrac12(k_1-k_2)$, graded symmetric under $(\alpha_1\leftrightarrow\alpha_2,\ \ell\to-\ell)$ — Harold's l. 257, which holds for this class and not for a general fluctuation. In position space (coordinates $X=\tfrac1n\sum x_i$ conjugate to $P$ and $y=E^{\!\top}x$ conjugate to $q$), (3.1) is independence of the centre of mass $X$ and arbitrary dependence on the $n-1$ relative coordinates: a "local expression" only in the sense that it does not depend on where the configuration sits, not that it is a function of one point.

*(3.2)* On (3.1) the quadratic form (2.2) reduces to the $P=0$ fibre, $V_{2n}=\mathrm{Vol}\cdot\int d\mu(q')d\mu(q)\,\tilde\sigma(q')K^{(n)}_{0}(q',q)\tilde\sigma(q)$ ($\mathrm{Vol}=(2\pi)^d\delta^d(0)$ the spacetime volume, as for any zero-momentum action density), with the full $O(d)$ acting on the relative momenta at $P=0$ (A1: the stabiliser of $P=0$ is $O(d)$); at $n=2$, level 0, $K^{(2)}_0=K_P(s=0)$, in the kernel's entire $s$-dependence (R51).

*(3.3) The general sentence for l. 258.* A translation-invariant configuration of $\Sigma_n$ (a saddle, an expectation value) depends on $n-1$ relative momenta — "$(n-1)$-local" in this sense; a general fluctuation of $\Sigma_n$ depends on $n$ momenta, the total momentum $P$ and $n-1$ relative ones, and the quadratic action is diagonal in $P$ but an integral kernel in the relative momenta. At $n=2$ the two statements are "one momentum" and "two momenta", the second being how the ledger's level-0 rows use the field (R51–R55: $K_P$ on functions of $\ell$ at fixed $P$).

\subsection*{Result}

Translation invariance fixes $P=0$ and nothing else: $n-1$ momenta remain at every $n$, one at $n=2$ (3.1); the fluctuation keeps $n$. The reduction is a property of the class (3.1), not of the field.

## §4 Level-0 quadratic action and source term (C4)

\subsection*{Setup}

A4, A5; (1.1), (2.2), (2.4). Level 0: $\Psi=\int\frac{d^dk}{(2\pi)^d}\,t(k)\,|T,k\rangle$ and the tachyon-pair component $\sigma(k_1,k_2)$ of $\Sigma_2$, written $\sigma(P,\ell)$, even in $\ell$ (1.7). $K_P(\ell',\ell)=C\,F\bigl(P^2,(\ell+\ell')^2,(\ell-\ell')^2\bigr)$ (049 (1.1)) with the flat measure $d^{26}\ell$ and 049 A4's normalisation: $C=+1$ is the ghost normalisation of the companion record; the book-normalised action-side stripped $V_4$ on the block is $K_P^{\rm book}=\kappa F$ with $\kappa>0$ (R43, R47; conv. C5, $V_n=-\{\cdot\}_{\rm pot}$). $K_P^{\rm book}(s)$ is positive definite at every real $s$ (R53), $\mu_I(s)>0$. The formulas below are written with $K_P:=K_P^{\rm book}$. Eigenpairs $(\mu_I(s),\lambda_I)$ of $K_P(s)$ on $L^2_{\rm even}(\mathbb R^{26},d^{26}\ell)$, real orthonormal (the eigenfunctions $\lambda_I=\lambda_I(P^2;\ell)$ depend on $P^2$, since $F$ is not separable in $s$: R48's integrand has $\lvert Z\rvert^{\alpha'(x-y-z)/4}$ over permutations of $(s,t,u)$), $I=(n,m,j)$ (050 addendum (1)). \subsection*{Derivation}

*(4.1) The action.* R1's action on the level-0 fields, with (2.2) and (2.4) at $n=2$ and $V_2(T(-k),T(k))=-\tfrac12L_0^+(k)=1-\tfrac{\alpha'}4k^2$ (conv. C5; 064 (1.2) at $k=0$):
$$\begin{aligned}
S^{(0)}_{\rm HS}[t,\sigma]={}&\frac12\int\frac{d^dk}{(2\pi)^d}\,t(-k)\Bigl(1-\frac{\alpha'}4k^2\Bigr)t(k)+\frac{g_s}{3!}V_3(\Psi^3)\\
&-\frac{g_s^2}{4!}\int\frac{d^dP}{(2\pi)^d}\int\frac{d^d\ell'\,d^d\ell}{(2\pi)^{2d}}\;\sigma(-P,\ell')\,K_P(\ell',\ell)\,\sigma(P,\ell)\\
&+\frac{2g_s^2}{4!}\int\frac{d^dP}{(2\pi)^d}\int\frac{d^d\ell'\,d^d\ell}{(2\pi)^{2d}}\;\sigma(-P,\ell')\,K_P(\ell',\ell)\;t\bigl(\tfrac P2+\ell\bigr)\,t\bigl(\tfrac P2-\ell\bigr).
\end{aligned}\tag{4.1}$$
The $\Sigma$ block is diagonal in $P$ and an integral operator in $\ell$ at each $P$; the source is the $\Psi$ pair of total momentum $P$, all relative momenta $\ell$ weighted by the same kernel. (The $\Psi^4$ term of the original action is absent: it has been traded for the two $\Sigma$ terms, R1.)

*(4.2) Eigen-mode form.* At real $s$, $\beta>\sqrt2$, $C\ne0$: $K_P(s)=\sum_I\mu_I(s)\,\lambda_I\otimes\lambda_I$ absolutely convergent at every $(\ell,\ell')$, $K_P(s)$ trace class (R55), $\mu_I(s)>0$ for every $I$ (R53, for the action-side kernel $\kappa F$), and $\{\lambda_I\}$ is complete in $L^2_{\rm even}$ (compact self-adjoint with trivial kernel, R53). With $\sigma(P,\ell)=(2\pi)^d\sum_I\hat\sigma_I(P)\lambda_I(\ell)$:
$$S^{(0)}_\Sigma+S^{(0)}_{\Sigma\Psi}=\int\frac{d^dP}{(2\pi)^d}\sum_I\mu_I(P^2)\Bigl[-\frac{g_s^2}{4!}\,\hat\sigma_I(-P)\,\hat\sigma_I(P)+\frac{2g_s^2}{4!}\,\hat\sigma_I(-P)\,\widehat{(tt)}_I(P)\Bigr],\qquad \widehat{(tt)}_I(P)=\frac1{(2\pi)^d}\int d^d\ell\,\lambda_I(\ell)\,t\bigl(\tfrac P2+\ell\bigr)t\bigl(\tfrac P2-\ell\bigr),\tag{4.2}$$
a decoupled family of modes $\hat\sigma_I(P)$ (the eigenbasis $\lambda_I=\lambda_I(P^2;\ell)$ depends on $P^2$), each with the quadratic coefficient $-\tfrac{g_s^2}{4!}\mu_I(P^2)$, an entire, sign-definite function of $P^2$ with no real zero (R53): in the centre-of-mass coordinate an infinite-derivative operator $\mu_I(-\partial^2)$ (its leading behaviour is $\beta^{-\alpha'P^2/2}$), not a mass term; what is absent is a real zero, i.e. a propagating pole. The $\Sigma$-EOM is $\mu_I(P^2)\,[\hat\sigma_I(P)-\widehat{(tt)}_I(P)]=0$ for every $I$, and since no $\mu_I$ vanishes at real $s$ (R53) and the $\lambda_I$ are complete,
$$\sigma(P,\ell)=t\bigl(\tfrac P2+\ell\bigr)\,t\bigl(\tfrac P2-\ell\bigr)\quad\text{on the level-0 block at real }P^2,\tag{4.3}$$
R1's $\Sigma=\Psi^{\otimes2}$ in momentum space, with the quotient (1.9) trivial there. Substituting back gives $+\tfrac{g_s^2}{4!}\int\sigma K_P\sigma$ with $\sigma=tt$, the quartic contact term $\tfrac{g_s^2}{4!}V_4(\Psi^4)$ at level 0, which is the $s$-channel form of 050 addendum (A.1) (R-a)/(R-b) with the collapse $\mu_I\mu_I^{-1}\mu_I=\mu_I$ performed mode by mode. (Under PH25 the source (4.3) is evaluated at Wick-rotated $\ell$, i.e. at complex Lorentzian strand momenta; the records state the kernel's properties on that space and do not continue $t$ itself.)

*(4.4) $P$-dependence: on shell versus off shell.* $K_P$ depends on $P$ only through $s=P^2$ (R48's $(s,t,u)$ reduction; 049 (1.2): the full $O(26)$ acts on the fibre), $s\mapsto K_P(s)$ is entire (049 (1.5)), and the $\mu_I(s)$ are analytic branches away from crossings (049 D1). Three consequences for the quadratic action. (i) *No real zero of the quadratic coefficient.* A propagating mode of mass $m$ would be a zero of the quadratic coefficient on $P^2=-m^2$: for the string field this is $1-\tfrac{\alpha'}4k^2=0$ at $k^2=-m_T^2=4/\alpha'$ (the tachyon pole); for $\sigma_I$ it would be $\mu_I(P^2)=0$, which does not happen at any real $P^2$ (R53: $\ker K_P(s)=0$ at every real $s$). With the eigenvalues of each partial wave ordered by size, $s\mapsto\mu_{n,j}(s)$ is continuous ($K_P(s)$ is an entire Hilbert–Schmidt-valued function, 049 (1.5); Weyl's inequality $\lvert\mu_j(A)-\mu_j(B)\rvert\le\|A-B\|$) and positive, hence bounded away from $0$ on every compact real interval: no fixed-label mode has a pole at real $s$. R53 does not exclude an analytic branch tending to $0$ at a real $s$ with degenerating eigenvectors; such a branch, like a complex zero off the plumbing lattice $\alpha's\in\{4,0,-4,\dots\}$ (PH18; 049 D1), would be a new singularity of the propagator $\tfrac{12}{g_s^2}\mu_I(P^2)^{-1}$ (weight $e^S$ with $S=-\tfrac{g_s^2}{4!}\mu_I x^2$ gives $\langle x^2\rangle=12/(g_s^2\mu_I)$, the convention of R68; 050 addendum (A.1) (R-c): $m_2^{-1}=2g_T^2\sum_I\mu_I^{-1}\lambda_I\otimes\lambda_I$). The propagator is unbounded through $\mu_I\to0$ as $I\to\infty$. "Real momentum" here is real total momentum $P$; the relative momenta are Wick-rotated (PH25). $P$ enters through $s$ only (R48) and $s$ is real for real $P$ (PH25, 049 (1.5)). (ii) *Off shell*, $\mu_I(s)$ is a smooth, sign-definite function of $s$: the $s$-dependence of the leading eigenvalues is dominated by the prefactor $\beta^{-\alpha's/2}$ of $F$ (049 Part 3: the ratio $\mu_1(4)/\mu_1(16/3)$ exceeds the factor from the prefactor alone at both $\beta$; numerical values from the companion SL(2,C) computation, not reproduced here; R51, normalisation $C=+1$, book action-side value $\times\kappa$). (iii) *On-shell external $\Psi$*: for $t(k)$ supported on $k^2=-m_T^2$ both strands $k_{1,2}=\tfrac P2\pm\ell$ are on shell iff $k_1^2=k_2^2$, i.e. $P\cdot\ell=0$, and $|\ell|=\rho_0(s)$, $\alpha'\rho_0^2=4-\alpha's/4$ (050 addendum (1); real for $\alpha's<16$); the source (4.2) at total momentum $P$ is supported on the 24-sphere $|\ell|=\rho_0(s)$ in the hyperplane orthogonal to $P$ (harmless for the $O(26)$-invariant pairings): $\widehat{(tt)}_I(P)\propto\lambda_I$ averaged over that sphere, and the exchange (R29) is $\sum_I\mu_I(s)\lambda_I(\ell_0)\lambda_I(\ell_0')$ at $|\ell_0|=|\ell_0'|=\rho_0(s)$ — the on-shell limit is analytic in $s$ on that region (R29's on-shell clause, 050 addendum (2)).

## §6 Interpretation remark (C6)

*The relabelling.* At fixed $P$ one may write $\sigma_q(P):=\sigma(P,q)$ and read the relative momenta $q$ as an internal index: (2.2) then looks like the action of a field $\sigma_q(X)$ local in the centre-of-mass coordinate $X$ (conjugate to $P$), with a $P$-dependent "mass matrix" $K_P(q',q)$ that is not diagonal in the index — "centre-of-mass locality", reading (iii) of `knowledge/decisions.md` (2026-10-01, second round). *Why it is not the definition* (Harold's decision, with the reasons the record supports): the auxiliary field is not expected to be local in spacetime, and momenta along non-compact directions are not labels — $q$ ranges over $\mathbb R^{d(n-1)}$ with the Lebesgue measure, there is no finite- or countable-dimensional internal space, and the index space itself depends on $P$ (the quotient $\ker K_P$ of (1.9), the stabiliser $O(d-1)$ of (2.2)), which no internal label does; at level 0 the "mass matrix" is a smoothing operator (2.5), i.e. it couples every label to every other. (Along compact directions the relative momenta would be discrete, Kaluza–Klein-like labels, a different situation outside this record.) *Where it helps.* (a) The eigen-mode form (4.2) is the closest legitimate version of the relabelling: at each $P$, $K_P$ is compact (R51) and definite (R53), so its eigenbasis gives a discrete index $I$ and modes $\hat\sigma_I(P)$. They are not local fields of the centre of mass: the basis $\lambda_I(P^2;\cdot)$ depends on $P^2$ and each mode carries the entire, non-polynomial coefficient $\mu_I(P^2)$. The tower is a momentum-space statement at fixed $P$, at level 0 under R51–R55's conditions. (b) The single-string picture of record 071 (the $n$ punctures merged into one by repeated OPE): the OPE of $e^{ik_1X}(z_1)e^{ik_2X}(z_2)$ expands in powers of $z_{12}$ with coefficients polynomial in the relative momentum, so (caveat, for record 071: the OPE also carries the relative momentum through the non-polynomial power $\lvert z_{12}\rvert^{\alpha'(P^2/4-\ell^2)}$, not only through the polynomial Taylor coefficients) the relative momenta are encoded in the level content of one string state at momentum $P$ — the internal label is then the string level, discrete, and the relabelling is the dictionary between the two descriptions; what it does not carry is the continuous dependence on $q$ at fixed level, which is why it is a dictionary for the picture and not a definition. (c) R27's annotation (the relative momentum is the loop momentum of the doubled surface) is a reason to integrate over it, not to label by it.
