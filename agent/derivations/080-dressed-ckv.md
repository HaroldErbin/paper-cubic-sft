## §1 (C1) – The criterion

\subsection*{Setup}
A1, A4. A sphere with two marked ordinary punctures $u_i\ne u_j$ in a global coordinate $u$ (the "patch frame": in 078 the circle frame, $u_{1,2}=\pm z$, $z=\tfrac1{2\beta}$). The conformal Killing vectors of the sphere vanishing at $u_i$ and $u_j$ form the one-dimensional space spanned by the dilation about the pair,
$$
v(u)\,\partial_u,\qquad v(u)=\kappa\,(u-u_i)(u-u_j),\qquad \kappa\in\mathbb C^*, \tag{1.1}
$$
(a holomorphic vector field on the sphere is a polynomial of degree $\le2$ in $u$; two prescribed zeros fix it up to scale). A local coordinate at $u_i$ is a map $u=F(w)$, $F(0)=u_i$, $F'(0)=a\ne0$, holomorphic on $|w|\le1$; in the local frame the same vector field reads $\tilde v(w)\partial_w$ with
$$
\tilde v(w)=\frac{v(F(w))}{F'(w)} \tag{1.2}
$$
(push-forward of a vector field, $v\,\partial_u=v\,\frac{dw}{du}\partial_w$). Write $\tilde v(w)=\kappa_i\sum_{k\ge1}v_kw^k$ with $v_1=1$, so $\kappa_i=v'(u_i)=\kappa(u_i-u_j)$, and define the intrinsic second-order datum
$$
\mu_i:=v_2=\frac{\tilde v''(0)}{2\,\tilde v'(0)} . \tag{1.3}
$$
It depends only on the vector field and the local coordinate, not on the global chart (because $\tilde v$ does not). It does depend on the phase of the local coordinate: under $w\to e^{i\theta}w$, $\mu_i\to e^{-i\theta}\mu_i$, so "$\mu$ real" is a property of a phase choice, while $\mu_i=0$ is intrinsic (the criterion (1.6) is unaffected).

\subsection*{Derivation}
*Möbius local map in the chart $u_i=0$, $u_j=\infty$.* Here $v=u\partial_u$ (take $\kappa(u_i-u_j)\to1$ in the limit $u_j\to\infty$) and $F(w)=\dfrac{\lambda w}{1+\mu w}$, $F'(w)=\dfrac{\lambda}{(1+\mu w)^2}$:
$$
\tilde v(w)=\frac{F(w)}{F'(w)}=\frac{\lambda w}{1+\mu w}\cdot\frac{(1+\mu w)^2}{\lambda}=w+\mu w^2 . \tag{1.4}
$$
*Möbius local map in a general chart.* $F(w)=u_i+\dfrac{a w}{1+\mu w}$, so $F(\infty)=:R=u_i+a/\mu$ (the pole of the coordinate, the companion's $R_i$), and with (1.1)
$$
\begin{aligned}
\tilde v(w)&=\kappa\,\frac{aw}{1+\mu w}\cdot\frac{(u_i-u_j)(1+\mu w)+aw}{1+\mu w}\cdot\frac{(1+\mu w)^2}{a}
=\kappa(u_i-u_j)\,w\,\Big[1+\Big(\mu+\frac{a}{u_i-u_j}\Big)w\Big],\\
\mu_i&=\mu+\frac{a}{u_i-u_j}=\frac{a\,(R-u_j)}{(u_i-u_j)(R-u_i)} .
\end{aligned}\tag{1.5}
$$
The germ is exactly quadratic: $v_k=0$ for $k\ge3$. Hence, for a Möbius local coordinate,
$$
\boxed{\ \tilde v=\kappa_i\,w\,\partial_w\ \text{ exactly}\iff \mu_i=0\iff F(\infty)=u_j\ } \tag{1.6}
$$
— the pole of the coordinate at $u_i$ sits at the other ordinary puncture. In the chart $u_i=0,u_j=\infty$ this is $\mu=0$: $F$ is a pure dilation $w\mapsto\lambda w$. In any chart it reads: $F=\phi^{-1}\circ(\lambda\,\cdot)$ where $\phi$ is the Möbius map sending $(u_i,u_j)\mapsto(0,\infty)$ — a dilation composed with the chart's fixed data ($\phi$ is fixed by the pair up to the dilation itself).

*General local map.* With $F(w)=u_i+aw+bw^2+cw^3+O(w^4)$, (1.2) gives
$$
\tilde v(w)=\kappa(u_i-u_j)\,w\Big[1+\Big(\frac{a}{u_i-u_j}-\frac{b}{a}\Big)w+O(w^2)\Big],\qquad
\mu_i=\frac{a}{u_i-u_j}-\frac{b}{a}=\frac{a}{u_i-u_j}-2a\,c_i, \tag{1.7}
$$
with $c_i=b/2a^2$ the Bergman–Zwiebach second-order datum (companion construction; for Möbius $b=-a\mu$, and (1.7) reduces to (1.5)). The exact criterion: $\tilde v=\kappa_iw\partial_w$ identically iff $F$ solves $F'(w)\,w=\kappa_i^{-1}v(F(w))=\dfrac{(F-u_i)(F-u_j)}{u_i-u_j}$, i.e. $\dfrac{dF}{(F-u_i)(F-u_j)}=\dfrac{dw}{(u_i-u_j)\,w}$, whose solutions with $F(0)=u_i$, $F'(0)\ne0$ are
$$
\frac{F-u_i}{F-u_j}=C\,w\quad\Longrightarrow\quad F(w)=\frac{u_i-u_jCw}{1-Cw},\qquad F(\infty)=u_j . \tag{1.8}
$$
$$
\boxed{\ \text{The CKV reads }\kappa_i\,w\partial_w\text{ in the local frame iff }F\text{ is Möbius with }F(\infty)=u_j.\ \text{No non-Möbius map qualifies.}\ } \tag{1.9}
$$
At finite order: $v_k=0$ for $2\le k\le K$ iff the $K$-jet of $F$ at $0$ agrees with that of (1.8) for some $C$ (the ODE determines the jet of $F$ order by order from $a=F'(0)$).

\subsection*{Commentary}
(1.1): $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ counting; in 078 (4.1) $\kappa=\beta$, $u_{1,2}=\pm z$, so $\kappa_1=\beta\cdot2z=+1$, $\kappa_2=-1$, matching $(f_2)_*(w\partial_w)=-v$. (1.2): the vector field is intrinsic; this is the only input. (1.4) is the brief's statement; (1.5) its covariant form, where $a/(u_i-u_j)$ is the contribution of the chart ($v$ is not linear in $u$ about $u_i$ unless $u_j=\infty$). (1.6): $R-u_j=0$ with $a\ne0$, $R\ne u_i$. (1.8): separation of variables; the integration constant $C$ is $-\mu$ in the chart $u_j=\infty$. The exponent of $w$ in (1.8) is forced to be $1$ by $F'(0)\ne0$: the general solution of the ODE with vector field $\kappa'(F-u_i)(F-u_j)$ for the wrong $\kappa'$ is $(F-u_i)/(F-u_j)=Cw^{\kappa'(u_i-u_j)}$, not a local coordinate unless the exponent is $1$ — which is why the normalisation $\kappa_i=v'(u_i)$ of the local-frame vector field is not a free choice.

\subsection*{Result}
(1.6), (1.9): the selection rule's premise — $v=\pm w\partial_w$ exactly in both ordinary frames — holds iff each ordinary local coordinate is the Möbius map whose pole is the other ordinary puncture. The failure is measured by the single intrinsic number $\mu_i$ of (1.3), exactly quadratic for Möbius maps. For the rigid $\mathcal V_3'$ in the $z$-frame ($u_i=0$, $u_j=\infty$, $F=f_0'$, 032 §1): $\lambda=1/\gamma'$, $\mu=\alpha'_{\rm v}/\gamma'$, so $\mu_1=\mu_2=\alpha'_{\rm v}/\gamma'$ (the second puncture by $z\to1/z$), zero iff $\alpha'_{\rm v}=0$ — 078's commentary line, now with its proof. [C1]

## §2 (C2) – The conservation law

\subsection*{Setup}
A1, A4; the notation of §1 with $u_{1,2}=\pm z$, $z=\tfrac1{2\beta}$, $\kappa=\beta$, so $v(u)=\beta(u^2-z^2)$, $\kappa_1=+1$, $\kappa_2=-1$ (078 (4.1)). The circle state $\Phi_2(A_1,A_2)$ is the element of the $u$-plane Fock space defined by $\langle\chi|\Phi_2(A_1,A_2)\rangle=\big\langle \chi(\infty)\,A_1(u_1)\,A_2(u_2)\big\rangle$, the inputs inserted through their local coordinates $u=F_i(w_i)$ and $\chi$ through $w_0=1/u$; modes on the output are $b_n=\oint_{|u|=1}u^{n+1}b(u)\,\frac{du}{2\pi i}$, the contour enclosing both ordinary discs. Levels: $b_nA=0$ and $L_nA=0$ for $n>L(A)$, the level of $A$ above $c_1\bar c_1|k\rangle$.

\subsection*{Derivation}
The 1-form $\omega:=v(u)\,b(u)\,du$ is coordinate-invariant ($b$ of weight 2, $v\partial_u$ of weight $-1$): under $u=F(w)$, $v\,b\,du=\tilde v\,\tilde b\,dw$. Inside the correlator it is meromorphic on the sphere with poles only at the insertions, and regular at $u=\infty$ because $\tilde v(w_0)=-\beta(1-z^2w_0^2)$ is regular there ($v$ is a global CKV). Cauchy's theorem on the annulus between $|u|=1$ and the two ordinary discs:
$$
\oint_{|u|=1}\frac{\omega}{2\pi i}=\sum_{i=1,2}\oint_{|w_i|=1}\frac{\tilde v^{(i)}(w_i)\,\tilde b(w_i)\,dw_i}{2\pi i} . \tag{2.1}
$$
Left side on the output: $\oint u^{n+1}b=b_n$ gives $\beta(b_1-z^2b_{-1})$. Right side on the inputs, with $\tilde v^{(i)}=\kappa_i\sum_{k\ge1}v^{(i)}_kw^k$ and $\oint w^{k}\tilde b(w)\,\frac{dw}{2\pi i}=b_{k-1}$:
$$
\hat b^{(i)}:=\oint\frac{\tilde v^{(i)}\tilde b}{2\pi i}=\kappa_i\sum_{k\ge1}v^{(i)}_k\,b_{k-1}=\kappa_i\big(b_0+\mu_i\,b_1+v^{(i)}_3b_2+\dots\big), \tag{2.2}
$$
and, passing the Grassmann-odd contour past $A_1$ for the second term (Koszul sign),
$$
\beta\big(b_1-z^2b_{-1}\big)\Phi_2(A_1,A_2)=\Phi_2\big(\hat b^{(1)}A_1,A_2\big)+(-1)^{|A_1|}\Phi_2\big(A_1,\hat b^{(2)}A_2\big),\qquad \kappa_1=1,\ \kappa_2=-1 . \tag{2.3}
$$
The antiholomorphic copy uses $\bar\omega=\bar v(\bar u)\bar b(\bar u)d\bar u$ with $\bar v=\beta(\bar u^2-z^2)$ ($v$ has real coefficients), giving $\beta(\bar b_1-z^2\bar b_{-1})$ on the left and $\hat{\bar b}^{(i)}=\kappa_i(\bar b_0+\mu_i^*\bar b_1+\dots)$ on the right ($\kappa_i$ real, $\overline{\tilde v}(\bar w)=\tilde v(w)^*$). Subtracting, with $O_b:=b_1^--z^2b_{-1}^-$:
$$
\frac1\beta\,O_b\,\Phi_2(A_1,A_2)=\Phi_2\big(D_1A_1,A_2\big)-(-1)^{|A_1|}\Phi_2\big(A_1,D_2A_2\big),\qquad
D_i:=b_0^-+\mu_ib_1-\mu_i^*\bar b_1+\sum_{k\ge3}\big(v^{(i)}_kb_{k-1}-\bar v^{(i)}_k\bar b_{k-1}\big). \tag{2.4}
$$
The same with $T$ in place of $b$ ($v\,T\,du$ is a 1-form in a correlator up to the anomaly, which is absent for a global CKV paired with a vector field regular everywhere on the sphere minus the insertions — the Schwarzian of a Möbius map vanishes and the $T$ transformation picks none up along (2.1)): $O_L:=L_1^--z^2L_{-1}^-$ and $D^L_i:=L_0^-+\mu_iL_1-\mu_i^*\bar L_1+\dots$.

For level-matched inputs ($A_i\in\mathcal H_0$, $b_0^-A_i=0=L_0^-A_i$) the first term of each $D_i$ drops. The identities of 078 (4.3) then read
$$
\boxed{\ \frac1\beta O_b\Phi_2(A_1,A_2)=\Phi_2\big(D_1'A_1,A_2\big)-(-1)^{|A_1|}\Phi_2\big(A_1,D_2'A_2\big),\quad D_i':=\mu_ib_1-\mu_i^*\bar b_1+\sum_{k\ge3}(v^{(i)}_kb_{k-1}-\bar v^{(i)}_k\bar b_{k-1})\ } \tag{2.5}
$$
and the selection rule $O_b\Phi_2(\mathrm{Sym}^2\mathcal H_0)=0$ holds iff the right side vanishes on all inputs. *If*: the rule on inputs of level $\le L$ at both punctures holds when $v^{(i)}_k=0$ for $2\le k\le L+1$, $i=1,2$, and on inputs of all levels when $v^{(i)}_k=0$ for all $k\ge2$, i.e. by (1.9) when both ordinary local maps are Möbius with poles at the other ordinary puncture. *Only if*, for Möbius maps (the case of the actual section, §3): $\mu_1\ne0$ breaks the rule already at level $(1,1)$, by the symmetrised pair of C4.1, (4.1)–(4.4), whose odd-sector component is one non-zero correlator; for maps without the $S_4$ frame the even and odd components vanish together only if $\mu_1=\mu_2=0$. For non-Möbius maps the converse at $k\ge3$ (that each $v_k\ne0$ is detected by some input pair) is not established here and is not needed. That a mode acts non-trivially on some input does not suffice on its own: the right side is a difference of two images and could cancel; the symmetrised pair is what closes the step.

\subsection*{Commentary}
(2.1): the standard conservation-law (Ward identity) argument, Erbin 2023 (Ward identities for a holomorphic current), as in 078 and Rastelli–Zwiebach hep-th/0006240 §3: the only inputs are the meromorphy of $\omega$ in the correlator and the regularity at $u=\infty$, which holds for every local map at the ordinary punctures ($v$ is fixed by the puncture positions alone, (1.1)) — so the left side of (2.3), $\beta(b_1-z^2b_{-1})$, is universal, and all dependence on the local maps sits in $\hat b^{(i)}$. - **A5** The SL(2,C) quartic section is $S_4$-symmetric (the vertex is a symmetric multilinear form: the coordinate at puncture $i$ of the surface $\Sigma$ is the coordinate at puncture $\sigma(i)$ of the relabelled surface $\sigma\Sigma$) — the defining property of the family (074 (1.6), "any $S_4$-symmetric section (the project's SL(2,C) family)").

\subsection*{Derivation}
*Criterion as a function on $Q_V$.* By (1.5)–(1.6), with $u_1-u_2=2z=1/\beta$:
$$

\qquad\text{rule at }q\iff R_1(q)=u_2\ \text{and}\ R_2(q)=u_1 . \tag{3.1}
$$
Equivalently in BZ data, by (1.7): the rule holds at $q$ iff $c_1(q)=\tfrac{1}{2(u_1-u_2)}=\tfrac\beta2$ and $c_2(q)=-\tfrac\beta2$. These are the data that decide the question: the pole positions $R_{1,2}(q)$ (or $c_{1,2}(q)$) of the vertex's coordinates at the two punctures of one half, in the circle frame, as functions on $Q_V$. They are not in the inputs of this record (the companion sources name the family and the datum, not the four-point closed form), so (3.1) is stated and not evaluated on $\mathcal V_{0,4}$'s interior; the two things that can be evaluated follow.

*The rigid halves (all $q$).* $F_i$ transported from $\mathcal V_3'$: $\mu_1=\mu_2=\alpha'_{\rm v}/\gamma'$, constant on $Q_V$ (§1 Result, frame independence). At 032's parameter point $\alpha'_{\rm v}=0$, $\gamma'=3$, $\beta=3/2$: $R_1=u_2=-\tfrac13$, $R_2=u_1=\tfrac13$ (078 (4.1): $f_1(\infty)=-z$); at $\alpha'_{\rm v}=\tfrac12$: $\mu_i=\tfrac16$. [C3.1]

*The boundary $|q|=1$.* The section of $\widehat{\mathcal P}_{0,4}$ is continuous across $\partial\mathcal F_s\cap\partial\mathcal V_{0,4}$ (the geometric BV master equation needs the vertex's boundary to cancel the propagator's: the surfaces *and* their coordinates agree there; Zwiebach 1993, hep-th/9206084, and Erbin 2023, arXiv:2301.01686, on vertex and Feynman regions sharing their boundary sections; R2 supplies the $|q|=1$ curve; 074 (1.6) first sentence states only that over $\mathcal F_s$ the local coordinates are the halves' own). *The interior: $\mu\equiv0$ is impossible for a symmetric section.* Suppose $R_1(q)=u_2$ for all $q\in Q_V$. \subsection*{Setup}

\subsection*{Derivation}
*C4.1 — The refutation mechanism does not transfer.* R84's deficit is the exact rule $O_b\mathcal I=0$ (and $O_L$), which by C2 needs $\mu_i=0$ at both punctures of the half. ($\bar b_{-1}\bar c_1|0\rangle=|0\rangle$, so $A_1=-c_{-1}c_1|k_1\rangle$: $L_0=\bar L_0=h_1$, level $(1,1)$ relative to $c_1\bar c_1|k_1\rangle$, ghost number 2, $b_0^-A_1=0$).
