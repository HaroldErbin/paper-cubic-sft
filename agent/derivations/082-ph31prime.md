## §1 (C1) – the vector field is a global CKV, non-vanishing at the three $\Psi$ punctures

Setup: global coordinate $u$ on the glued sphere; the $\Sigma$-half is the disc $|u|\le\rho$ carrying its two ordinary punctures at $u=\pm z$ and the gluing circle $|u|=\rho$; the other patch is any rigid patch whose boundary is that circle, carrying the three $\Psi$ punctures at generic points $u_1,u_2,u_3$ with $|u_k|>\rho$ (all $\ne\pm z$). Only these facts are used, not the patch's maps.

$v=\beta(u^2-z^2)\partial_u$ has polynomial coefficient of degree 2. In $w=1/u$, $\partial_u=-w^2\partial_w$, so $v=-\beta(1-z^2w^2)\partial_w$, regular at $w=0$ (value $-\beta\partial_w$, non-zero). Hence $v$ is a holomorphic vector field on the whole sphere, i.e. an element of $\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb C)$ – a global conformal Killing vector of the glued sphere, independent of the choice of the second patch. (1.1)

A non-zero element of $\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb C)$ has exactly two zeros counted with multiplicity (a section of $T\mathbb P^1=\mathcal O(2)$). Here they are $u=\pm z$ (simple, $z\ne0$), and $u=\infty$ is not a zero. (1.2)

Therefore $v(u_k)=\beta(u_k^2-z^2)\ne0$ for $k=1,2,3$: $v$ vanishes at no $\Psi$ puncture. (1.3) The real field $v+\bar v$ continues through the circle unchanged, so the same contour $\oint b(v)$ can be deformed either into the $\Sigma$-half (giving 078's $O_b$) or into the other patch (§2). Holomorphy in the annulus is all that is needed; no condition on the second patch's maps enters.

Result C1: proven (elementary). Limiting case: if two of the $u_k$ are moved onto $\pm z$ (the zeros), $v$ vanishes there – the configuration of 078's own conservation law on a three-punctured patch; with generic $u_k$ this cannot happen.

## §2 (C2) – the conservation law on the three-$\Psi$ side

\subsection*{Setup}
Glued sphere as in §1: $\Sigma$-half $D=\{|u|\le1\}$ with punctures $\pm z$ (local coordinates in which $v=\pm w\partial_w$, 078 (4.1)); other patch $P=\{|u|\ge1\}$, with coordinate $t=1/u$ ($P=\{|t|\le1\}$), punctures $t_k=1/u_k$, local maps $t=h_k(w_k)$, $h_k(0)=t_k$, $h_k(w)=t_k+h_{k1}w+h_{k2}w^2+h_{k3}w^3+\dots$, $h_{k1}\ne0$. $\Xi(A_1,A_2,A_3)$: the circle state of $P$, a ket in the $t$-frame Fock space, for inputs $A_k\in\mathcal H_0$; it may carry antighost insertions $B_j=\oint b(\xi_j)$ (the moduli insertions of the five-point vertex), written to the left of the inputs. $\eta=\eta^t(t)\partial_t$: any global CKV, $\eta^t(t)=\eta_{-1}+\eta_0t+\eta_1t^2$. Modes: $b[\eta]:=\oint_{|t|=1}\frac{dt}{2\pi i}\eta^t(t)b(t)=\eta_{-1}b_{-1}+\eta_0b_0+\eta_1b_1$ (with $b_n=\oint\frac{dt}{2\pi i}t^{n+1}b(t)$), $\bar b[\bar\eta]$ the same with bars and complex-conjugate coefficients, $b^-[\eta]:=b[\eta]-\bar b[\bar\eta]$; likewise $L[\eta]$, $L^-[\eta]$.

\subsection*{Derivation}
In the frame $w_k$ the field is $\eta^{(k)}(w)\partial_w$ with $\eta^{(k)}(w)=\eta^t(h_k(w))/h_k'(w)=\sum_{m\ge0}\eta^{(k)}_mw^m$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\eta^{(k)}_0&=\frac{\eta^t(t_k)}{h_{k1}},\qquad
\eta^{(k)}_1=\eta^{t\prime}(t_k)-\frac{2h_{k2}}{h_{k1}^2}\,\eta^t(t_k),\\
\eta^{(k)}_2&=\tfrac12\eta^{t\prime\prime}(t_k)\,h_{k1}-\frac{h_{k2}}{h_{k1}}\,\eta^{t\prime}(t_k)+\frac{4h_{k2}^2-3h_{k1}h_{k3}}{h_{k1}^3}\,\eta^t(t_k).
\end{aligned}\tag{2.1}
$$
The 1-form $\eta^t(t)b(t)dt$ is holomorphic on $P$ minus the three coordinate discs ($\eta$ is regular at $t=0$, an unpunctured interior point of $P$). Deforming the boundary contour inwards,
$$
b[\eta]\,\Xi(A_1,A_2,A_3)=(-1)^{n_B}\sum_{k=1}^3(-1)^{|A_1|+\dots+|A_{k-1}|}\,\Xi(\dots,b[\eta]_kA_k,\dots),\qquad b[\eta]_k:=\sum_{m\ge0}\eta^{(k)}_m\,b_{m-1},
\tag{2.2}
$$
$n_B$ the number of antighost insertions. With $T$:
$$
L[\eta]\,\Xi(A_1,A_2,A_3)=\sum_{k=1}^3\Xi(\dots,L[\eta]_kA_k,\dots)+\sum_j\Xi\big|_{B_j\to\oint b([\eta,\xi_j])},\qquad L[\eta]_k:=\sum_{m\ge0}\eta^{(k)}_m\,L_{m-1}.
\tag{2.3}
$$
Subtracting the antiholomorphic copies: $b^-[\eta]\Xi=(-1)^{n_B}\sum_k\pm\Xi(\dots,b^-[\eta]_kA_k,\dots)$, $b^-[\eta]_k=\sum_m(\eta^{(k)}_mb_{m-1}-\bar\eta^{(k)}_m\bar b_{m-1})$. On $A_k\in\ker b_0^-$ the zero-mode term is $(\eta^{(k)}_1-\bar\eta^{(k)}_1)b_0A_k=i\,\mathrm{Im}\,\eta^{(k)}_1\,b_0^+A_k$, which vanishes only when $\eta^{(k)}_1$ is real (pure dilation at the puncture, the 078 situation). (2.4)

Mode content: on an input of level $(N,\bar N)$ above $c_1\bar c_1|p\rangle$, $b_{m-1}A=0$ for $m-1>N$, so (2.2) truncates at $m=N+1$. For Möbius local maps (the SL(2) vertices of this project) $\eta^{(k)}$ is itself in $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ and $\eta^{(k)}_{m\ge3}=0$ exactly. On a tachyon $A=c_1\bar c_1|p\rangle$ only the creation term survives: $b[\eta]_kA=\eta^{(k)}_0\,b_{-1}c_1\bar c_1|p\rangle=\eta^{(k)}_0\,\bar c_1|p\rangle$, $\bar b[\bar\eta]_kA=\bar\eta^{(k)}_0\,\bar b_{-1}c_1\bar c_1|p\rangle=-\bar\eta^{(k)}_0\,c_1|p\rangle$. (2.5)

Circle side. For $\eta=v$ (§1): $v^t(t)=-\beta(1-z^2t^2)$, so $b[v]=-\beta(b_{-1}-z^2b_1)$: in the $t$-frame the $v$-contour is, up to an overall sign, the BPZ image of 078's $O_b$ (this is 078's leg-0 form). The sign depends on the inversion: with $I(t)=1/t$ a weight-2 mode goes to $+b_{-n}$, the rule $(-1)^nb_{-n}$ belongs to $I(t)=-1/t$; immaterial here. For $\tilde v:=\beta(t^2-z^2)\partial_t$ (zeros at $t=\pm z$, i.e. $u=\pm1/z$, inside $P$ since $z<1$): $b^-[\tilde v]=\beta O_b$ literally in the $t$-frame. Which of the two implements "$O_bx$" for $x=G_W^{-1}(\cdot)$ depends on the frame in which $G$ identifies the outside functional with a ket; fixed in §3. Both are global CKVs; (2.2)–(2.4) hold for each.

\subsection*{Commentary}
(2.1): Taylor expansion of $\eta^t(h(w))\cdot h'(w)^{-1}$ with $1/h'=h_1^{-1}\big(1-\tfrac{2h_2}{h_1}w+(\tfrac{4h_2^2}{h_1^2}-\tfrac{3h_3}{h_1})w^2+\dots\big)$; checked in the script (`C2-taylor`). (2.2): the counterclockwise $t$-contour bounding $P$ is homologous in $P\setminus\cup_k\{\text{discs}\}$ to the sum of counterclockwise contours around the discs; holomorphic maps preserve orientation, so each becomes $\oint_0\frac{dw}{2\pi i}\eta^{(k)}b(w)=\sum_m\eta^{(k)}_mb_{m-1}$; the Koszul sign is that of moving the odd contour past $A_1,\dots,A_{k-1}$ and the $n_B$ odd insertions; $\{b(\eta),b(\xi_j)\}=0$ makes the insertions inert. (2.3): $T$ is even, no Koszul sign, but $[L[\eta],\oint b(\xi_j)]=\oint b([\eta,\xi_j])$, so the moduli insertions contribute unless $[\eta,\xi_j]$ is exact on the patch: the $O_L$ law has an extra term on the five-point side that 078's $O_L$ law did not. (2.4): $\bar b_0A=b_0A$ on $\ker b_0^-$, $b_0^+=b_0+\bar b_0$.

\subsection*{Result}
C2: for every global CKV $\eta$, the $b^-[\eta]$ contour on the circle state of the three-$\Psi$ patch equals (with Koszul signs) the sum over the three inputs of $\sum_m(\eta^{(k)}_mb_{m-1}-\mathrm{c.c.})$, whose leading term $\eta^{(k)}_0b_{-1}^{(\pm)}$ is non-zero whenever $\eta$ does not vanish at the puncture; the $L$ version carries an additional moduli-insertion term. Proven (contour deformation).

## §4 (C4) – the six-point composite without H1/PH31

\subsection*{Setup}

\subsection*{Derivation}

\tag{4.1}
$$
*(4.2) What survives.* $G\Pi G=G$ on $\pi_W\mathcal I$ (074 (2.2)): exact, hypothesis-free. ## §5 (C5) – status of PH31′

*Formulation.* Let $V\subset W^{\rm even}$ be the annihilator in $W^{\rm even}$ of the joint span of the $b$- and $L$-functionals of 078 (4.3) ($\chi\circ O_b$ and $\chi\circ O_L$, the latter in the leg-0 frame), restricted to $W$ – the combinations whose $c_0$, $\bar c_0$-sector components cancel. $\ker O_b\cap W^{\rm even}$ is cut out by the $b$-part alone; the $L$-identity enters only through these mixed combinations (083 E1), not as a subspace $\ker O_L|_W$, since $O_L$ does not commute with $\pi_W$. PH31′ splits into two statements that 074's argument needs jointly:

- (a) *Image.* The extended $\pi_W\Phi_2:\mathrm{Sym}^2\mathcal H_0\to W$ is onto $V$ modulo $\ker G$: the cokernel of the circle-state map is exactly the one generated by the selection identities. Evidence: at $K=2$ the cokernel is 5, the joint span of the identities (078 `C4-identity-count`; $b$ alone 3, mixed 2); at $K=3$ record 083 (E1) closes 078's bracket $[34,60]$: cokernel exactly 60 = joint span ($b$ alone 34, $L$ alone 2, mixed 24), over $\mathbb Q$ at a rational evaluation point, for every $L\ge2$, count 60 at two independent points; at generic $P$ this holds off a proper subvariety (083 §3 item 3). So (a) has exact support at the two lowest truncations.
- (b) *Membership.* $x=\int\pi_W\pi_{\rm even}K\Phi_3^{[b]}\in V$ (modulo $\ker G$).

R80 C4's sector formula needs $x\in\pi_W\mathcal I$, i.e. (a) and (b). C3 reduces (b) to the fine-tuned identity (3.4) (witness (3.3), three tachyons; $O_b\Phi_3^{[b]}\ne0$ proven), and (3.5) argues that the model's dressing violates it. So PH31′ is not expected to restore R80 C4 whatever the status of (a), and (b) has no motivation that would let it be registered as a hypothesis. (a) alone remains a meaningful conjecture about the image of the circle-state map; its use would be to make $P_{\mathcal I}$ computable (the $G$-orthogonal projector onto $V$ instead of onto an unknown image) and to settle the pole criterion of (4.3) (pure-sector vectors of $V$ at $h_*$).

*Is (a) worth registering?* Motivation: exact at $K=2$ and $K=3$ (5 and 60); the deficit grows with $K$ as the number of admissible $\chi$ does (078, 083). Falsifier: at some $K$, a cokernel over $\mathbb Q$ strictly larger than the joint span of the $b$- and $L$-functionals – e.g. 083's relations-plus-rank closure failing at two independent evaluation points. A mod-$p$ deficit above the identity count does not falsify: the mod-$p$ rank is a lower bound on the rank over $\mathbb Q$, so its deficit is only an upper bound on the true cokernel (078 §3), and an unlucky prime or point can inflate it. Discharge: the same closure at $K=4$, then an argument for all $K$. It is a characterisation of the image, not a rescue of R80 C4; register it only if the projected composite is to be computed.

*Scope.* Everything here is at $\alpha'_{\rm v}=0$. At $\alpha'_{\rm v}\ne0$ the selection identities are not selection rules on $\mathcal H_0$ (078 §4 Commentary; full rank at $K\le3$, $L\le2$ for $\alpha'_{\rm v}=1/2$), PH31 is not refuted there, and R80 C4 in that model is untouched by this record.

\subsection*{Result}
C5: PH31′ = (a) ∧ (b); (b) needs the fine-tuned identity (3.4) for the model's dressing – argued to fail (C3.5, open), and in any case without a motivation – so PH31′ is not a motivated rescue of R80 C4, and the honest six-point statement at $\alpha'_{\rm v}=0$ is the projected one (4.1)–(4.2). (a) is a separate, motivated conjecture on the image of $\pi_W\Phi_2$.

Its second half – that the five-point vector obeys the same selection rules as the two-puncture circle states – would need an identity between the five-point region and the circle region that nothing in the model suggests. On the five-point side the selection operator corresponds to a conformal Killing vector that does not vanish at the three string-field punctures, and already three tachyons give a non-zero component before the dressing; after the dressing, that component vanishes only if a weighted average of two sector ratios cancels for all momenta. That it does not cancel for the model's actual dressing is argued (varying the momenta at fixed total momentum, then comparing pole positions), not computed. The six-point composite in this model should therefore be stated with the projector onto the circle states: this form is exact, and it equals the sector formula only on the projected component. Two questions stay open: the size of the correction term, and where the poles of the projected composite sit. Those poles need not sit at the zeros of the sector transforms – when the circle states contain no vector of a single sector there, the zeros move rather than disappear, possibly to other real timelike momenta. The first half of the hypothesis – that the circle-state map reaches everything the selection rules allow – is a separate and plausible conjecture, now matching the measured gap exactly in the two lowest truncations (at sampled momenta); it is worth registering only if the projected composite is to be computed. None of this touches the model at non-zero vertex parameter, where the selection rules do not arise.
