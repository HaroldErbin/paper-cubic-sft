# Conventions ledger — Cubic closed SFT

One entry per convention this project uses, with the source line per document and the quantity each was checked on. Created 2026-09-26; the standing conventions (ghost number, sphere normalisation, $c_0^\pm$, $b_0^\pm$) are those of the project.

## C1 — Exchange sign of the basic pairing $\omega_1(A,B)=\langle A\vert c_0^-\vert B\rangle$

- **Statement.** On its support $\epsilon(A)+\epsilon(B)=1$, $\omega_1(B,A)=(-1)^{\epsilon(A)\epsilon(B)}\,\omega_1(A,B)$: $s=+1$ (graded symmetric in physical parity).
- **Conventions it rests on.** (i) physical statistics $\epsilon=N_{\rm gh}\bmod 2$; (ii) closed-string BPZ conjugation with the inversion $I^+(z)=1/z$ (book `chapters/bos_worldsheet_cft.tex` l. 1098 and l. 1171–1184, live LaTeX commit 1dbb721; the standard closed-SFT convention, Zwiebach hep-th/9206084, footnote p. 13): $\mathrm{bpz}(b_n)=b_{-n}$, $\mathrm{bpz}(c_n)=-c_{-n}$, the same for $\bar b_n$, $\bar c_n$; hence $\mathrm{bpz}(c_0^\pm)=-c_0^\pm$ (book `bos:eq:conjugation-c0pm-b0pm`). The $I^-(z)=-1/z$ rule, $\mathrm{bpz}(c_n)=(-1)^{n-1}c_{-n}$, $\mathrm{bpz}(b_n)=(-1)^n b_{-n}$ (stated here until 2026-10-17, and in the paper's appendix A), differs by $(-1)^n$ on level-$n$ modes: it agrees with $I^+$ on zero modes and on level-matched states, not on states that are not level matched (decision 2026-10-13); (iii) BPZ conjugation of a product of modes is order-preserving, $\mathrm{bpz}(AB)=\mathrm{bpz}(A)\,\mathrm{bpz}(B)$, no graded sign (`cft_operator_formalism.tex` l. 1134–1146, live LaTeX commit 1dbb7217); (iv) $\langle c_{-1}\bar c_{-1}c_0^-c_0^+c_1\bar c_1\rangle=-\tfrac12$.
- **Sources.** Erbin 2023 `bos:eq:closed-product-rearrangement` ($\langle A,B\rangle=(-1)^{(1+|A|)(1+|B|)}\langle B,A\rangle$); explicit mode computation, derivation 055 (B3.4); ledger R58.
- **Checked on.** $A=c_1\bar c_1\lvert0\rangle$, $B=c_0^+c_1\bar c_1\lvert0\rangle$: $\omega_1(A,B)=\omega_1(B,A)=-\tfrac12$.
- **Where the project disagreed.** Record 002 R2.6 took $s=-1$ (a dropped sign); annotated 2026-09-26. Ledger row R7's $k=2$ statement is unaffected ($s^2=1$); 055 A6/B3.3/B3.4 attributed $s$ to the order rule (iii); corrected 2026-09-30.
- **What fixes $s$ (002 step 4′; node check 2026-09-30).** $s=-\sigma_0$ with $\mathrm{bpz}(c_0^\pm)=\sigma_0c_0^\pm$, on every sector (transposition of $c_0^-$ against the graded-symmetric plain BPZ product). Once $\mathrm{bpz}(L_m)$ is fixed, $\sigma_0$ is the only free ghost sign compatible with the mode algebra, and $\sigma_0=(-1)^{h_c}=-1$ for either inversion $z\mapsto\pm1/z$, so $s=+1$ is not a convention choice. Flipping $\sigma_0$ flips every ghost-mode sign and turns $\omega_1$ into $\Omega_1$ (next bullet). Rule (iii) is not independent: BPZ is the graded anti-involution of the mode algebra, which on creation monomials (the basis of states) is order-preserving. On products that are not creation monomials, (iii) without a graded sign is inconsistent: $b_{-1}c_1\lvert0\rangle=\lvert0\rangle$ would map to $0$. The non-graded reversed rule multiplies a bra of $r$ ghost modes by $(-1)^{r(r-1)/2}$. It leaves the exchange sign unchanged on the zero-mode sector (check REV of `codes/verify-002-dictionary.py`) and flips it on pairs with one further $b$–$c$ pair (e.g. $c_{-2}c_1\bar c_1\lvert0\rangle$ against $b_{-2}c_0^+c_1\bar c_1\lvert0\rangle$; hand computation), so it is not an alternative convention. Other signs do depend on the order rule, e.g. R35's $(-1)^{k(k-1)/2}$.
- **The BV odd symplectic form.** $\Omega_1(A,B):=(-1)^{\epsilon(A)}\omega_1(A,B)$ is odd and plainly antisymmetric on its support, hence graded antisymmetric in $\epsilon$ and in $\epsilon+1$ alike (002 (D4.3)–(D4.4)). A $(-1)^{\epsilon(B)}$ twist gives $-\Omega_1$. $\omega_1$ is symmetric and is not a symplectic form. The ledger rows (R7, R13 Route C, R35, R58) use the untwisted $\omega_1$ and $\omega_k=\omega_1^{\otimes k}$; $\Omega_k=(-1)^{\epsilon(X)}\omega_k$ would multiply Route C's $\omega_k(\lambda_k,\sigma_k)$ by $(-1)^k$.
- **Consequence.** $\omega_k=\omega_1^{\otimes k}$ is graded symmetric for every $k$ (R58).
- **Qualifiers (028 addendum P.6, node check 2026-09-30).** (iii) holds on normal-ordered (creation) words; (ii) is the $I^-$ rule, equal to the book's closed-string $I^+$ rule on level-matched states.

## C2 — Antibracket sign convention (A4)

- **Statement.** De Witt convention, as in Henneaux–Teitelboim ch. 15: $(F,G)=\dfrac{\partial^rF}{\partial\phi^i}\dfrac{\partial^lG}{\partial\phi^+_i}-\dfrac{\partial^rF}{\partial\phi^+_i}\dfrac{\partial^lG}{\partial\phi^i}$, antifields of parity opposite to their fields and $\mathrm{gh}(\phi^+)=-\mathrm{gh}(\phi)-1$; the BV action is even, $\mathrm{gh}=0$. For an even $S$, $(S,S)=2\,\dfrac{\partial^rS}{\partial\phi^i}\dfrac{\partial^lS}{\partial\phi^+_i}$.
- **Conventions it rests on.** Physical ghost number and parity of the fields (the project conventions; 016 §1: $\Psi$, $\Sigma$, $\tilde X$, $Y$ even, gauge parameter and ghost odd, antifields of the auxiliaries odd; the worldsheet $N_{\rm gh}$ of the antifields is not pinned).
- **Sources.** Record 016 §1 (parities, and the trivial-symmetry generator $\tfrac12\phi^+_i\mu^{ij}\phi^+_j$ with the graded antisymmetry (16.2)); record 056 A4 (the formula above); record 058 Part B (the antibracket of the finite cyclic $L_\infty$ model realised as $P=\omega^{-1}\cdot\operatorname{diag}((-1)^{\deg})$ — a symmetric matrix was caught as vacuous during its development — checks B0–B3 of `codes/verify-bv-master-equation.py`; R62).
- **Checked on.** The reduction $(S,S)=2\,\partial^rS/\partial\phi^i\,\partial^lS/\partial\phi^+_i$ for even $S$ used in the decoupling lemma, 056 §3(e) and check `C2.3` of `codes/verify-gap3e-contractible-pair.py` (all-even representation). No computation in the project yet fixes a sign that depends on the relative sign of the two terms.
- **Odd symplectic form.** On the string field the odd symplectic form is $\Omega_1(A,B)=(-1)^{\epsilon(A)}\langle A\vert c_0^-\vert B\rangle$ (C1). It is odd and antisymmetric on its support, so graded antisymmetric in the field parity used here and in the suspended degree alike. Which overall sign of $\Omega_1$ inverts to the antibracket above is not fixed by any computation in the project. On $\Sigma$, $\omega_2$ and $\Omega_2=(-1)^{\epsilon(X)}\omega_2$ are even in every grading whose parity is a function of $\epsilon=N_{\rm gh}\bmod2$ (002 (D4.9)). Pairing $\Sigma$ with an odd antifield (016 §1) therefore needs the $\Sigma$-sector parity declared by another grading, e.g. R36's $(N_{\rm gh}-4)/2$ on the classical complex (Argued).
- **The auxiliary pairing sign in record 067 (2026-10-01).** In the finite-model BV Hessian of record 067, the odd symplectic pairing of the auxiliary sector is fixed as $J[\Sigma,\Sigma^+]=+1$, $J[\Sigma^+,\Sigma]=-1$, which gives the $y^+$ row of $\mathcal Q_0$ the sign $\sigma=+1$ ($\mathcal Q_0y^+=H_{\Sigma\Psi}x+H_{\Sigma\Sigma}y$). This is a local convention tied to the sign of $J$: it is absorbed by $y^+\to-y^+$, which flips $J$, the $(\Sigma^+,c)$ entry and the antifield shift of the canonical lift together. It settles the "$\mp$" of 056 (3.5) inside the model's $P$ as "$-$". Graded symmetry of the BV Hessian does not fix it (it only relates $H[\Psi,\Sigma]$ to $H[\Sigma,\Psi]$ and $H[c,\Sigma^+]$ to $H[\Sigma^+,c]$); the relative sign of $H_{\Sigma\Sigma}$, $H_{\Sigma\Psi}$, $H_{\Psi\Psi}$ is their common origin in $S^{\rm cub}$ and is falsified by $T^{\rm eff}=T^{(2)}$ in the script's C3-decouple check.
- **Where the project leaves signs untracked.** 056 (3.5): $s\hat Y^+=\mp\tfrac{g_s^2}{12}p\hat Y$ ("$\mp$"); 056 (4.3): $(S_0,\Xi)\ \dot=\ Y^+\!\cdot D_cY$ ("$\dot=$"), and with it the sign and normalisation at antifield number two (056 C3.3). A graded computation under this entry would settle them.

## C3 — Reality conjugation

- **Statement.** $\ddagger:=(-1)^{N_{\rm gh}+1}\,\mathrm{bpz}^{-1}\circ\mathrm{hc}$ on single-strand states, extended componentwise to $\mathcal H_0^{\otimes k}$ (028 (C2)); $\ddagger^2=1$ (028 addendum P.3; PH7 discharged). With it $\omega_1$ is equivariant, $\omega_1(A^\ddagger,B^\ddagger)=\omega_1(A,B)^*$, given Zwiebach (2.37) and $s=+1$ (C1, R58); a constant sign makes it anti-equivariant.
- **Statistics qualifier.** On a Grassmann-even total field whose coefficients carry physical statistics, $X^\ddagger=X$ componentwise is Zwiebach (3.6); at $N_{\rm gh}=2$ it is Zwiebach's condition itself (tachyon: $\phi^*(p)=\phi(-p)$). For an odd-$N_{\rm gh}$ object with commuting coefficients, Zwiebach (3.18) (classical gauge parameter) reads $\Lambda^\ddagger=-\Lambda$ ($\ddagger$ anticommutes with $Q$ between $N_{\rm gh}=1$ and $2$); the Route C multipliers are declared $\lambda_k^\ddagger=\lambda_k$ because that makes $\omega_k$ equivariant and $S_C$ real.
- **Conventions it rests on.** C1 ($s=+1$, bpz rules); physical ghost number (the project conventions).

## C6 — Symmetry type of the multi-string spaces $\mathcal H^{\wedge n}$

- **Statement.** $\mathcal H^{\wedge n}$ is the space of tensors in $\mathcal H^{\otimes n}$ whose components are graded symmetric under permutations of the strands, in a parity fixed by the role of the strands. *Input strands* (arguments of vertices and products: $\Psi^n$, $\Sigma_n$, gauge parameters $\Lambda$, single-string equations of motion ($\mathcal E_\Psi$)): graded symmetric in the physical parity $\varepsilon$ of the strand – even strands have symmetric components ($\operatorname{Sym}^n\mathcal H_0$), odd strands antisymmetric ones ($\ell_{n+2}(\Lambda_1,\Lambda_2,\Psi^n)$ is antisymmetric in the two parameters). *Output strands* (strands the pairing $\omega_n$ sets against input strands: outputs of $p_{m,n}$, multipliers $\Theta_k$): graded symmetric in the shifted parity $\varepsilon+1$ – at $N_{\rm gh}=3$ symmetric components, $\Lambda^n_{\rm gr}\mathcal H_3=\operatorname{Sym}^n(\Pi\mathcal H_3)$, the dual of $\operatorname{Sym}^n\mathcal H_0$. For strands of opposite parity both gradings give the exchange sign $+1$, so a mixed tensor needs no role. In the physical parity on every strand a $\Theta_k$ with $k\ge2$ odd strands is plainly antisymmetric and $\omega_k(\Theta_k,\Sigma_k)\equiv0$, for any signs of $\omega_1$.
- **Conventions it rests on.** C1 ($\omega_n=\omega_1^{\otimes n}$ with Koszul signs, graded symmetric, R58); physical ghost number (the project conventions).
- **Sources.** Input strands: EKS 1403.0940 (2.24)–(2.25) (degree = Grassmann parity, wedge graded symmetric), MS 1109.4101 §III B ($SA_c=TA_c/I$) with the even shift $A_c=s^{-2}\tilde A_c$ (A8); neither has an $n$-string pairing or a multi-output map. Output strands: record 026 §1.3 (1.7) (raised outputs graded symmetric in $\bar\varepsilon$); record 055 B2 (only $\Lambda^k_{\rm gr}\mathcal H_3$ pairs with $\operatorname{Sym}^k\mathcal H_0$); R58; proofreader-appendix-a-20261001T151500 B1 ($k=2$ hand check); record 076 (the per-role statement, `agent/computations/verify-076-hwedge-convention.py`).
- **Checked on.** The $k=2$ hand check ($\Theta=B_1\otimes B_2-B_2\otimes B_1$ against $\Sigma=A_1\otimes A_2+A_2\otimes A_1$ gives $0$); numerically at $\dim\mathcal H_{2,3}=3$: raised two-leg outputs of a graded-symmetric $V_4$ plainly symmetric and pairing non-trivially with $\operatorname{Sym}^2\mathcal H_0$, the EKS-wedge $\Theta$ pairing to zero, $\ell_3$ antisymmetric and non-zero on two odd arguments, exchange signs equal on mixed and opposite on homogeneous strands (record 076, 11/11 PASS). The two symmetry types on homogeneous strands are different subspaces and not inter-convertible by a re-signing of components (diagonal obstruction).
- **Where the project was loose.** The paper notation sheet (§1, until 2026-10-01) and the project's new notes define $\mathcal H^{\wedge n}$ as the graded-symmetric power in the physical parity for every strand (EKS's wedge): right for input strands, not for the multipliers and the product outputs. C6's first version (2026-10-01) wrote "components symmetric under permutations", wrong for odd input strands (gauge parameters, where EKS's wedge gives antisymmetric components). R13's Route C writes $\lambda_k\in\operatorname{Sym}^k$ loosely in the output sense.

## C8 — Rule K: evaluation of multilinear maps and forms on string fields with Grassmann-odd coefficients

- **Statement.** On string fields of unrestricted ghost number, $\Psi=\Psi^ae_a$, whose components carry the Grassmann parity that makes the field even ($\abs{\Psi^a}=\varepsilon_a$), multilinear maps and forms (vertices $\mathcal V_n$, products, the pairings $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_n$, $\Omega_n$) are evaluated with the coefficients moved to the left, each coefficient passing only the basis states to its left. The first slot's coefficient passes nothing. For an odd map this differs from the standard Koszul extension, in which the map also passes each coefficient and picks up its parity; at one output the two differ by $(-1)^{\abs B}$ on the inputs. The rule fixes the sign of the IBL generating identity at one output: under rule K, $p^{\Omega}_{m,n}(\Psi^m)=p_{m,n}\bigl((P\Psi)^m\bigr)$ with $P\Psi=\sum_a(-1)^{\varepsilon_a}\Psi^ae_a$ (the signs depend only on the parities of coefficients and basis states, which $P$ does not change); $p^{\Omega}_{m,n}(B)=(-1)^{\abs B}p_{m,n}(B)$ on homogeneous inputs (R87). For two odd superfields, rule K gives $\Omega(L,K)=g_{cd}L^cK^d$, symmetric in $L$ and $K$; the Koszul passage of the coefficients through $\Omega$ as an odd form gives the opposite sign. Under rule K the polarised master equation at bidegree $(m,n)$ is, up to one overall sign common to all $(m,n)$, the sum of the compositions of $p^{\Omega}$ (R87, $R^{\Omega}=-E$). Under a uniform Koszul evaluation the one-output relations of $p^{\Omega}$ are the Koszul relations of $\ell^{\Omega}$, which differ from those of $\ell$ by $2\ell_2(Q_Bx,y)$ at $(2,1)$ ($x$ even, $y$ odd), so the theorem reads "in the standard Koszul convention for the odd maps $\ell_n$" while the generating-function form is read with rule K. On ghost-two fields alone ($P\Psi=\Psi$) the rule is immaterial.
- **Conventions it rests on.** C1 ($\Omega_n(X,Y)=(-1)^{\abs X}\langle X,Y\rangle_n$, the $n$-fold form with the Koszul sign of `eq:conv:pairing-n`); C6 (string fields as even superfields); physical ghost number (the project conventions).

## C10 — Ghost numbers of $\hbar$ and $g_s$ in the IBL reading

- **Statement.** Local to the IBL reading of record 103. $g_s$ is CFL's $\tau$ at genus zero ($g_s^{k+\ell-2}=\tau^{k+\ell-2}$) and has ghost number $0$ and CFL degree $0$. ## C12 — Propagator measure and ghost insertion: $\mathrm ds\,\mathrm d\theta$ against $\mathrm d^2t/(2\pi\vert t\vert^2)$
